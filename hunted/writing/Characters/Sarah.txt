# SARAH REEVES

## BASIC INFORMATION
- **Full Name**: Sarah Reeves
- **Age**: Late 30s
- **Occupation**: Former public defender (8 years)
- **Family**: Married to Hank (deceased), mother of Eli (18), Emma (12), Olivia (10)
- **Hunting**: Has her own hunting award - equally capable as rest of family

## ARCHETYPE
**The Avenger** - Transforms from content wife/mother to revenge-obsessed hunter. Her journey embodies the story's theme: revenge consumes the avenger as much as the target.

## CHARACTER FUNCTION
The protagonist of Acts 2-3. Her transformation from practical wife to revenge-obsessed hunter embodies the story's theme about the cost of revenge.

## PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION
- Similar age to Hank (late 30s)
- A few inches shorter than Liam

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
- Practical, grounded
- Initially skeptical of Hank's prepper lifestyle but learned the skills
- Eight years as a public defender: saw murderers walk because evidence got lost, witnesses recanted, DAs decided cases weren't worth the trouble
- Deep distrust of police/courts to deliver justice—not paranoia, experience
- Grief transforms into cold determination
- Face-to-face moment with Liam awakens something primal
- Obsession with revenge blinds her to simpler solutions
- Leads family's defensive preparation

## RELATIONSHIPS
- Hank: husband, his death transforms her
- Eli: shares her obsession with revenge
- Emma: sees her growing fear, ignores it
- Olivia: sees her growing fear, ignores it
- Liam: the target of her revenge

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Beginning State (Act 1)**: Content wife and mother. Watches her family with love. Learned Hank's skills—she's capable, but not obsessed with preparedness.
- **Inciting Incident**: Comes home to find Hank dead, daughter held hostage. Face-to-face with the killer awakens something primal.
- **Escalation (Act 2)**: Rejects police, investigates alone. Nearly catches Liam. Transforms the house into a fortress. Her obsession grows while her younger children's fear is ignored.
- **Crisis Point (Act 3)**: Watches Eli die for the same revenge she's pursuing. Sends the younger girls to safety—but stays to fight. Both she and Liam are caught in the final trap.
- **End State**: Dies to Liam, but her last words are victory: "The girls got out. Police coming." A Tragic Arc: her revenge consumed her, but her sacrifice wasn't completely in vain—the girls survived by making the practical choice she couldn't.

## DIALOGUE STYLE
- Brief, practical in Act 1 ("You get everything?", "Uh huh")
- Transforms to cold and threatening after Hank's death
- Key line: "If you ever come back, you're dead."
- Key line: "Then I'll have to come for you."
- Legal background shows in confrontation with police—precise, argumentative: "That's a credible, specific threat of future violence." Knows the language.
- Throws her experience at authority: "I was a public defender for eight years. I know exactly what happens next."
- Contempt for system she once served: "I've seen murderers walk because evidence got lost. Because witnesses recanted. Because some DA decided it wasn't worth the trouble."
- Remains practical even in extreme crisis—half-blinded by mace, still gives clear instructions: "Come out here. Just stay out of the mace. The police will get here soon."
- Commands become tactical: "Positions. Now.", "We hold the high ground."
- Final words show acceptance mixed with defiance: "The girls got out. Police coming."
- Raw grief erupts as violence: "YOU TOOK EVERYTHING FROM ME!" - each word punctuated by a blow
- Grief weaponized into coldness: "Dad's dead." Too sharp, too cold—she regrets it but doesn't take it back
- Desperation breaks through tactical control: "ELI, NO!" when she can't stop her son's fatal choice

## KEY SCENES
- **Act 1, Scene 4: Family Time** - Watches from the grill as Hank teaches the kids self-defense. Content, smiling—this is her family, her life. Brief warmth before everything changes.
- **Act 2, Scene 9: The Hostage** - Hears Emma's scream, bursts in to find Hank dead, Olivia held hostage. Face cycles through grief/horror/rage—settles into cold. "If you ever come back, you're dead." / "Then I'll have to come for you." Pulls her own gun (same model as Hank's), fires blindly after mace attack. Practical even in chaos: "Come out here. Just stay out of the mace." Whispers: "I'll find you."
- **Act 2, Scene 10: Sarah's Investigation** - Same night: confronts police as they seize guns. "I was a public defender for eight years. I know exactly what happens next." Watches them take Hank's body. Next morning: Bobby babysits while she and Eli investigate. Cabela's footage → phone call to Bobby → bar → apartment. Attacks Liam—"YOU TOOK EVERYTHING FROM ME!" Eli saves her twice (from knife, from strangling). Sarah jumps from fire escape, lands on Liam. Both hurt—tussle on ground but Liam escapes. Sees "CYA SOON" on car. "We need to get ready."
- **Act 3, Scene 11: Parallel Preparation (MONTAGE)** - Transforms house into fortress: trip wires, molotov cocktails, broken glass under windows, hot oil trap. Family meeting: "I need to talk to you about what we're doing." Emma pushes back: "This is wrong. All of this. Dad taught us to defend ourselves, not... this." Sarah's cold response: "Dad's dead." Regrets it immediately but doesn't take it back. "He'll come. And we'll be ready." Obsession consuming her while Emma/Olivia grow scared.
- **Act 3, Scene 12: The Return** - Commands family positions: "Positions. Now." Fires blind over secondary doors at trapped Liam. Fights Liam on stairs with Hank's hunting knife—slashes his arm, cheek, thigh. Wounded but presses advantage. Disarmed when Liam catches her wrist, bones grinding. Retreats: "We hold the high ground. Let him come to us."
- **Act 3, Scene 13: Bobby's Return** - Not present in basement scenes—holds position upstairs with family.
- **Act 3, Scene 14: Eli's Death** - Tries to stop Eli: "ELI, NO!" Watches helplessly as her son is overpowered and killed. "No... no, no, no..." Races Liam for the open gatehouse doors—he's faster. Maces him, slams bedroom door, retreats to attic with girls. Sends Emma/Olivia through secret passage: "Go to the secret room. Wait there. Don't come out until I call." Final standoff: "Just us now."
- **Act 3, Scene 16: The Escape** - Final fight in attic. "They're not here. Just me." Triggers crossbow trap. Both caught in spring trap. "Gone. You lose."
- **Act 3, Scene 17: The Crawling Struggle** - Strangled by Liam: "The girls got out. Police coming." Dies to her own obsession. The tragedy of revenge.
