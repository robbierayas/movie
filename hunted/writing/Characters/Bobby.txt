# BOBBY

## BASIC INFORMATION
- **Full Name**: Bobby (last name TBD)
- **Age**: Early 40s (same as Hank)
- **Occupation**: Construction (works with Hank sometimes)
- **Family**: Single

## ARCHETYPE
**The Buddy / The Sacrifice** - Hank's longtime friend and hunting companion. Provides contrast to Hank's capability.
Arrives to help in Act 3 and dies trying.

## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Establishes Hank's social world and provides someone for Hank to interact with naturally. His "pray and spray" gun
preference contrasts with Hank's knife expertise, highlighting what makes Hank special. Returns in Act 3 as cavalry that
fails—reinforces that outside help can't save the family; they must save themselves (or call 911).

## PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION
- Typical neckbeard - unkempt facial hair
- Always wearing camo, even when unnecessary
- Smokes and drinks beer
- Softer build than Hank - less physically capable

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
- Talkative and crass with Hank - his full unfiltered self
- Quiet and polite around Hank's family - knows his personality would be off-putting to them
- Enthusiastic about guns, hunting, guy stuff
- Not as skilled or knowledgeable as Hank but tries to keep up
- Good-natured, not mean-spirited

## RELATIONSHIPS
- Hank: longtime friend, hunting buddy, coworker - one of the few people he can fully be himself around
- Sarah: was his lawyer in a past case, which is how they have a closer relationship beyond just being Hank's friend
- Kids: respectful, keeps his rougher edges hidden
- Liam: saw him at Cabela's, never formally met, killed by him

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Beginning State (Act 1)**: Hank's buddy, admires but can't match his friend's skills and family life. The "pray and
spray" guy—relies on firepower over finesse.
- **Acts 2-3**: Not present during Hank's murder. Returns in Act 3 when the girls call him.
- **Crisis Point (Scene 15)**: Arrives to help, finds Girly seemingly dead. Goes hunting with her one last time. Dies in
the basement—burned on the stairs before he can fire a shot.
- **End State**: Dies trying to help his friend's family. His "pray and spray" shotgun never gets used. The irony Liam
noted at Cabela's proves tragically accurate.

## DIALOGUE STYLE
- Crass and unfiltered with Hank - swears, makes crude jokes ("No shit?", "That's some fortune cookie shit")
- Code-switches to polite and quiet around the family
- Enthusiastic about things he likes - "Oh hell yes", "Now we're talking"
- Uses hunter/gun slang naturally ("pray and spray")
- Teases Hank back affectionately ("Mister Karate Man")
- Self-deprecating about his own skills vs Hank's
- Sincere admiration slips through the banter ("You make it look easy", "I don't know how you do it")
- Grief breaks through in fragments: "Sarah. I came as soon as I could. I can't... Hank..." - can't finish the sentence
- Honors his friend even in crisis: "Hank gave this to me years ago. Said I might need it someday." - giving away Hank's
gift to protect Hank's family
- Returns to what he knows under pressure: "Alright. Let's go hunting." - falls back on familiar competence
- Silent communication with Girly speaks louder than words—subtitled gestures show the bond from dozens of hunts together

## KEY SCENES
- **Act 1, Scene 3: The Cabela's Encounter** - Shopping with Hank at Cabela's. Enthusiastic about tactical shotgun:
"Pray and spray, baby." Teases Hank as "Mister Karate Man," gets ribbed back about knife skills. Liam says he works at
a bar — Bobby remembers the name. Unknowingly part of Liam's target selection process.
- **Act 1, Scene 4: Family Time** - Code-switches to family-friendly mode. Plays with Girly ("Who's the best gorilla?"),
gets used as Hank's comedic demonstration of "how not to fight." Taps out dramatically for the kids' entertainment. Uncle
Bobby figure—rough edges hidden.
- **Act 2, Scene 10: Sarah's Investigation** - Next morning: arrives to babysit Emma and Olivia. Kids run to him: "Unky
Buh-by!" Grief-stricken but there for the family: "Sarah. I came as soon as I could. I can't... Hank..." Gives Sarah
Hank's old gun: "Hank gave this to me years ago. Said I might need it someday." Later via phone: identifies Liam from
Cabela's footage: "Holy shit. That's... that's the guy from the store. He was asking Hank questions about survival
stuff." Remembers bar name: "Mulligan's? Maybe?"
- **Act 3, Scene 13: Bobby's Return** - Arrives at burning house: "Sarah! Eli!" Finds Girly motionless near porch: "No.
No, no, no—" / "Girly? Come on, girl." She wakes—relief. "Alright. Let's go hunting." Silent communication with Girly
(subtitled): "Stay." / "If you're not back in 5 seconds..." / "I know." Goes down basement stairs: "I know you're down
there, you piece of—" Hits accelerant. Burns. Screams. Dies with arm outstretched—reaching toward nothing. Palm up.
Fingers slightly curled. Girly ignores command, dies fighting beside him.
