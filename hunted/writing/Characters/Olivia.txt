# OLIVIA REEVES

## BASIC INFORMATION
- **Full Name**: Olivia Reeves
- **Age**: 10
- **Occupation**: Student
- **Family**: Child of Hank (deceased) and Sarah (deceased), sibling to Eli (18, deceased) and Emma (12)
- **Hunting**: Has own hunting award - equally capable as rest of family
- **Specialty**: Baseball - made the travel team, youngest - giggles through self-defense training

## ARCHETYPE
**The Innocent** - Youngest, follows Emma's lead, survives through trust in her sister.

## CHARACTER FUNCTION
The youngest survivor. Follows Emma's lead to safety. Represents innocence preserved through practical choice rather than
violence.

## RELATIONSHIPS
- Hank: loved father
- Sarah: fears mother's transformation
- Eli: older sibling lost to revenge
- Emma: follows, trusts, escapes with
- Liam: source of terror

- Girly: her dog, corrects Bobby about the name

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Beginning State (Act 1)**: Happy 10-year-old. Daddy's girl. Reminds everyone about homeschool. Made the travel team.
Just a kid.
- **Inciting Incident**: Taken hostage by Liam. Terror.
- **Escalation (Act 2)**: Follows Emma's lead, clearly scared. Hands shake wiring alarms.
- **Crisis Point (Act 3)**: Small voice asking for Eli. Realizes the horror around her.
- **End State**: Follows Emma through the secret passage. Survives because her sister made the practical choice.
Innocence preserved.

## DIALOGUE STYLE
- Enthusiastic kid in Act 1 ("Daddy!", "Wait! What about homeschool?", "I made the travel team!")
- Corrects adults playfully ("Her name is Girly, Uncle Bobby!")
- Becomes quiet and scared after trauma—questions, not statements ("What if he doesn't come back?", "What do we do?")
- Small voice in the darkness ("Eli...?")
- Simple questions adults can't answer: "Why are we doing this?" / "Shouldn't we just leave?" - child's practicality
ignored
- Unguarded emotional honesty: "I'm scared." - cuts through all the tactical talk
- Hope and grief equally unfiltered: "Unky Buh-by!" → "Bobby..." - no armor between feeling and expression

## KEY SCENES
- **Act 1, Scene 4: Family Time** - Daddy's girl—sprints to greet Hank. Reminds everyone about homeschool. Giggles
through training. Corrects Bobby about Girly's name. Made the travel team! Happy kid before everything changes.
- **Act 2, Scene 9: The Hostage** - Runs to door first. "Dad! We're home!" Taken hostage by Liam. Terrified.
- **Act 3, Scene 15: Parallel Preparation (MONTAGE)** - Closes doors with Emma. "Why are we doing this?" / "Shouldn't we
just leave?" At family meeting: "I'm scared." Simple honesty that Sarah can't answer properly.
- **Act 3, Scene 16: The Return** - "What do we do?" (crying). Huddles with Emma. Can't escape through window because of
drone.
- **Act 3, Scene 17: Bobby's Return** - Face lights up: "Unky Buh-by!" Hope flooding her voice. Then Liam's mocking echo
from below. "Bobby..." Face crumples. Knows he's dead.
- **Act 3, Scene 18: Eli's Death** - Escapes through secret passage with Emma when Sarah sends them away. Takes Emma's
hand.
- **Act 3, Scene 19: The Escape** - Huddles in secret basement room with Emma. Sees Liam strangling Sarah at top of shaft:
"MOM! MOMMY!" Emma pulls her back. Escapes through secret door and basement stairs with Emma.
- **Act 3, Scene 20: The Crawling Struggle** - Watches from police car. Safe because she followed Emma. Innocence
preserved through practical choice.
