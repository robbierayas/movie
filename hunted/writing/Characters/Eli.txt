# ELI REEVES

## BASIC INFORMATION
- **Full Name**: Eli Reeves
- **Age**: 18
- **Occupation**: Student (assumed)
- **Family**: Child of Hank (deceased) and Sarah (deceased), sibling to Emma (12) and Olivia (10)
- **Gender**: Male
- **Hunting**: Has own hunting award - equally capable as rest of family
- **Specialty**: Archery (bow and arrow) - "Coach says I might make varsity next year." Uses bow in Act 3

## ARCHETYPE
**The Zealot** - Shares mother's obsession with revenge. Where Emma chooses practicality, Eli chooses confrontation. Dies for it.

## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Represents the tragic cost of shared obsession. Goes all-in on revenge with Sarah, dies for it. Their death reinforces the story's theme that revenge consumes as much as it protects.

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Beginning State (Act 1)**: Serious kid, takes training seriously. Trying to be cool while still being young. Pride in his skills.
- **Inciting Incident**: Witnesses father's murder, sister held hostage. Frozen with horror but anger building.
- **Escalation (Act 2)**: Goes all-in on mother's revenge mission. Helps track Liam, fights him at apartment, prepares traps. "Dad's dead." No hesitation.
- **Crisis Point (Act 3)**: Traps Liam inside—"TWO MEN ENTER! ONE MAN LEAVES!" But can't wait for him to come to them.
- **End State**: Dies confronting Liam. "I'm not Dad" proves tragically true—he wasn't ready. His death is the cost of shared obsession.

## DIALOGUE STYLE
- Trying to sound mature/cool in Act 1 ("I've been practicing", sibling banter with Emma)
- Becomes intense after father's death—direct, blunt ("Dad's dead.")
- Movie references when escalated ("TWO MEN ENTER! ONE MAN LEAVES!") - uses pop culture as battle cry
- Attempts at reassurance that ring hollow: "It's gonna be okay. We just have to be smart. Stay together." - trying to be the man of the house
- Rage overtakes words: "YOU KILLED HIM!" - accusation, not strategy
- Dying words are defiance, not fear: "You aren't a man. You're just... a thing. A broken thing that pretends—" - attacks Liam's identity even while dying

## KEY SCENES
- **Act 1, Scene 4: Family Time** - Takes self-defense training seriously. Shows off his disarm technique—"I've been practicing." Archery going well, might make varsity. Trying to look cool while still being a kid.
- **Act 2, Scene 9: The Hostage** - Witnesses father's body, sister held hostage. Holds Emma back, frozen with horror through the standoff. After Liam flees: "Mom?" First word since seeing his father dead—checking on Sarah, already stepping into protector role.
- **Act 2, Scene 10: Sarah's Investigation** - Insists on going with Sarah: "I'm going with you." No hesitation—already channeling grief into action. At apartment, SAVES SARAH TWICE: (1) tackles Liam when he grabs a knife, (2) baton to Liam's skull when he's strangling Sarah. Gets buried under bookshelf but keeps fighting. Can't get down fire escape fast enough—watches Liam escape.
- **Act 3, Scene 11: Parallel Preparation (MONTAGE)** - Sets up crockpot with hot oil near stairwell, positions arrows for his bow. Checks sight lines: "Perfect." When Sarah says they're ready: "It's gonna be okay. We just have to be smart. Stay together." Puts hand on Emma's shoulder—trying to be reassuring but sharing Sarah's obsession.
- **Act 3, Scene 12: The Return** - Readies bow at bottom of stairs. Fires at Liam—arrow glances off helmet. Releases hot oil trap: "NOW!" When Liam tries to leave, fires again: "TWO MEN ENTER! ONE MAN LEAVES!" Wants the kill but Sarah holds him back: "No. We hold the high ground."
- **Act 3, Scene 13: Bobby's Return** - Not present in basement—hears kids cheer "Unky Buh-by!" Then hears Liam's mocking echo from below. Knows Bobby is dead.
- **Act 3, Scene 14: Eli's Death** - Hears Liam mock "Unky Buh-by?" Rage overcomes strategy. Opens gatehouse doors despite Sarah's "ELI, NO!" Charges: "YOU KILLED HIM!" Fires bow (arrow deflects off armor), draws hunting knife. Fight on landing—wounds Liam with knife to side, but Liam overpowers him. Shotgun blast to thigh. Dying defiance: "You aren't a man. You're just... a thing. A broken thing that pretends—" Killed viciously. His revenge cost them the gatehouse doors.
