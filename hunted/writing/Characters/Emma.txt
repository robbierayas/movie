# EMMA REEVES

## BASIC INFORMATION
- **Full Name**: Emma Reeves
- **Age**: 12
- **Occupation**: Student
- **Family**: Child of Hank (deceased) and Sarah (deceased), sibling to Eli (18, deceased) and Olivia (10)
- **Hunting**: Has own hunting award - equally capable as rest of family
- **Specialty**: Former gymnast ("I didn't quit. I... graduated."), now shoots .22 rifle for fun - enough for the drone, not enough for Liam

## ARCHETYPE
**The Pragmatist** - Makes the sensible choice when everyone else is consumed by revenge. Saves herself and Olivia by calling 911.

## CHARACTER FUNCTION
The practical one. Makes the sensible choice that saves both herself and Olivia. Represents the story's ironic theme: the unglamorous option (calling 911) succeeds where survivalist ingenuity fails.

- Practical, level-headed
- Participates in preparation but grows increasingly scared
- Recognizes when things have gone too far

## RELATIONSHIPS
- Hank: learned from father
- Sarah: follows mother's preparation but sees the obsession
- Eli: sees sibling consumed by revenge, diverges
- Olivia: protector, leads to safety
- Liam: fear rather than revenge
- Bobby: uncle figure, they call him for help (Scene 15)
- Girly: family pet, loses her too

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Beginning State (Act 1)**: Normal 12-year-old. Sibling rivalry with Olivia, ketchup diplomacy with Eli. Training is just routine.
- **Inciting Incident**: Witnesses father's murder, screams for him.
- **Escalation (Act 2)**: Participates in preparation but grows scared. Whispers with Olivia about leaving. Sees the obsession consuming her mother and brother.
- **Crisis Point (Act 3)**: Witnesses Eli's death. Realizes revenge cost her brother. When told to "finish" Liam, she makes a different choice.
- **End State**: Grabs Olivia and escapes through the secret passage. Calls 911. Survives because she chose the unglamorous practical option over revenge.

## DIALOGUE STYLE
- Normal kid banter in Act 1 ("Say please", "I didn't quit. I... graduated.")
- Growing fear shows through hesitation ("Mom... this feels wrong.")
- Whispered conspiracies with Olivia ("I want to go to Aunt Rachel's.")
- Final decision is action, not words—grabs Olivia and goes
- Perceptive observations that cut through denial: "I don't think Mom wants to leave." - sees what adults won't admit
- Invokes father's values against mother's obsession: "Dad taught us to defend ourselves, not... not this. He wouldn't want this."
- Hope is loud and unguarded: "Unky Buh-by! UNKY BUH-BY!" - then silenced by horror

## KEY SCENES
- **Act 1, Scene 4: Family Time** - Normal kid. Sibling rivalry ("Show-off", "I didn't quit. I... graduated."). Tries at self-defense training. Just enjoying family time before everything changes.
- **Act 2, Scene 9: The Hostage** - Screams "DAD!" seeing Hank's body. Held back by Eli during hostage standoff.
- **Act 3, Scene 11: Parallel Preparation (MONTAGE)** - Closes and locks windows with Olivia. Olivia: "Why are we doing this?" Emma: "Mom said to." Olivia: "Shouldn't we just leave?" Emma: "I don't think Mom wants to leave." Exchange look of fear. Later pushes back at family meeting: "This is wrong. All of this. Dad taught us to defend ourselves, not... not this. He wouldn't want this." Sarah's cold "Dad's dead" silences her but doesn't change her mind.
- **Act 3, Scene 12: The Return** - "There's something out there!" (sees drone at window). Huddles with Olivia, scared. After Liam retreats to basement, grabs her .22 rifle and shoots down the drone: "I got it." One less advantage for Liam.
- **Act 3, Scene 13: Bobby's Return** - Cheers with Olivia: "Unky Buh-by! UNKY BUH-BY!" Hope flooding her voice. Then hears Liam's mocking echo from below. Hope dies.
- **Act 3, Scene 14: Eli's Death** - Witnesses Eli's fatal charge. Retreats to attic with Sarah and Olivia. Escapes through secret passage when Sarah sends them to the secret room: "Go to the secret room. Wait there." Takes Olivia's hand. They disappear into the passage.
- **Act 3, Scene 17: The Crawling Struggle** - Watches from police car as Liam is shot. "Let's go." Chose the sensible option. Survived.
