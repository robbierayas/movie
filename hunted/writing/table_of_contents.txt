# TABLE OF CONTENTS

## Act 1 - The Predator

### Scene 1 - The Humiliation
- Location: INT. BAR - NIGHT
- Characters: Liam, Regular Customer, Customer's Friends
- Summary: Liam works his bartending shift. Liam explains in voiceover how he has always been unlucky. A friendly regular
he doesn't really know is joking with him. Voiceover says he IS lucky that he possesses so much skill to overcome the bad
luck. Others walk in, one an attractive woman, to join customer who crack a joke at Liam's expense. He takes it as
humiliation. Voiceover explains people who get all the luck don't realize they have no skill.

### Scene 2 - Bar Revenge
- Location: EXT. BAR - NIGHT
- Characters: Liam
- Summary:  Via voiceover, Liam reflects on what happened next—hunting down a look-alike that same night, keeping the
victim's glasses as a trophy.Voiceover stops as brutal second kill happens on street alley. Liam stores the glasses from
his second kill. We glimpse a hair bow—trophy from his first kill (unseen, unexplained). Via voiceover, some time has
passed, Liam explains he's finished reading "The Most Dangerous Game." The idea takes hold: he wants a real challenge.
Someone who can fight back.

### Scene 3 - The Cabela's Encounter
- Location: INT. CABELA'S - DAY
- Characters: Liam, Hank, Bobby
- Summary: Liam spots Hank in his element among survival gear—knowledgeable, confident, clearly capable. This is the
worthy prey he's been looking for. Liam mentions he works at a bar (Mulligan's) and needs a knife for protection—Bobby
remembers this detail. Liam follows him to the parking lot, gets his license plate, and begins tracking him home.

### Scene 4 - Family Time
- Location: EXT./INT. REEVES PROPERTY - VARIOUS
- Characters: Hank, Bobby, Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia, Girly (dog)
- Summary: Liam is not shown for a few minutes. Bobby and Hank arrive with groceries for a cookout. The family is eager
to eat but Olivia reminds about their "homeschool". All look to Hank to decide and he says they will do homeschool first.
Homeschool is self-defense class taught by Hank. The kids take turns practicing with Hank or a dummy. Bobby plays with
Girly—when Olivia tells him to call her "Girly," Bobby says "Then call me Unky Buh-by" and the kids laugh. Bobby joins in
doing something wacky. Sarah watches all of it while grilling. After, they all sit to eat, with feeling that everything
is fine. Cut to..

### Scene 5 - The Surveillance
- Location: EXT./INT. REEVES PROPERTY - VARIOUS (MONTAGE)
- Characters: Liam, Hank, Sarah, Children, Girly (observed)
- Summary: Liam staring through binoculars. Liam stalks the family over two days. Goes through their mail (voiceover
narrates). Gives family overview: wife Sarah, three kids, Hank's prepper lifestyle. Notes hunting awards through windows.
Observes the family dog Girly—a Rottweiler. Learns schedules, routines, the layout of the large property. Plans the
attack.

### Scene 6 - Let's Do This
- Location: EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - DAY
- Characters: Liam, Family (leaving)
- Summary: Liam watches the family leave—Sarah and the kids drive off. Only Hank's car remains, but Hank isn't home yet.
Voiceover ends with "Let's do this." Liam approaches the property to begin the hunt.

## Act 2 - The Hunt

### Scene 7 - The Flat Tire
- Location: EXT. SUBURBAN STREET / REEVES PROPERTY - DUSK
- Characters: Liam, Hank
- Summary: Liam plants a bear trap in Hank's driveway, shredding his tire. Hank sees the sabotage and knows it's an
ambush. First crossbow arrow misses; second hits Hank's thigh. Hank throws a flashbang and sprints for the house. A nail
trap in the porch handrail impales his hand. They fight on the porch—Hank catches Liam's knife arm and throws him over
the railing. Hank gets inside and locks up. Liam hurls objects through windows: "We're just getting started!"

### Scene 8 - The Battle Inside
- Location: INT. REEVES HOUSE - DUSK
- Characters: Liam, Hank
- Summary: Hank wraps his wounded hand with his blackbelt as a tourniquet and grabs a sai from the closet. Liam enters
through a shattered window. Hank ambushes him—disarms his knife with the sai. Liam fights dirty, throwing furniture and
anything he can grab. Hank hesitates to destroy his own home, which costs him. Brutal back-and-forth: "This wasn't
supposed to be this hard." Hank nearly wins—spinning leg takedown, gets on top, breaks Liam's ribs—but Liam grabs a
broken table leg and stabs him. "Good game." Hank's last words: "My family. They're not part of this." Liam: "You were
[the challenge]." Shoots him. Headlights sweep the walls—the family is home.

### Scene 9 - The Hostage
- Location: INT./EXT. REEVES HOUSE - DUSK
- Characters: Liam, Hank (dead), Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia
- Summary: The family arrives home to a dark house. Olivia runs inside before Sarah can stop her. Emma screams. Sarah
bursts in to find: destroyed room, Hank dead in the hallway, Liam holding Olivia hostage with a knife. The exchange:
"If you ever come back, you're dead." "I already got what I came for." "Then I'll have to come for you." "Catch me if
you can." Liam releases Olivia, maces the room, flees. Sarah pulls her own gun (same model as Hank's) and fires blindly.
Liam drops his jacket in his escape. Sarah finds a Cabela's receipt in the pocket—dated a few days ago. Her first lead.

### Scene 10 - Police Confrontation
- Location: REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT (same night as Hank's murder)
- Characters: Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia, Detective Martinez, Detective Cole, Crime Scene Techs
- Summary: Police process the crime scene. They seize all discharged handguns as evidence but leave a .22 rifle (not
involved). Sarah confronts detectives—uses her legal background to argue the threat is credible. Police are dismissive,
offer "increased patrols." Sarah watches Hank's body wheeled out in a bag. FLASHBACK: A past domestic violence case where
Sarah warned cops, they failed, the wife was killed anyway. FLASHBACK: Meeting Hank at a self-defense class after that
case—he's the first person who could match her intensity. FLASH FORWARD (IMAGINED): Sarah imagines a future where she's
telling someone they never found Hank's killer—the imagined failure drives her to act. Something hardens behind her eyes:
"Not again."

### Scene 11 - Bobby Arrives
- Location: EXT./INT. REEVES HOUSE - NEXT MORNING
- Characters: Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia, Bobby
- Summary: Sarah is at the printer collecting documents. Bobby arrives—Emma and Olivia run to him: "Unky Buh-by!" Bobby
offers Sarah an untraceable gun, warns about offense vs. defense. Sarah takes it, checks it professionally—her sleeve
rides up revealing a tattoo Bobby's never seen. "Oh, Hank never told you?" FLASHBACK: Moxie's bar—Hank subdues a drunk,
but Sarah sucker-punches the guy after. Bar erupts. Hank gets cut—the wound that becomes his facial scar. FLASHBACK: The
ultimatum—"You have to choose. Fighting—or a family with me." She chose family. Back in present: Emma asks where she's
going. Sarah puts the gun down: "We are just finding him and reporting him." Eli insists on going with her.

### Scene 12 - The Investigation
- Location: CABELA'S / PARKING LOT / MULLIGAN'S BAR / SARAH'S CAR - LATE MORNING TO NOON
- Characters: Sarah, Eli, Cabela's Manager, Bobby (phone), Bartender, Detective Martinez (phone)
- Summary: Sarah poses as a paralegal with fake legal documents. Uses the Cabela's receipt from Liam's jacket to pull
security footage—finds Liam at the register. Calls Bobby, who recognizes him from the store and remembers he worked at a
bar. At Mulligan's, bartender gives her Liam's name and address. FLASHBACKS interspersed: pregnant Sarah planning
off-grid living (Hank promises "a castle"), pregnant Sarah almost fighting a rude stranger, soccer game years later where
Hank's hand on her arm keeps her leashed, and Sarah teaching kids self-defense too intensely until Hank sidelines her.
Eli forces Sarah to call police—she leaves Liam's name on Martinez's voicemail but withholds the address.

### Scene 13 - The Apartment Fight
- Location: LIAM'S APARTMENT BUILDING / APARTMENT 3B / FIRE ESCAPE / ALLEY - AFTERNOON
- Characters: Sarah, Eli, Liam
- Summary: Sarah and Eli catch Liam leaving his apartment with a bag—his car already loaded with boxes (metal plates,
tools, drone kit). Sarah attacks—brutal fight through his pathetic apartment and murder wall. Eli saves her twice (knife
tackle, baton to skull when Liam strangles her). Liam escapes via fire escape. Sarah JUMPS from the platform and lands on
him in the alley. Ground tussle—both too hurt to finish it, Sarah's ankle injured. Liam escapes, writes "CYA TONIGHT" on
a car window. Sarah wipes the message before Eli can read it: "Nothing that matters."

### Scene 14 - The False Identity
- Location: INT. REEVES HOUSE - KITCHEN - LATER (same day, evening)
- Characters: Sarah, Eli, Bobby, Detective Martinez (phone)
- Summary: Sarah and Eli return battered. Phone rings—Detective Martinez: "We picked him up. Liam Brennan. Found him
across town." Sarah realizes the truth: the stalker used a stolen identity. The man arrested is an innocent victim of
identity theft. She can't prove it without admitting she found the real apartment. Running is pointless—he could be
anyone now. "We need to get ready."

## Act 3 - The Hunted

### Scene 15 - Parallel Preparation
- Location: INT. LIAM'S HIDING SPOT / INT. REEVES HOUSE - LATE AFTERNOON TO NIGHT (INTERCUT)
- Characters: Liam, Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia
- Summary: ~24 hours after Hank's death. Intercut between Liam preparing in hiding (healing, building armor, modifying
drone) and the family preparing at home (building traps, family talk where Sarah snaps "Dad's dead"). Ends with Liam
approaching the house at nightfall and Sarah seeing him: "He's here."

### Scene 16 - The Return
- Location: EXT./INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT
- Characters: Liam, Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia, Girly
- Summary: Liam returns for what he thinks will be easy cleanup kills. He deploys a surveillance drone to patrol the
exterior, keeping everyone pinned inside. Girly the dog runs out to attack him but he uses a taser on her. He walks up
the steps into a prepared battlefield. Molotov door trap triggers—first floor catches fire but fire-resistant
construction contains it. First trap springs. The structure inverts—now Liam is the one being hunted. Liam takes the
first floor; family retreats upstairs but loses access to basement weapons cache. Running battle through the modified
house begins. Each trap uses skills Hank taught his family. Kids shoot down the drone with something they grabbed before
retreating upstairs.

### Scene 17 - Bobby's Return
- Location: EXT./INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT
- Characters: Liam, Bobby, Girly, Sarah, Emma, Olivia
- Summary: The kids hear Bobby's truck and cheer "Unky Buh-by!" Liam hears the nickname from below. Bobby arrives, finds
tased Girly, revives her. They enter together. Bobby goes to basement; Liam burns him on the stairs with accelerant.
Bobby dies with one arm outstretched. Girly attacks—brutal fight damages Liam's armor. Liam gets Bobby's shotgun, shoots
the dog twice. The second shot flings Girly into Bobby's outstretched arm—holding her in death. Liam mocks "Unky Buh-by?"
up the stairs to confirm Bobby's death.

### Scene 18 - Eli's Death
- Location: INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT
- Characters: Liam, Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia
- Summary: Family hears Liam's mocking "Unky Buh-by?" from below—Bobby is dead. Eli, enraged, opens the gatehouse doors
seeking revenge. He charges Liam with bow and knife. Brutal fight—Eli wounds Liam but is overpowered. Eli's dying words:
"You aren't a man." Liam kills him viciously. NOW the doors are open. Sarah and Liam race for them—Liam blocks her. Sarah
retreats, maces Liam, slams interior door. Liam kicks it down. Family in attic with hatch locked. Liam shoots off the
lock with shotgun. Sarah sends girls to wait in secret room.

### Scene 19 - The Escape
- Location: INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT
- Characters: Sarah, Liam, Emma, Olivia
- Summary: Sarah flees into secret passage. Liam catches her at the top of the 3-story shaft. About to throw her down,
Sarah screams for the girls to run. Emma and Olivia open the secret door below—see Liam holding their mother—and scream.
The distraction allows Sarah to grab Liam and throw them both down the shaft. They fall 3 stories. Sarah dies (neck
broken from fall and Liam's grip). Liam is very hurt but alive. Girls escape via step ladder. Liam removes last of his
armor.

### Scene 20 - The Crawling Struggle
- Location: EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - NIGHT
- Characters: Liam, Police, Emma, Olivia
- Summary: Liam walks out of the burning house. Armorless, broken. Police arrive. Emma and Olivia watch from safety. Liam
ties Hank's blackbelt around his head, raises finger-guns at officers: "BANG BANG." Suicide-by-cop. The killer is dead.
The children survived because they called 911.
