# Scene 10 - Sarah's Investigation

## LOCATION
VARIOUS - NIGHT/DAY (Same night → Next morning → Afternoon)

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia, Bobby (phone), Detectives, Cabela's Manager, Bartender, Liam

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Same night as Hank's murder. Police are still at the house, processing the scene. Hank's body hasn't been moved yet.

### Key Beats

**SAME NIGHT - Police Confrontation:**
1. Detectives question Sarah while crime scene techs work around them
2. Police ask about the guns—multiple shots fired, they're seizing ALL firearms as evidence (standard procedure)
3. Sarah pushes back: "He said he's coming back. That's a credible threat."
4. Police are skeptical—they'll look into it, but she should let them handle it
5. Sarah's work history comes out—she's seen this before. "I was a public defender for eight years. I know exactly what happens next. You'll investigate for a few weeks, hit a dead end, and move on. Meanwhile he's out there planning."
6. The cops don't take her seriously. One mutters something about "letting us do our job."
7. Sarah watches them take Hank's body. Cold fury building.

**NEXT MORNING - Bobby Arrives:**
8. Bobby pulls up to the house. Emma and Olivia run to him—"Unky Buh-by!"
9. Sarah thanks him for coming. Bobby's shaken—can't believe Hank's gone.
10. Sarah tells Eli they're going. Emma asks where. "To find him."
11. Eli insists on going with her. Sarah doesn't argue.

**CABELA'S - Late Morning:**
12. Sarah asks the manager to see security footage from a few weeks ago
13. Manager is hesitant until Sarah explains her husband was murdered
14. They find it: Hank and Bobby at the knife counter. And behind them—LIAM, watching.
15. Sarah takes a PHOTO OF THE SCREEN with her phone

**PHONE CALL TO BOBBY:**
16. Sarah calls Bobby from the Cabela's parking lot, shows him the photo via text
17. Bobby recognizes him—"That's the guy who was asking Hank questions about survival stuff"
18. Bobby remembers Liam said he worked at a bar nearby. Mentioned learning.
19. Sarah has a lead.

**THE BAR - Around Noon:**
20. Sarah shows the photo to the bartender working the day shift
21. "Liam? Yeah, he worked here. Quit without notice a few weeks back."
22. Sarah: "Address?" Bartender hesitates. Sarah: "He killed my husband."
23. Bartender writes it on a napkin.

**LIAM'S APARTMENT - Afternoon:**
24. Sarah and Eli approach the building. Run-down, pathetic.
25. They reach apartment 3B. Sarah reaches for the door—it OPENS.
26. Liam stands there with a bag. Leaving. Everyone freezes.
27. Sarah attacks. Brutal fight through the apartment.
28. Eli tries to help—gets knocked down by a falling bookshelf.
29. Liam escapes via fire escape. Sarah pursues, twists her ankle.
30. In the alley, Liam writes "CYA SOON" on his car window. Drives off.

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: Her legal background makes her distrust of police specific and personal—not paranoia, experience. Cold determination as she tracks Liam down.
- **Eli**: Steps up, insists on going. Already channeling his grief into action.
- **Bobby**: Grief-stricken but there for the family. The "Unky Buh-by" moment shows his bond with the kids.
- **Liam**: Seen objectively for first time—pathetic apartment, murder wall of research. The contrast with his self-aggrandizing voiceover.

### Closing
Sarah knows who he is. She knows he's coming back. "We need to get ready."

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FOYER - NIGHT

Crime scene tape. Evidence markers. TECHS photographing the destroyed living room.

SARAH sits on the stairs, wrapped in a shock blanket. Her eyes are red but dry now. Watching everything.

Two DETECTIVES stand over her. DETECTIVE MARTINEZ, sympathetic. DETECTIVE COLE, impatient.

                    DETECTIVE MARTINEZ
          Mrs. Reeves, I know this is difficult. But we need
          to understand what happened.

                    SARAH
          I told you what happened. He killed my husband.
          He took my daughter hostage. I shot at him. He ran.

                    DETECTIVE COLE
          Multiple firearms discharged. We're going to need
          to take all of them. Standard procedure.

Sarah's head snaps up.

                    SARAH
          All of them?

                    DETECTIVE COLE
          Ballistics. Chain of custody. We need to determine
          which weapon killed your husband.

                    SARAH
          I know which weapon killed my husband. The knife
          in that man's hand.

                    DETECTIVE MARTINEZ
          Mrs. Reeves—

                    SARAH
          He said he's coming back. Those were his words.
          "Catch me if you can." That's a credible, specific
          threat of future violence.

Detective Cole exchanges a look with Martinez.

                    DETECTIVE COLE
          We'll look into it. But right now, we need to
          process the scene and let you—

                    SARAH
          I was a public defender for eight years, Detective.

She stands. The blanket falls away.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          I know exactly what happens next. You'll investigate
          for a few weeks. Hit a dead end. The case goes cold.
          Meanwhile, he's out there. Planning.

                    DETECTIVE COLE
          Ma'am, if you'd let us do our job—

                    SARAH
          I've seen your job. I've seen murderers walk because
          evidence got lost. Because witnesses recanted. Because
          some DA decided it wasn't worth the trouble.

She steps closer to Cole.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          You're going to take my guns, tell me to sit tight,
          and leave me here with three children and a man who
          promised to come back.

                    DETECTIVE MARTINEZ
          We can arrange increased patrols—

                    SARAH
          Patrols.

She laughs. No humor in it.

A TECH approaches, interrupts.

                    TECH
          Detectives. Coroner's ready to move the body.

Sarah goes still.

Through the doorway, she watches them wheel Hank out on a gurney. A black bag. Zipped closed.

Emma, Olivia, and Eli huddle in the kitchen doorway, watching too.

Sarah's jaw tightens. Something hardens behind her eyes.

EXT. REEVES HOUSE - DRIVEWAY - NEXT MORNING

A pickup truck pulls up. BOBBY climbs out, looking like he hasn't slept.

The front door opens. EMMA and OLIVIA run out.

                    EMMA/OLIVIA
          Unky Buh-by!

Bobby catches them both. Holds them tight. His eyes are wet.

                    BOBBY
          Hey. Hey, I got you.

Sarah steps onto the porch. ELI behind her.

                    BOBBY (CONT'D)
          Sarah. I came as soon as I could. I can't... Hank...

                    SARAH
          I know.

                    BOBBY
          What do you need?

                    SARAH
          Stay with the girls. We won't be long.

Bobby looks at Eli, then back at Sarah. Understanding dawns.

                    BOBBY
          Sarah, maybe you should let the police—

                    SARAH
          The police took our guns and told me to sit tight.

Bobby doesn't have an answer for that.

                    EMMA
          Where are you going?

Sarah looks at her daughter.

                    SARAH
          To find him.

                    ELI
          I'm going with you.

Sarah meets his eyes. Sees the same cold fury she feels.

                    SARAH
          Get in the car.

INT. CABELA'S - MANAGER'S OFFICE - LATE MORNING

Sarah and Eli sit across from a nervous MANAGER. A security monitor shows camera feeds.

                    MANAGER
          Ma'am, we don't usually let customers review
          footage without a police request—

                    SARAH
          My husband was murdered two days ago. The man
          who killed him was in this store. Talking to my
          husband.

The manager hesitates.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Please.

The manager sighs. Starts clicking through footage.

                    MANAGER
          What day are we looking at?

                    SARAH
          Three weeks ago. Saturday afternoon. Knife counter.

The manager scrubs through footage. Customers blur past.

Then—there. HANK and BOBBY at the counter. Hank demonstrating a knife grip.

And behind them, watching: LIAM.

Sarah leans forward. Her hand grips the desk.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Stop. There.

The manager freezes the frame. Liam's face, slightly blurry but recognizable.

Sarah pulls out her phone. Takes a photo of the screen.

                    ELI
          That's him?

                    SARAH
          That's him.

EXT. CABELA'S PARKING LOT - CONTINUOUS

Sarah and Eli walk to the car. Sarah's already dialing.

                    SARAH
          Bobby. I'm sending you a photo. Tell me if you
          recognize him.

She texts the image. Waits.

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          Holy shit. That's... that's the guy from the store.
          He was asking Hank questions about survival stuff.
          Knives, traps, all that.

                    SARAH
          What else?

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          He said he was learning. Worked at some bar
          nearby. I don't remember which one.

                    SARAH
          Think.

A long pause.

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          Mulligan's? Maybe? It's that dive off Route 9.

                    SARAH
          Thanks, Bobby. Stay with the girls.

She hangs up. Looks at Eli.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Let's go.

INT. MULLIGAN'S BAR - NOON

A dark, mostly empty bar. A few day-drinkers nursing beers.

Sarah approaches the BARTENDER wiping down glasses. Shows him the phone.

                    SARAH
          You know this man?

The bartender squints at the photo.

                    BARTENDER
          Liam? Yeah, he worked here. Quit a few weeks
          back. No notice, just gone.

                    SARAH
          Address?

                    BARTENDER
          I can't just give out—

                    SARAH
          He killed my husband.

The bartender's face changes.

                    BARTENDER
          Jesus.

He looks around. Grabs a napkin. Writes.

                    BARTENDER (CONT'D)
          I don't know if he's still there. But that's
          what was on his paperwork.

EXT. LIAM'S APARTMENT BUILDING - DAY

A run-down building. Cracked sidewalks. Rusted fire escapes.

Sarah and Eli approach the entrance. Eli's hand rests on something in his jacket—a weapon.

                    SARAH
          Stay behind me.

They enter the building. Climb stairs. The hallway smells like mildew and old cooking.

Stop at apartment 3B.

Sarah reaches for the door—

It OPENS.

Liam stands there, bag over his shoulder. Keys in hand. Leaving.

For a split second, everyone freezes.

Recognition flickers across Liam's face. The wife. Here.

Then Sarah LUNGES.

No hesitation. No words. Pure violence.

INT. LIAM'S APARTMENT - CONTINUOUS

She tackles Liam back into the apartment. They crash through the doorframe. Slam into his RESEARCH TABLE—the murder wall of photos and notes exploding around them.

Pictures of her family scatter across the floor. Hank's face stares up at them.

Sarah's on top. Straddling him. Fists HAMMERING down.

                    SARAH
          YOU TOOK EVERYTHING FROM ME!

Left. Right. Left. Each word punctuated by a blow.

Liam covers his face. Eats punches. Tries to protect himself.

She's not trained like Hank. But she's ANGRY. And fast.

Her fist catches his jaw. His lip splits.

Her palm strikes his nose—the one Hank broke. Still healing. It CRUNCHES again.

Liam HOWLS. Blood streams from his nostrils. He bucks his hips—throws her off.

Sarah hits the floor. Rolls. Already getting up—

Liam's faster. He grabs a KNIFE from the scattered mess. Lunges at her.

ELI crashes into him from the side. BATON swinging.

The baton CRACKS against Liam's wrist. The knife clatters away.

Liam spins. Backhands Eli across the face. The boy staggers.

Sarah's back up. Grabs a LAMP from the cluttered desk. Ceramic base. Heavy.

Swings.

CRACK. Connects with Liam's ribs. He doubles over.

She swings again. He catches it. They struggle—

He WRENCHES the lamp away. Raises it over her head—

ELI tackles him from behind. They crash into the BOOKSHELF.

The shelf TOPPLES. Books and clothes and junk avalanche over both of them.

Liam kicks free first. Eli's buried under debris.

                    ELI
          Mom—!

Sarah doesn't wait. She charges Liam.

He sidesteps. Grabs her arm. Uses her momentum.

SLAMS her into the wall. Plaster cracks.

His hands find her throat. Squeezing.

Sarah claws at his face. Can't breathe. Vision darkening—

A BATON smashes into the back of Liam's skull.

Eli. Standing over them. Breathing hard.

Liam releases Sarah. Staggers. Dazed.

He looks at Eli. At Sarah gasping on the floor. At the window.

He runs.

Sarah pushes herself up, coughing.

                    SARAH
          Don't let him—

EXT. FIRE ESCAPE - CONTINUOUS

Liam's already through the window. Stumbling down the rusted stairs.

Sarah climbs through after him. Eli right behind her.

Liam reaches the second floor platform. Blood dripping from his nose, his head. He looks down. Ten feet to the alley.

He jumps.

Sarah doesn't hesitate. She LEAPS from the platform—

—and lands ON TOP OF HIM.

They crash into the concrete together. A tangle of limbs.

Liam's head bounces off the ground. He goes limp for a second. Dazed.

Sarah tries to get up. Her ankle SCREAMS. Something's wrong. She collapses back down, grimacing.

Liam stirs. Blinks. Blood in his eyes.

Sarah crawls toward him. Grabs his jacket.

He shoves her off. Weak. Both of them hurt.

She swings. Catches his jaw. No power behind it.

He swings back. Clips her temple.

They grapple on the ground. Neither can finish it.

Above them, Eli starts down the fire escape ladder. Rungs groaning.

Liam looks up. Sees the kid coming.

He shoves Sarah away. Forces himself to his feet. Wobbles.

Sarah grabs for his ankle—misses.

Liam stumbles toward the street. Half-running, half-staggering.

Sarah tries to stand. Her ankle buckles. She goes down hard.

                    SARAH
          NO!

Eli's still two floors up. Too far.

EXT. ALLEY - CONTINUOUS

Liam reaches the street with a car with dirty windows parked on the street in front of him.

Liam pauses. Uses his finger to write on his car window:

                    CYA SOON

He runs away.

Sarah limps toward the street, fury in her eyes.

Sarah stands at the end of the alley, breathing hard. Eli catches up, holding his ribs.

                    ELI
          Mom...

Sarah, staring at the message on the car and looking around.

                    SARAH
          Now I know who he is. And he's coming back.

She turns to Eli.

                    SARAH
          We need to get ready.

## Production Notes

**Timing:**
- Same night (Hank's murder): Police scene, guns seized, body removed
- Next morning: Bobby arrives to babysit
- Late morning: Cabela's footage review
- Noon: Mulligan's bar
- Afternoon: Liam's apartment confrontation

**Key Story Elements:**
- Guns seized as evidence establishes why family can't just "shoot him" in Act 3
- Sarah's legal background (public defender) makes her distrust specific, not paranoid
- "Catch me if you can" callback from Scene 9 shows Sarah using his own words
- Bobby's "Unky Buh-by" moment establishes his bond with kids (pays off in Scene 14)
- Photo of Cabela's screen (not home security) keeps investigation grounded
- Phone call to Bobby is more efficient than in-person visit
- Liam's pathetic apartment contrasts with his self-aggrandizing Act 1 voiceover
- "CYA SOON" establishes his arrogance and sets up Act 3

**Fight Choreography Notes:**
- Eli saves Sarah TWICE: (1) tackles Liam when he grabs knife, (2) baton to skull when Liam is strangling her
- Sarah fights as "rage-driven avenger" - aggressive, reckless, burns out fast
- Liam caught off-guard but recovers - still dangerous even surprised
- Sarah JUMPS from fire escape and LANDS ON Liam (not just chasing)
- Both injured in landing - Sarah's ankle, Liam's head (dazed)
- Ground tussle: neither can finish it, both too hurt
- Liam escapes because Eli is still two floors up on fire escape
- Accumulated damage: Liam's nose (re-broken), head wound, ribs; Sarah's ankle, throat bruises
- Estimated screen time: 2-3 minutes
