# Scene 14 - The False Identity

## LOCATION
REEVES HOUSE - KITCHEN - LATER (Same day, evening)

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Eli, Bobby, Detective Martinez (phone)

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Sarah and Eli return home, battered from the apartment fight. Then the police call with "good news."

### Key Beats

1. Sarah sits with ice on her ankle. Eli's face is bruised. Bobby hovers.
2. Bobby asks what happened—Sarah says Liam got away.
3. Phone rings—Detective Martinez.
4. "Mrs. Reeves. Good news. We picked him up. Liam Brennan."
5. Sarah goes very still. "Across town."
6. Martinez confirms—different address, but the ID matched. "You're safe now."
7. Sarah thanks him. Hangs up.
8. Bobby asks hopefully if they got him.
9. Sarah explains: "They got someone named Liam Brennan."
10. The stalker stole someone's identity to work at the bar. The man they arrested is an innocent victim of identity
theft.
11. Sarah realizes: he could be anyone now. Any name. Any face.
12. Running is pointless—he could follow them anywhere.
13. "We need to get ready."

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: The moment she hears "across town," she knows. The police have the wrong man. And she can't explain how she
knows without admitting she found the real apartment.
- **Bobby**: His hope dies when he understands. His best friend's killer is still out there.
- **Eli**: Understanding dawns—they're on their own.

### Closing
The police think the case is closed. Sarah knows it's just beginning. The man who killed Hank is a ghost—untraceable,
unknowable. The only option is to stand and fight. "We need to get ready."

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - KITCHEN - LATER

Sarah sits at the table, ice pack on her ankle. Eli across from her with a bruised face. Bobby hovers, worried.

                    BOBBY
          You found him? And you didn't—

                    SARAH
          He got away.

Her phone RINGS. She looks at the screen. DETECTIVE MARTINEZ.

She answers.

                    DETECTIVE MARTINEZ (V.O.)
          Mrs. Reeves. Good news. We picked him up.

Sarah goes very still.

                    DETECTIVE MARTINEZ (V.O.) (CONT'D)
          Liam Brennan. Found him at his residence in the east side. He's in custody now.

Sarah's face doesn't change. But something behind her eyes dies.

                    SARAH
          East side?

                    DETECTIVE MARTINEZ (V.O.)
          That's right. Different address than you might
          expect, but the ID matched. You're safe now,
          Mrs. Reeves.

A long pause.

                    SARAH
          Thank you, Detective.

She hangs up.

Bobby looks hopeful.

                    BOBBY
          They got him?

Sarah stares at nothing.

                    SARAH
          They got someone named Liam Brennan... living on the east side.

                    ELI
          But that's...

                    SARAH
          He stole Liam's identity to work at the bar.
          The man they arrested is just some guy got used.

Bobby's face falls.

                    BOBBY
          So the real killer's still...

                    SARAH
          Out there. Could be anyone now. Any name.
          Any face.

                    BOBBY
          You have to leave! Come to my place.

She looks at Eli.

                    SARAH
          We can't run. He could follow us anywhere
          and we'd never see him coming. At least
          here, we know the ground.

                    BOBBY
          Sarah, that's crazy!

She stands. Tests her ankle. Winces but stays up.

                    SARAH
          Bobby, I am not giving up my castle to my husband's murderer. If he comes back, I'll be ready. Now stop getting in the way and let us prepare.

Bobby senses he is not talking to the same Sarah he is used to.

                    BOBBY
          Fine. You and I both know what Hank would be prioritizing, but do it your way. I'll drive by to make sure you're ok later. Don't worry, I won't come up to the house.

                    SARAH
          I know. It just has to be this way.

Bobby leaves.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          We need to get ready.

## Production Notes

**Key Story Elements:**
- **THE FALSE IDENTITY TWIST**: Liam used stolen SSN/ID for his bar job. Police arrest the real identity theft victim.
Sarah knows it's wrong but can't prove it.
- Sarah can't correct the police without admitting she found the apartment herself—and she wiped the threatening message
- This is why running is pointless: he could be anyone now. Any name. Any face.
- Bobby's hope dying reinforces the tragedy—there's no cavalry coming
- "We need to get ready" is the transition to Act 3

**Timing:**
- Same day as the apartment fight, evening
- Leads directly into Scene 15 (Parallel Preparation)

**Thematic Elements:**
- The system has failed again—just like Sarah's past case
- The stalker's "invisible man" nature is now literal: he has no real identity
- Sarah's decision to stay and fight is now the only rational choice, not stubbornness
