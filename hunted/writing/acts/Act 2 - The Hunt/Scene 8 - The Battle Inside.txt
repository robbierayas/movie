# Scene 8 - The Battle Inside

## LOCATION
INT. REEVES HOUSE - DAY

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Hank

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
The fight moves inside the house. Both men are already injured from the outside battle.

### Key Beats
1. Hank wraps his wounded hand with his blackbelt (tourniquet) and grabs a sai from the closet
2. Liam enters through a shattered window. Hank ambushes him, disarms his knife with the sai
3. Liam fights dirty - throws furniture, lamps, anything. Hank hesitates to destroy his home, which costs him
4. Brutal back-and-forth. "This wasn't supposed to be this hard." Hank nearly wins multiple times
5. Hank nearly wins - spinning leg takedown, gets on top, breaks Liam's ribs - but Liam grabs a broken table leg and
stabs him
6. "Good game." Hank's last words: "My family. They're not part of this." Liam: "You were [the challenge]." Shoots him

### Character Moments
- **Hank**: Fights with everything he has. Dies hard.
- **Liam**: Realizes he underestimated his target. Frustrated, injured, but survives.

### Closing
Hank is dead. Liam is wounded, exhausted. Before he can collect himself—headlights outside.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Hank moves through the dim house. Blood trails behind him from his mangled hand.

He stops at a hallway closet. Opens it. Inside: a Karate uniform on a hangar, a BLACK BELT hanging on a hook, a SAI on the ground.

He wraps the belt tight around his wounded hand. Pulls it tight with his teeth.

He takes one sai. Tests its weight. Familiar.

A CREAK from the living room.

Hank turns and freezes, listening in the darkness.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Liam climbs through a shattered window. Glass crunches under his boots. Blood dried on his face from cuts.

He scans the room. Hunting trophies on the walls. Competition ribbons. Family photos.

Liam, muttering.

                    LIAM
          Some fortress.

He draws his hunting knife. Moves deeper into the house.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS

Hank waits around a corner. Back pressed to the wall. Breathing controlled despite the pain.

He can hear Liam's footsteps. Boot on hardwood. Careful now. Hunting.

Closer.

Closer.

Liam rounds the corner—

Hank STRIKES.

The sai flashes out. It hits Liam in the shoulder. Liam steps back then stabs forward at Hank. Hank catches Liam's knife
between the twin prongs. TWISTS.

The hunting knife clatters to the floor.

Liam throws a wild haymaker off-balance with the other hand.

Hank ducks under it. Drives the blunt end of the sai into Liam's ribs.

Liam doubles over, gasping.

                    LIAM
          The fuck—

Hank doesn't let him finish.

Hank closes in but Liam backpedals faster—Hank's injuries slowing him. Liam grabs a framed PHOTO off the wall, swings it at Hank.

Hank blocks, strikes with the sai at Liam's shoulder.

Liam backpedals. Crashes into a SIDE TABLE. A lamp wobbles. Liam grabs it—holds it up like a weapon.

Hank hesitates. His house. His things. Sarah's grandmother's lamp.

That hesitation costs him. Liam hurls it. Hank instinctively catches it—hands full for one second too long.

Liam grabs the side table. Swings it at Hank. Full strength. Desperate.

CRACK. It connects with Hank's shoulder. Wood splinters. Hank staggers sideways.

Liam swings again. Hank blocks with the sai—absorbs the blow—but the impact pushes him back.

Liam sees the weakness. Targets it.

He grabs a BOOKSHELF. Throws it down toward Hank.

The shelf TOPPLES. Books avalanche. Hunting trophies crash. Photo frames shatter. All of it piling onto Hank.

Hank disappears under the debris.

Liam backs away. Breathing hard. Holding at his ribs.

                    LIAM
          This wasn't supposed to be this hard.

The debris SHIFTS.

Hank rises like a specter from the wreckage. Bleeding from a gash on his forehead now. Glass in his hair. But still
standing. Now holding the knife and sai.

He doesn't respond. Doesn't waste breath.

He closes distance. Slices Liam's forearm—a shallow cut, but it bleeds.

Hank tries to slice again but Liam blocks with a pillow and tackles Hank. They clash in the LIVING ROOM. Trading blows.

Hank is faster. More skilled. Every strike precise. Controlled power. But Liam fights dirty.

Liam lands an elbow to the throat when Hank commits to a strike. Hank gags, steps back. Liam steps toward Hank and Hank
prepares.

Liam tries a knee to the groin. Hank blocks, forces Liam against the wall, and punches ribs breaking some.
Liam forces him off.

Liam grabs the FIREPLACE POKER. Heavy iron. Swings wild.

Hank brings up the sai to block—CLANG—the impact jars his wounded hand. The makeshift bandage slips. Blood flows fresh.

Liam swings again at the arrow wound. Hank SCREAMS through gritted teeth. Nearly drops the weapon. Hank steps back.

Liam presses. Swings again. And again.

Hank blocks. Ducks. Sidesteps. Losing ground. Being driven back.

Having had enough, Hank pulls out his gun from concealed holster under his
clothes.

Liam grabs a CERAMIC VASE from the mantle. Hurls it.

It SHATTERS against Hank's skull. He drops to one knee. Vision swimming. Hank drops the gun.

Liam stands over him. Gasping for air. Both of them wrecked.

                    LIAM
          Just... fucking... DIE.

Hank looks up. Blood in his eyes. From the floor, he sweeps Liam's legs—spinning takedown. Liam crashes down. Hank mounts
him, drives fists into the broken ribs.

Liam thrashes and kicks. His fingers scrabble across the debris-strewn floor.

They close on something.

A BROKEN TABLE LEG. Jagged end. Eighteen inches of splintered wood.

Liam swings it up. No aim. Just force.

The jagged end DRIVES into Hank's side. Below the ribs. Deep.

Hank gasps. Eyes go wide. Hank's grip on Liam loosens.

He looks down. Sees the wood jutting from his torso. Dark blood welling around it.

Hank's hands find Liam's throat. Squeezing. But his strength is draining. His grip loosens.

He slumps sideways. Off of Liam. Onto his back.

He stares at the ceiling. Breathing wet. Shallow.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Both men lie in the wreckage, gasping.

Liam sits up first. Sees Hank's gun across the room.

He drags himself to it. Picks it up.

Hank lies on the floor. Breathing wet, ragged. The table leg still in his side. He's not getting up.

Liam staggers over. Stands above him.

                    LIAM
          Good game.

Hank looks up at him. No fear. Just exhaustion.

Hank, barely audible.

                    HANK
          My family. They're not part of this.

Liam shakes his head.

                    LIAM
          I've got no interest in your family, Hank. They're not the challenge.

He aims the gun.

                    LIAM
          You were.

BANG.

Hank's body goes still.

Liam stands there, swaying. Blood dripping from a dozen wounds. He looks around at the destroyed room.

                    LIAM
          Fuck.

He starts to move toward the door—

HEADLIGHTS sweep across the walls through the broken windows.

A car pulling up the driveway.

Liam freezes.
