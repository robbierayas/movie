# Scene 11 - Bobby Arrives

## LOCATION
REEVES HOUSE - NEXT MORNING

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia, Bobby

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Next morning. Bobby arrives to help with the kids. Sarah is at the printer, collecting documents. The house still shows
signs of the previous night's violence.

### Key Beats

1. Bobby pulls up to the house. Emma and Olivia run to him—"Unky Buh-by!"
2. Sarah thanks him for coming. Bobby's shaken—can't believe Hank's gone.
3. Bobby offers his gun but warns Sarah about going on offense vs. defense
4. Sarah takes the gun, checks it. Bobby sees a TATTOO on her forearm he's never seen before.
5. "Oh, Hank never told you?" — implies a whole past life even Hank's best friend doesn't know about
6. FLASHBACK: The Moxie's bar fight—Sarah and Hank at a bar. Hank subdues a drunk. Sarah escalates—sucker-punches the guy
after Hank handles it. Bar erupts. In the chaos, Hank gets cut. The wound that becomes his facial scar.
7. FLASHBACK: The ultimatum—Hank sits her down. "You have to choose. Fighting—or a family with me." She chooses family.
8. Back to present: Emma asks where she's going. Sarah puts the gun down—"We are just finding him and reporting him"
9. Eli insists on going with her. Sarah doesn't argue.

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: The tattoo reveals a past no one knows about. The flashbacks show who she really is—someone who escalates,
who can't walk away. Hank was the one who kept her in check. Now he's gone.
- **Eli**: Steps up, insists on going. Already channeling his grief into action.
- **Bobby**: Grief-stricken but there for the family. The tattoo unsettles him—he's seeing Sarah for the first time.
- **Emma**: Already sensing her mother's darkness. "To find him?" She knows.

### Closing
The flashbacks reveal the truth: Hank's scar came from Sarah's inability to walk away from a fight. She chose family
once. Now, with Hank gone, what's keeping her? She leaves the gun on the counter—but everyone knows it's a gesture.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - OFFICE - NEXT MORNING

Sarah stands at the printer, collecting pages as they spit out. Official-looking documents.

Through the window, she sees a pickup truck pull up the driveway.

She gathers the pages. Slips them into a folder.

EXT. REEVES HOUSE - DRIVEWAY - CONTINUOUS

Bobby climbs out, looking like he hasn't slept.

The front door opens. EMMA and OLIVIA run out.

                    EMMA/OLIVIA
          Unky Buh-by!

Bobby catches them both. Holds them tight. His eyes are wet.

                    BOBBY
          Hey. Hey, I got you.

Sarah steps onto the driveway. ELI behind her.

                    BOBBY (CONT'D)
          Sarah. I came as soon as I could. I can't... Hank...

                    SARAH
          I know. Emma! Olivia! Let him get inside! Go finish eating.

The kids go ahead to the kitchen. Bobby and Sarah stand on the driveway for a moment, talking.

                    BOBBY
          What do you need?

                    SARAH
          Stay with the kids. I won't be long.

Bobby looks at Eli, then back at Sarah. Understanding dawns.

                    BOBBY
          Sarah, maybe you should let the police—

                    SARAH
          The police took our guns and told me to sit tight.

                    BOBBY
          They took all of them?

                    SARAH
          Yeah... but I just need one.

                    BOBBY
          Whoa I know you wanna make it right, but you have the kids.

                    SARAH
          It's to protect them.

                    BOBBY
          Is it? Now I know you know, but you are going to have to set the example for them from now on.

                    SARAH
          So you don't think I can do it?

                    BOBBY
          It's like... this house is setup for defense, not offense, because you can last longer on defense. That's what
          Hank would...

He trails off, still grappling with the loss.

                    BOBBY (CONT'D)
          You know what he used to tell me? Every time
          I'd show him a new gun? "Live by the sword,
          die by the sword, Bobby." Every single time.

Bobby hesitates. Then reaches into his truck, pulls out a HANDGUN. Holds it out to her.

                    BOBBY
          Well, I don't feel right giving this to you, but I don't feel right keeping it from you.

He hands her the gun.

                    BOBBY
          Johnny Law isn't gonna be able to trace this old boy. And I know you know, but if they do catch you,...

                    SARAH
          Call my lawyer.

Sarah takes it, unsmiling. Checks the chamber. Professional.

As she does, her sleeve rides up. Bobby catches a glimpse of a "lover not a fighter" TATTOO on her back. Old ink.

                    BOBBY
          What the... is that a tattoo?

Sarah stretches to make it easier to see.

                    SARAH
          Oh, Hank never told you?

A flicker of something—almost amusement—crosses her dead eyes.

FLASHBACK — INT. MOXIE'S BAR — NIGHT (YEARS EARLIER)

Sarah and Hank, younger. Dating. Laughing over beers at a corner table.

A DRUNK GUY at the next table gets loud, shoving his BUDDY. The buddy stumbles into Sarah's chair.

Hank stands. Calm. Big.

                    HANK
          Hey. Easy, brother.

The drunk is surprised and swings. Hank catches his arm, pins it behind his back, guides him firmly into his seat. Smooth. Controlled.
Over in seconds.

                    HANK (CONT'D)
          Sit down. Cool off.

The drunk sputters but stays seated. The bar relaxes. Hank sits back down. Takes a sip of his beer. Done.

But Sarah isn't done. She leans forward, staring at the drunk.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          That all you got?

                    HANK
          Sarah.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          Big man, pushing people around—

                    HANK
          Sarah. It's done. Let it go.

She holds his eyes. Nods. Sits back.

Hank turns to flag the bartender—

The INSTANT his back is turned, Sarah tosses her empty bottle at the buddy's head.

The table erupts. The drunk's friends throw things and rush Hank's and Sarah's table.

In the chaos, the drunk swings a broken BOTTLE. Hank nearly dodges, but it catches Hank across the cheek. Glass cuts deep. Blood runs down his face.

Hank disarms him and everyone freezes.

Sarah freezes. Staring at what she caused.

                    HANK (CONT'D)
          We need to go. Now.

They walk quickly towards the exit. Hank's hand pressed to his bleeding face. She looks back at the chaos and the blood running
between his fingers.

FLASHBACK — INT. HANK AND SARAH'S APARTMENT — DAY (WEEKS LATER)

Hank sits across from Sarah at a small kitchen table. The cut has healed into a fresh SCAR across his cheekbone. The one
he'll carry forever.

He's not angry. Not judging. Just clear.

                    HANK
          You have to choose.

Sarah looks away.

                    HANK (CONT'D)
          Fighting—or a family with me.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          That's not fair.

                    HANK
          I love you. I've loved you since you wanted to punch my lights out...the FIRST time. But I can't raise kids
          with
          someone who can't think of their safety first.

Long silence. Sarah's jaw works.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          What if I can't stop? I can't make myself forget the things that happened... the things I saw.

                    HANK
          That's different. If you can't control it, I am not going to be make you control it. But you can try to control
          it...if you choose to.

She looks at him. The scar she gave him. The steady eyes she doesn't deserve.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          I choose you.

He reaches across. Takes her hand.

END FLASHBACKS.

INT. REEVES HOUSE — KITCHEN — PRESENT

Bobby watches Sarah tuck the gun into her waistband.

                    EMMA
          Where are you going?

Sarah looks at her daughter.

                    SARAH
          Out for a bit.

                    EMMA
          To find him?

                    SARAH
          To find him.

                    ELI
          I'm going with you.

Sarah meets his eyes. Sees the same cold fury she feels. She wants to say no. But she knows her son.

                    SARAH
          Eli—

                    ELI
          Don't. I'm eighteen. You can't stop me
          from going on my own, so either I go with
          you or I go alone.

Sarah looks at Bobby. He sees it too—she can't win this one.

                    SARAH
          We are just finding him and reporting him.

She puts the gun down on the counter looking at Emma.

                    SARAH
          So I won't be needing this.

They start to the car.

## Production Notes

**Timing:**
- Next morning after Hank's murder
- Brief scene—setup for the investigation

**Key Story Elements:**
- Bobby's "Unky Buh-by" moment establishes his bond with kids (pays off in Scene 17)
- Bobby's warning about "defense vs. offense" foreshadows the final stand
- Sarah leaving the gun shows she's trying to do this right—but also sets up that she's unarmed for the apartment fight
- Eli going with her sets up their teamwork in Scene 13
- THE TATTOO: Bobby sees it, "Oh, Hank never told you?"—implies a whole past life. The tattoo is from before Hank, her
truly wild days
- FLASHBACK: Moxie's bar fight—Sarah sucker-punched a guy AFTER Hank handled it. The chaos gave Hank his facial scar. She
caused the one mark he carried forever.
- FLASHBACK: The ultimatum—"You have to choose. Fighting—or a family with me." She chose family. But now that family is
broken and the choice is unmade.
- The flashbacks recontextualize Sarah putting the gun down: she's done this before—chosen restraint. But last time she
had Hank to choose FOR.
