# Scene 11 - Bobby Arrives

## LOCATION
REEVES HOUSE - NEXT MORNING

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia, Bobby

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Next morning. Bobby arrives to help with the kids. Sarah is at the printer, collecting documents. The house still shows signs of the previous night's violence.

### Key Beats

1. Bobby pulls up to the house. Emma and Olivia run to him—"Unky Buh-by!"
2. Sarah thanks him for coming. Bobby's shaken—can't believe Hank's gone.
3. Bobby offers his gun but warns Sarah about going on offense vs. defense
4. Sarah initially takes the gun, but when Emma asks where she's going...
5. Sarah puts the gun down—"We are just finding him and reporting him"
6. Eli insists on going with her. Sarah doesn't argue.

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: Torn between revenge and setting the right example for her kids. Chooses to leave the gun—for now.
- **Eli**: Steps up, insists on going. Already channeling his grief into action.
- **Bobby**: Grief-stricken but there for the family. The "Unky Buh-by" moment shows his bond with the kids.
- **Emma**: Already sensing her mother's darkness. "To find him?" She knows.

### Closing
Sarah and Eli leave to hunt. Bobby stays with the girls. The gun stays on the counter—Sarah's attempt to do this "the right way."

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - OFFICE - NEXT MORNING

SARAH stands at the printer, collecting pages as they spit out. Official-looking documents.

Through the window, she sees a pickup truck pull up the driveway.

She gathers the pages. Slips them into a folder.

EXT. REEVES HOUSE - DRIVEWAY - CONTINUOUS

BOBBY climbs out, looking like he hasn't slept.

The front door opens. EMMA and OLIVIA run out.

                    EMMA/OLIVIA
          Unky Buh-by!

Bobby catches them both. Holds them tight. His eyes are wet.

                    BOBBY
          Hey. Hey, I got you.

Sarah steps onto the porch. ELI behind her.

                    BOBBY (CONT'D)
          Sarah. I came as soon as I could. I can't... Hank...

                    SARAH
          I know. Emma! Olivia! Let him get inside! Go finish eating.

The kids go ahead to the kitchen. Bobby and Sarah walk to the kitchen, talking.

                    BOBBY
          What do you need?

                    SARAH
          Stay with the kids. I won't be long.

Bobby looks at Eli, then back at Sarah. Understanding dawns.

                    BOBBY
          Sarah, maybe you should let the police—

                    SARAH
          The police took our guns and told me to sit tight.

                    BOBBY
          They took all of them?

                    SARAH
          Yeah... but I just need one.

                    BOBBY
          Whoa I know you wanna make it right, but you have the kids.

                    SARAH
          It's to protect them.

                    BOBBY
          Is it? Now I know you know, but you are going to have to set the example for them from now on.

                    SARAH
          So you don't think I can do it?

                    BOBBY
          It's like... this house is setup for defense, not offense, because you can last longer on defense. That's what Hank...

Bobby hesitates. Then reaches into his waistband, pulls out a HANDGUN. Holds it out to her.

                    BOBBY
          Well, I don't feel right giving this to you, but I don't feel right keeping it from you.

He hands her the gun.

                    BOBBY
          Johnny Law isn't gonna be able to trace this old boy. And I know you know, but if they do catch you,...

                    SARAH
          Call my lawyer.

Sarah takes it. Checks the chamber. Professional.

                    SARAH
          Thank you, Bobby.

                    EMMA
          Where are you going?

Sarah looks at her daughter.

                    SARAH
          Out for a bit.

                    EMMA
          To find him?

                    SARAH
          To find him.

                    ELI
          I'm going with you.

Sarah meets his eyes. Sees the same cold fury she feels. She looks at Bobby, thinking of being the example.

                    SARAH
         We are just finding him and reporting him.

She puts the gun down on the counter looking at Emma.

                    SARAH
          So I won't be needing this.

They start to the car.

## Production Notes

**Timing:**
- Next morning after Hank's murder
- Brief scene—setup for the investigation

**Key Story Elements:**
- Bobby's "Unky Buh-by" moment establishes his bond with kids (pays off in Scene 17)
- Bobby's warning about "defense vs. offense" foreshadows the final stand
- Sarah leaving the gun shows she's trying to do this right—but also sets up that she's unarmed for the apartment fight
- Eli going with her sets up their teamwork in Scene 13
