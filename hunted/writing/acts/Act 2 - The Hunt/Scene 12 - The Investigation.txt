# Scene 12 - The Investigation

## LOCATION
CABELA'S / PARKING LOT / MULLIGAN'S BAR / SARAH'S CAR - LATE MORNING TO NOON

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Eli, Cabela's Manager, Bobby (phone), Bartender, Detective Martinez (phone)

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Sarah and Eli begin their hunt for Liam's identity. They follow the trail from Cabela's security footage to the bar where
he worked.

### Key Beats

**CABELA'S - Late Morning:**
1. Sarah shows the manager phony legal documents—claims she's a paralegal investigating a fraud case
2. She has the Cabela's receipt from Liam's jacket and needs to confirm who made the purchase
3. Manager pulls up the security footage from that date. They find LIAM at the register.
4. Sarah takes a PHOTO OF THE SCREEN with her phone

**PHONE CALL TO BOBBY:**
5. Sarah calls Bobby from the Cabela's parking lot, shows him the photo via text
6. Bobby recognizes him—"That's the guy who was asking Hank questions about survival stuff"
7. Bobby remembers Liam said he worked at a bar nearby. Mentioned learning.
8. Sarah has a lead.

**THE BAR - Around Noon:**
9. Sarah shows the photo to the bartender working the day shift
10. "Liam? Yeah, he works here. Missed his shifts this week though."
11. Sarah: "Address?" Bartender hesitates. Sarah: "He killed my husband."
12. Bartender writes it on a napkin.

**THE CALL:**
13. Eli tells Sarah she needs to call the police with the name
14. Sarah calls, gets voicemail—leaves a message with Liam's name but hangs up without giving the address
15. Eli: "You didn't give them the address." Sarah: "We'll go check if he's even there first."

### Flashbacks (interspersed during investigation)

**SETTLED BUT NOT CURED** — Three flashbacks showing the darkness never fully died after the ultimatum:
1. **Pregnant - the castle conversation**: Sarah pregnant, planning off-grid living. Hank laughs—"Kids are gonna need a
real house." Promises to build a castle. Sweet moment showing their dynamic.
2. **Pregnant - almost fighting**: Sarah pregnant at a store. Someone is rude. She steps forward, fists clenching—catches
herself. Walks away. But her eyes don't.
3. **Soccer game**: Years later, kids' soccer game. Parent gets aggressive. Sarah rises. Hank's hand on her arm. She sits
back down. The darkness still there, just leashed.

**HOMESCHOOL FLASHBACK** — Sarah teaching kids self-defense years ago. Too intense. Pushing too hard, scenarios too
violent. Hank gently sidelines her: "I got this, babe." Shows even with family, the darkness leaked through.

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: Cold, methodical. Uses fake legal documents without hesitation—she'll do whatever it takes. A hunter
tracking prey. The flashbacks show this isn't new—Hank spent years holding her back.
- **Eli**: Silent support. Watching his mother lie convincingly. Learning from her.

### Closing
Sarah has a name and an address. She called the police with the name but kept the address for herself. The flashbacks
have shown us: this is who she always was. Hank just kept it contained. Now she's going to find him.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. CABELA'S - MANAGER'S OFFICE - LATE MORNING

Sarah and Eli sit across from a nervous MANAGER. Sarah slides a folder of official-looking documents across the desk.

                    SARAH
          I'm a paralegal with Hartfield & Associates.
          We're investigating a fraud case and need to
          confirm a purchase made at this location.

She pulls out the crumpled RECEIPT from Liam's jacket.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          This transaction. A few days ago. I need to
          verify who made the purchase.

The manager examines the receipt, then the documents. They look legitimate.

                    MANAGER
          You have a subpoena?

                    SARAH
          We're still in discovery. This is just
          confirming the individual's identity for our
          records. Five minutes of footage.

The manager hesitates. Looks at the documents again. Sighs.

                    SARAH
          I know it seems out of the blue, but this way we avoid bringing police down to the store.

The manager pulls up the footage. Scrubs to the timestamp.

There—LIAM at the counter. Paying for hunting supplies.

Sarah leans forward. Her hand grips the desk.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Stop. There.

The manager freezes the frame. Liam's face, clear at the register.

                    MANAGER
          That's your guy?

                    SARAH
          That's him.

Sarah pulls out her phone. Takes a photo of the screen.

                    SARAH
          Is there any better angles?

The manager steps backward 1 minute with a keystroke. On screen, Bobby, Hank and Liam are standing together.

EXT. CABELA'S PARKING LOT - CONTINUOUS

Sarah and Eli walk to the car. Sarah's already dialing.

                    SARAH
          Bobby. I'm sending you a photo. Tell me if you
          recognize him.

She texts the image. Waits.

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          Holy shit. That's... that's the guy from the store.
          He was asking Hank questions about survival stuff.
          Knives, traps, all that.

                    SARAH
          What else?

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          He said he was learning. Worked at some bar
          nearby. I don't remember which one.

                    SARAH
          Think.

A long pause.

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          Mulligan's? Maybe? It's that dive off Route 9.

                    SARAH
          Thanks, Bobby. Stay with the girls.

She hangs up. Looks at Eli.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Let's go.

She starts the car. Pulls out of the parking lot.

FLASHBACK — INT. HANK AND SARAH'S FIRST HOUSE — KITCHEN — DAY (YEARS EARLIER)

Sarah, visibly PREGNANT, stands at the counter with a map spread out. Circling remote locations.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          Look at this. Forty acres. Off-grid. Solar
          panels, water filtration, root cellar—we could
          be completely self-sufficient.

Hank leans against the doorframe, watching her with affection.

                    HANK
          Kids are gonna need a real house, babe.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          I'm talking about survival. If something
          happens—

                    HANK
          I'll build us a castle. How about that?

She looks up. Can't help a small smile.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          If you're building me a castle, then I can't pass on that.

He crosses to her. Wraps his arms around her from behind, hands on her belly.

                    HANK
          Castle it is.

She leans back into him. For a moment—safe.

FLASHBACK — INT. GROCERY STORE — DAY (SAME ERA)

Sarah, still pregnant. Loading groceries into a cart.

A MAN in the aisle bumps her cart. Hard. Doesn't apologize. Shoves past.

Sarah's whole body tenses. Her fists clench.

She takes a step toward him—then catches herself. Looks down at her belly.

Walks away. But her eyes don't walk away.

INT. MULLIGAN'S BAR - NOON

A dark, mostly empty bar. A few day-drinkers nursing beers.

Sarah approaches the BARTENDER wiping down glasses. Shows him the phone.

                    SARAH
          You know this man?

The bartender squints at the photo.

                    BARTENDER
          Liam? Yeah, he works here. Missed his shifts
          this week though. No call, nothing.

                    SARAH
          Address?

                    BARTENDER
          I can't just give out—

                    SARAH
          He killed my husband.

The bartender's face changes.

                    BARTENDER
          Jesus.

He looks around. Grabs a napkin. Writes.

                    BARTENDER (CONT'D)
          I don't know if he's still there. But that's
          what was on his paperwork.

INT. SARAH'S CAR - OUTSIDE MULLIGAN'S - CONTINUOUS

Sarah sits behind the wheel, staring at the napkin. Eli waits.

                    ELI
          Call the police.

Sarah doesn't move.

                    ELI (CONT'D)
          Mom. You have his name. Call them.

Sarah looks at him. Sees Hank in his eyes. She pulls out her phone. Dials.

It rings. Rings. Voicemail.

                    SARAH
          Detective Martinez, it's Sarah Reeves. I have
          a name. The man who killed my husband.
          Liam. L-I-A-M.
          (reads last name)
          That's what the bar had on file. He works
          there but missed his shifts this week.

She hangs up. Starts the car.

                    ELI
          You didn't give them the address.

                    SARAH
          We'll go check if he's even there first.

FLASHBACK — EXT. SOCCER FIELD — DAY (YEARS LATER)

Kids' soccer game. Sarah and Hank in folding chairs. EMMA, younger—maybe 8—on the field.

A PARENT on the opposing side jumps up, screaming at the REFEREE.

                    ANGRY PARENT
          That's BULLSHIT! She was offsides! Open
          your EYES!

Sarah rises from her chair. Moving toward the man.

Hank's hand on her arm. Firm. Not rough.

She stops. Looks at him. Sits back down.

The darkness—still there. Just leashed.

FLASHBACK — EXT. REEVES PROPERTY — DAY (YEARS EARLIER)

Backyard. Younger kids—ELI maybe 13, EMMA 7, OLIVIA 5. Sarah is teaching them self-defense. But she's too intense.
Demonstrating holds that are too aggressive. Describing scenarios too violent for their ages.

                    YOUNGER SARAH
          —and if someone grabs you from behind, you
          don't just twist away. You drive your elbow
          back into the solar plexus, then you STOMP
          the instep—

OLIVIA's lip trembles. EMMA looks scared.

Hank steps in. Gentle hand on Sarah's shoulder.

                    HANK
          I got this, babe.

Sarah stops. Something flickers behind her eyes—frustration, maybe shame. She nods. Steps back.

Hank kneels down to Olivia's level. Warm smile.

                    HANK (CONT'D)
          Okay, little bear. Show me your mean face.

Olivia makes a face. Everyone laughs. The tension breaks.

Sarah watches from the background. Arms crossed. The look in her eyes—she knows what she is.

END FLASHBACKS.

INT. SARAH'S CAR — PRESENT

Sarah pulls out of the parking lot. Her face set. Determined.

No one's hand on her arm anymore.

## Production Notes

**Timing:**
- Late morning: Cabela's footage review
- Noon: Mulligan's bar, then call to police with identity (not address)

**Key Story Elements:**
- Sarah uses phony legal documents (printed in Scene 11) to pose as a paralegal investigating fraud
- The receipt from Liam's jacket (found in Scene 9) gives her the exact timestamp to check
- She never mentions her dead husband at Cabela's—stays in character as a legal professional
- Phone call to Bobby is more efficient than in-person visit
- Eli forces Sarah to call—she wouldn't have done it on her own. Shows Eli as the moral check now that Hank is gone
- Sarah gets voicemail, leaves the name but hangs up without the address. Deflects Eli: "We'll go check if he's even
  there first." The rationalization is thin and they both know it
- Sarah gives police the NAME but not the ADDRESS—setting up the false identity twist
- This scene sets up Scene 14's revelation: police will arrest the wrong Liam using this name
- FLASHBACK: Pregnant/castle—shows the loving Hank-Sarah dynamic, his grounding influence. "I'll build us a castle."
Establishes what she's lost.
- FLASHBACK: Pregnant/grocery store—the darkness was there even while carrying their child. She walked away that time.
- FLASHBACK: Soccer game—Hank's hand on her arm. Years later, still leashed. Shows the pattern.
- FLASHBACK: Homeschool—Sarah too intense teaching kids self-defense. Hank sidelines her: "I got this, babe." Even with
family, it leaked through.
- Final image: "No one's hand on her arm anymore"—the leash is gone. All flashbacks build to this: Hank was the only
thing keeping her human.
