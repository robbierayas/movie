# Scene 12 - The Investigation

## LOCATION
CABELA'S / PARKING LOT / MULLIGAN'S BAR / SARAH'S CAR - LATE MORNING TO NOON

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Eli, Cabela's Manager, Bobby (phone), Bartender, Detective Martinez (phone)

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Sarah and Eli begin their hunt for Liam's identity. They follow the trail from Cabela's security footage to the bar where he worked.

### Key Beats

**CABELA'S - Late Morning:**
1. Sarah shows the manager phony legal documents—claims she's a paralegal investigating a fraud case
2. She has the Cabela's receipt from Liam's jacket and needs to confirm who made the purchase
3. Manager pulls up the security footage from that date. They find LIAM at the register.
4. Sarah takes a PHOTO OF THE SCREEN with her phone

**PHONE CALL TO BOBBY:**
5. Sarah calls Bobby from the Cabela's parking lot, shows him the photo via text
6. Bobby recognizes him—"That's the guy who was asking Hank questions about survival stuff"
7. Bobby remembers Liam said he worked at a bar nearby. Mentioned learning.
8. Sarah has a lead.

**THE BAR - Around Noon:**
9. Sarah shows the photo to the bartender working the day shift
10. "Liam? Yeah, he worked here. Quit without notice a few weeks back."
11. Sarah: "Address?" Bartender hesitates. Sarah: "He killed my husband."
12. Bartender writes it on a napkin.

**PHONE CALL TO POLICE:**
13. Sarah calls Detective Martinez with Liam's name from the bar's records
14. She gives them the identity but NOT the address—she wants to find him herself first
15. Martinez says they'll run it, tells her to stay safe

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: Cold, methodical. Uses fake legal documents without hesitation—she'll do whatever it takes. A hunter tracking prey.
- **Eli**: Silent support. Watching his mother lie convincingly. Learning from her.

### Closing
Sarah has a name and an address. She called the police with the name but kept the address for herself. Now she's going to find him.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. CABELA'S - MANAGER'S OFFICE - LATE MORNING

Sarah and Eli sit across from a nervous MANAGER. Sarah slides a folder of official-looking documents across the desk.

                    SARAH
          I'm a paralegal with Hartfield & Associates.
          We're investigating a fraud case and need to
          confirm a purchase made at this location.

She pulls out the crumpled RECEIPT from Liam's jacket.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          This transaction. Three weeks ago. I need to
          verify who made the purchase.

The manager examines the receipt, then the documents. They look legitimate.

                    MANAGER
          You have a subpoena?

                    SARAH
          We're still in discovery. This is just
          confirming the individual's identity for our
          records. Five minutes of footage.

The manager hesitates. Looks at the documents again. Sighs.

                    MANAGER
          What time?

                    SARAH
          2:47 PM. Register three.

The manager pulls up the footage. Scrubs to the timestamp.

There—LIAM at the counter. Paying for hunting supplies.

Sarah leans forward. Her hand grips the desk.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Stop. There.

The manager freezes the frame. Liam's face, clear at the register.

Sarah pulls out her phone. Takes a photo of the screen.

                    MANAGER
          That's your guy?

                    SARAH
          That's him.

EXT. CABELA'S PARKING LOT - CONTINUOUS

Sarah and Eli walk to the car. Sarah's already dialing.

                    SARAH
          Bobby. I'm sending you a photo. Tell me if you
          recognize him.

She texts the image. Waits.

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          Holy shit. That's... that's the guy from the store.
          He was asking Hank questions about survival stuff.
          Knives, traps, all that.

                    SARAH
          What else?

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          He said he was learning. Worked at some bar
          nearby. I don't remember which one.

                    SARAH
          Think.

A long pause.

                    BOBBY (V.O.)
          Mulligan's? Maybe? It's that dive off Route 9.

                    SARAH
          Thanks, Bobby. Stay with the girls.

She hangs up. Looks at Eli.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Let's go.

INT. MULLIGAN'S BAR - NOON

A dark, mostly empty bar. A few day-drinkers nursing beers.

Sarah approaches the BARTENDER wiping down glasses. Shows him the phone.

                    SARAH
          You know this man?

The bartender squints at the photo.

                    BARTENDER
          Liam? Yeah, he worked here. Quit a few weeks
          back. No notice, just gone.

                    SARAH
          Address?

                    BARTENDER
          I can't just give out—

                    SARAH
          He killed my husband.

The bartender's face changes.

                    BARTENDER
          Jesus.

He looks around. Grabs a napkin. Writes.

                    BARTENDER (CONT'D)
          I don't know if he's still there. But that's
          what was on his paperwork.

INT. SARAH'S CAR - OUTSIDE MULLIGAN'S - CONTINUOUS

Sarah sits behind the wheel, staring at the napkin. Eli waits.

She pulls out her phone. Dials.

                    SARAH
          Detective Martinez? It's Sarah Reeves... I have
          a name. The man who killed my husband.

She reads from the napkin.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Liam. L-I-A-M.
              (reads last name)
          That's what the bar had on file. He worked
          there until a few weeks ago.

                    DETECTIVE MARTINEZ (V.O.)
          That's good work, Mrs. Reeves. We'll run it.
          Where are you now?

                    SARAH
          Home. With my kids.

A beat.

                    DETECTIVE MARTINEZ (V.O.)
          Stay there. Let us handle this.

                    SARAH
          Of course.

She hangs up. Starts the car.

                    ELI
          You didn't give them the address.

                    SARAH
          No. I didn't.

She pulls out of the parking lot.

## Production Notes

**Timing:**
- Late morning: Cabela's footage review
- Noon: Mulligan's bar, then call to police with identity (not address)

**Key Story Elements:**
- Sarah uses phony legal documents (printed in Scene 11) to pose as a paralegal investigating fraud
- The receipt from Liam's jacket (found in Scene 9) gives her the exact timestamp to check
- She never mentions her dead husband at Cabela's—stays in character as a legal professional
- Phone call to Bobby is more efficient than in-person visit
- Sarah gives police the NAME but not the ADDRESS—setting up the false identity twist
- The lie to Martinez ("Home. With my kids.") shows Sarah is committed to handling this herself
- This scene sets up Scene 14's revelation: police will arrest the wrong Liam using this name
