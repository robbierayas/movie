# Scene 7 - The Flat Tire

## LOCATION
EXT. SUBURBAN STREET / REEVES PROPERTY - DAY

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Hank

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Liam waits near Hank's car, down the block from the property. Hank drives home from work.

### Key Beats
1. Hank's tire goes flat halfway up the driveway - he sees a bear trap embedded in it and knows it's sabotage
2. First arrow misses; second arrow hits Hank's thigh. He throws a flashbang and sprints for the house
3. Nail trap in handrail impales Hank's hand - he goes down on the porch stairs
4. Liam catches up; they struggle on the porch. Hank catches Liam's knife arm, pushes him over the railing
5. Hank gets inside and locks up. Liam throws objects through windows: "We're just getting started!"

### Character Moments
- **Liam**: Confident his plan is working. Quickly learns he underestimated Hank.
- **Hank**: Alert, competent. Fights with everything he has.

### Closing
Hank gets inside and locks the door. Liam throws objects through windows: "We're just getting started!"

## FULL SCRIPT

EXT. REEVES DRIVEWAY - DUSK

Hank's truck turns off the main road onto the long driveway. Gravel crunches under tires.

Halfway up the drive—THUMP THUMP THUMP.

The truck pulls to the side. Hank climbs out, walks around to the front passenger tire. Flat.

He crouches. Examines it.

A bear trap twisted with the tire rubber.

Hank's head snaps up. His eyes scan the treeline.

Hank, quietly.

                    HANK
          Shit.

Movement in the trees. Hank spots it.

From a different direction, an ARROW whistles through the air—

Hank dives. Hits gravel hard. The arrow THUNKS into the truck door where his head was.

He's up and running before the echo fades. Sprinting toward the house. Two hundred feet of open ground.

His boots pound the dirt. Heart hammering. Eyes locked on the porch.

A bush is along Hank's path to the front door. Liam stands from it aiming the crossbow. Hank freezes long enough for Liam
to get a shot.

Another arrow hisses. The shot goes into Hank's right thigh.

Liam nocks another arrow, walking calmly. No rush. The predator's confident pace.

                    LIAM
          Looks like I overthought it. I thought this would be a challenge.

Hank doesn't waste breath answering. Fifty feet to the porch.

He reaches into his pocket—pulls out a FLASHBANG. Pops the pin.

Throws it to Liam without looking and begins running for door. Muscle memory. Combat instinct.

BANG. Blinding white light.

Liam staggers, eyes watering. Drops the bow. Claws at his face.

                    LIAM
          Fuck—

EXT. REEVES PORCH - CONTINUOUS

Hank reaches the porch stairs. Takes them two at a time—

His hand grabs the HANDRAIL.

His momentum stops dead.

NAILS. Driven through from underneath. Points up. Five of them buried in his palm.

Hank SCREAMS. Tries to pull free—the nails TEAR through flesh. Blood sprays across the wood.

His legs buckle. He goes down hard on the porch steps.

He begins to pick himself up to open the door.

Behind him, Liam recovers. Blinks away tears. Sees his target crawling.

He CHARGES up the stairs, pulling out a hunting knife.

Liam drops onto Hank's back. Knee between the shoulder blades. HUNTING KNIFE drawn.

Hank bucks. Rolls. They tumble together. The arrow causing Hank a lot of pain.

Now Liam's on top, knife plunging down—

Hank catches the blade-arm with his good hand. Both hands locked on Liam's wrist. The knife trembles three inches from
his throat.

Liam's weight behind it. Pushing.

Hank's wounded hand slips. Blood making everything slick.

                    LIAM
          Some expert.


Hank focuses himself and begins pushing up on Liam. Knife is six inches away. Then Hank throws Liam off him and both
stand.

Liam tries to run at Hank with the knife. Hank deflects the knife but doesn't fully disarm Liam. In another move, Hank
pushes Liam away from the house.

Liam flies backward. Crashes over the porch railing. He falls four feet and hits the ground hard.

Hank's already moving. One hand gushing blood, dripping down his fingers and arm. He fumbles for his keys with his good hand.

Liam (o.s.) laughs.

                    LIAM (O.S.)
          Oh, you're good...

Hank jams the key in the lock. Turns it.

He's inside. SLAMS the door. DEADBOLT. CHAIN.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Hank backs away from the door, breathing hard. Blood dripping from his ruined hand.

He looks at the wound. Deep. Multiple punctures.

He looks at his leg. He pushes the head of the arrow further through and breaks it off.

A CRASH.

He spins. A ROCK smashes through the living room window. He watches the window. He continues pulling the arrow out and
tying a sweater hanging by the door as a bandage.

Another CRASH. Another window.

EXT. REEVES PORCH - CONTINUOUS

Liam grabs anything he can find—rocks, a garden gnome, a potted plant—hurling them through windows.

                    LIAM
          Come on out, Hank! We're just getting started!

Glass shatters. Again. Again.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Hank doesn't panic. He's already moving deeper into the house.

He knows this isn't over. It's just beginning.
