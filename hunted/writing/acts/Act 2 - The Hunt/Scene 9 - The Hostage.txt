# Scene 9 - The Hostage

## LOCATION
INT./EXT. REEVES HOUSE - DUSK

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Hank (dead), Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Headlights. The family is home early. Liam is still inside with Hank's body.

### Key Beats
1. The family pulls up to the house—something feels wrong (no lights)
2. Olivia runs inside before Sarah can stop her. Emma and Eli follow.
3. Sarah hears Emma's scream from outside, runs to the door
4. Sarah bursts in to see: destroyed room, Hank dead in hallway, Liam holding Olivia hostage
5. Sarah's face cycles through grief, horror, rage—then goes cold
6. The exchange:
   - Sarah: "If you ever come back, you're dead."
   - Liam: "I already got what I came for."
   - Sarah: "Then I'll have to come for you."
   - Liam: "Catch me if you can."
7. Liam releases Olivia, maces the room, flees. Sarah pulls her own gun (same model as Hank's), fires blindly
8. Liam drops his jacket in his escape. Sarah finds a Cabela's receipt in the pocket—dated three weeks ago

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: Sees her husband dead. Rage and grief in an instant, but protects the kids first.
- **Liam**: Doesn't care about witnesses—this is just another challenge.
- **Olivia**: Terrified, used as a shield.
- **Eli/Emma**: Horrified, helpless.

### Closing
Liam vanishes into the trees, but leaves his jacket behind. Sarah finds a Cabela's receipt in the pocket—her first lead.
She holds her children on the porch, eyes streaming from the mace, staring at the darkness. Her whispered vow: "I'll find
you."

## FULL SCRIPT

EXT. REEVES DRIVEWAY - DUSK

SARAH's SUV pulls up the driveway. The house looks dark. Wrong.

Sarah frowns at the dark windows.

                    SARAH
          Why aren't the lights on?

The car stops. OLIVIA (10) unbuckles first, already opening her door.

                    SARAH
          Olivia, wait—

But Olivia's out, running toward the front door. EMMA (12) follows. ELI (18) trails behind them, headphones around his
neck.

                    OLIVIA
          Dad! We're home!

The front door is ajar. Olivia pushes through.

EXT. REEVES DRIVEWAY - CONTINUOUS

Sarah is halfway to the door when Emma SCREAMS from inside.

                    EMMA (O.S.)
          DAD!

Sarah runs.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FOYER - CONTINUOUS

Sarah bursts through the door.

The living room is DESTROYED. Furniture shattered. Glass everywhere.

And in the hallway—HANK. Motionless. Blood pooled beneath him.

Eli holds Emma back, both frozen in horror. And in the corner—LIAM has Olivia, hand over her mouth, knife to her throat.

Her face cycles through grief, horror, rage—then settles into something cold.

                    SARAH
          Let her go.

                    LIAM
          I'm walking out of here. She comes with me to the car. Then I let her go.

Sarah's eyes flick to Hank's body. Back to Liam.

                    SARAH
          If you ever come back, you're dead.

Liam smiles. Blood still crusted on his face.

                    LIAM
          I already got what I came for.

                    SARAH
          Then I'll have to come for you.

Something flickers in Liam's eyes. Interest. Challenge.

                    LIAM
          Catch me if you can.

He backs toward the door, Olivia still in his grip. Sarah doesn't move. Eli holds Emma, both frozen.

At the threshold, Liam shoves Olivia forward—

And sprays BEAR MACE into the room.

Sarah screams. Eyes burning. She reaches blindly—pulling her gun from her hidden holster. The gun is the same model as
Hank's.

She FIRES. Once. Twice. Three times. Wild shots into the darkness.

EXT. REEVES HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Liam sprints across the open ground toward the treeline. A bullet whizzes near his head. He doesn't slow.

He vanishes into the trees.

A JACKET lies crumpled on the ground near the porch—dropped in his escape.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Sarah drops to her knees, still half-blind.

                    ELI
          Mom?

                    SARAH
          Come out here. Just stay out of the
          mace. The police will get here soon.

Olivia crashes into her, sobbing. Emma and Eli pile on.

Sarah holds them. All of them. Eyes streaming, staring at the darkness beyond the porch.

Her eyes land on the jacket in the yard. She releases the kids for a moment, walks to it. Picks it up.

She checks the pockets. Finds a crumpled RECEIPT. Cabela's. Dated three weeks ago.

She stares at it. Pockets it.

Sarah's voice, barely a whisper.

                    SARAH
          I'll find you.
