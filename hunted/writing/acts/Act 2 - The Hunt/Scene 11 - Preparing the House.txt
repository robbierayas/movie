# Scene 11 - Preparing the House

## LOCATION
INT. REEVES HOUSE - LATE AFTERNOON TO SUNDOWN

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Sarah has lost Liam's trail but prepares anyway. She knows he'll come back—for the challenge, not to silence witnesses.

### Key Beats
1. Sarah gathers Hank's prepper supplies—the materials for traps. Hiding is not enough. Need active traps.
2. Eli is all-in, eager to help with the most dangerous setups starting a crockpot and setting arrow quiver on second
floor
3. Emma participates but shows growing fear
4. Olivia follows Emma's lead, clearly scared. they close windows and doors of first floor.
5. The house begins its transformation into a fortress
6. The younger two see this going beyond defense into something darker. Eli cajoles them.

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: Grief channeled into cold preparation. The obsession is taking hold.
- **Eli**: Shares the obsession, commits fully.
- **Emma**: Practical but afraid—recognizes this is going too far.
- **Olivia**: Follows Emma, reflects her fear.

### Closing
The house is being transformed. The family is split between those consumed by revenge and those growing scared.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - BASEMENT - LATE AFTERNOON

Sarah descends the stairs. She scans Hank's prepper storage: shelves of supplies, tools, emergency gear.

She starts pulling items: trip wire, steel cables, a propane tank, containers of oil.

Eli appears behind her.

                    ELI
          What do you need?

                    SARAH
          Everything.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR - LATE AFTERNOON

Eli sets up a CROCKPOT near the stairwell. Fills it with cooking oil.

He positions a QUIVER OF ARROWS against the wall. Checks sight lines to the stairs below.

Eli nods to himself.

                    ELI
          Perfect.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FIRST FLOOR - LATE AFTERNOON

Emma and Olivia move through the first floor. Emma closes and locks windows. Olivia follows, doing the same with doors.

                    OLIVIA
          Why are we doing this?

                    EMMA
          Mom said to.

                    OLIVIA
          But... shouldn't we just leave?

Emma pauses. Looks at her little sister.

                    EMMA
          I don't think Mom wants to leave.

They exchange a look. Fear.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - VARIOUS - AFTERNOON TO DUSK

MONTAGE of trap-building:

- Sarah rigs a MOLOTOV COCKTAIL above the main doorway, connected to trip wire.

- Eli tests a CROSSBOW, aiming down the stairwell.

- Sarah positions BROKEN GLASS beneath windows.

- Emma and Olivia watch from a doorway, holding each other.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - DUSK

The family gathers. The house looks different now—weapons positioned, trip wires barely visible, defensive positions established. Late sunlight fades through the windows.

Sarah stands before her children. Eli beside her. Emma and Olivia on the couch, holding hands.

                    SARAH
          I need to talk to you about what we're doing.
          About staying safe.

                    EMMA
          This isn't staying safe. This is—

                    SARAH
          Let me finish.

Emma falls silent. But her jaw is set.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Actually... no. If you have something to say,
          say it. All of you. I want to hear it.

A long pause. Olivia looks at Emma. Emma looks at the floor.

                    OLIVIA
          I'm scared.

                    SARAH
          I know, baby. I know.

                    EMMA
          This is wrong. All of this. Dad taught us to
          defend ourselves, not... not this.

She gestures at the weapons. The trip wires. The transformed house.

                    EMMA (CONT'D)
          He wouldn't want this.

Sarah's face tightens. Something flickers—pain, anger, grief.

                    SARAH
          Dad's dead.

The words hang in the air. Too sharp. Too cold.

Sarah's expression shifts. She regrets it immediately. But she doesn't take it back.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          I'm sorry. I shouldn't have...

She takes a breath. Tries again.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          The police know who did this now. They have
          his name. His face. It's only going to be
          like this until they catch him.

                    OLIVIA
          What if he comes here first?

                    SARAH
          He might not even come here. He knows we're
          ready now. He knows we're watching.

She doesn't believe it. They can all tell.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          But if he does... we'll be ready.

Eli puts a hand on Emma's shoulder.

                    ELI
          It's gonna be okay. We just have to be
          smart. Stay together.

Emma doesn't look convinced. Olivia buries her face in Emma's side.

Sarah stares at the front door. The last light fading outside.

                    SARAH
          He'll come. And we'll be ready.
