# Scene 18 - Eli's Death

## LOCATION
INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Liam mocks "Unky Buh-by?" from the bottom of the stairs. Eli hears it—realizes Bobby is dead. Driven by revenge, he opens
the gatehouse doors to confront Liam.

### Key Beats
1. INT. SECOND FLOOR: The family hears Liam's mocking "Unky Buh-by?" from below. Silence. Bobby is dead.
2. Eli's face hardens. He grabs his bow and moves toward the gatehouse doors.
3. Sarah tries to stop him—"Eli, NO!"—but he's already unlocking the reinforced doors.
4. Eli throws open the doors. "YOU KILLED HIM!"
5. Liam is halfway up the stairs. Shotgun ready. "The one who wouldn't let me leave."
6. Eli fires the bow—arrow hits Liam's chest armor, deflects.
7. Eli charges with his hunting knife. Brutal fight on the landing/second floor hallway.
8. Liam overpowers him. Shoots him in the leg with the shotgun.
9. Eli, dying, spits out: "You aren't a man."
10. Liam kills him with the knife—same method as Hank. "Not even worth the effort."
11. Sarah witnesses her son's death.
12. The gatehouse doors are now OPEN. Sarah and Liam both realize it.
13. They RACE for the doors. Liam gets there first, blocks her.
14. Sarah retreats one room, MACES Liam, slams the interior door.
15. Liam kicks down the weak interior door. Family already in attic.
16. Liam shoots off the attic hatch lock with shotgun.
17. Sarah sends Emma and Olivia to wait in the secret room.

### Character Moments
- **Eli**: Opens the doors seeking revenge for Bobby—his mistake costs his life and leaves the family vulnerable.
- **Sarah**: Watches her child die for the same obsession she instilled. Now the doors are open because of it.
- **Liam**: The "not a man" insult stings—he kills Eli more viciously because of it.
- **Emma/Olivia**: See clearly now that revenge leads to death.

### Closing
Eli is dead. The gatehouse doors—their best defense—are open because of his rage. Sarah sends the girls to the secret
room.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR HALLWAY - NIGHT

The family huddles behind the GATEHOUSE DOORS. Metal-reinforced. Sealed. Their fortress.

From below, echoing up the stairwell:

                    LIAM (O.S.)
          Unky Buh-by?

Silence.

The mockery hangs in the air.

Olivia's face crumples. She knows what it means.

                    OLIVIA
          Bobby...

Emma pulls her close. Sarah's jaw tightens.

But ELI—

Eli's face transforms. Grief becoming rage. He grabs his BOW from where it leans against the wall.

                    SARAH
          Eli. Don't.

He moves toward the gatehouse doors. His hand finds the lock.

                    SARAH
          ELI, NO!

She reaches for him—

He throws the doors OPEN.

                    ELI
          YOU KILLED HIM!

INT. REEVES HOUSE - MAIN STAIRWELL - CONTINUOUS

Liam is halfway up the stairs. Shotgun in hand. He looks up at the boy silhouetted in the doorway.

A smile spreads across his bloody face.

                    LIAM
          The one who wouldn't let me leave. "Two men enter, one man leaves." Remember?

                    ELI
          I'LL KILL YOU!

He raises the bow. Fires.

The arrow SLAMS into Liam's chest—hits the remaining armor plate. SPARKS. Deflects.

Liam doesn't slow down.

Eli drops the bow. Draws his HUNTING KNIFE. Charges down the stairs.

They meet on the landing.

The fight is brutal. Close quarters. Eli is fast—trained by Hank. His knife flashes.

He catches Liam's arm. Draws blood.

Liam grunts. Swings the shotgun like a club.

Eli ducks. Comes up stabbing.

The blade finds a gap in Liam's damaged armor. Sinks into his side.

                    LIAM
          Fuck—

He HEADBUTTS Eli. The boy staggers back.

Liam rips the knife out of his own side. Blood flows.

He raises the shotgun. Aims at Eli's leg.

BOOM.

Eli's thigh EXPLODES. He screams. Crumples.

Can't stand. Can't run.

                    SARAH (O.S.)
          ELI!

Sarah appears at the top of the stairs. She starts toward them—

Liam swings the shotgun toward her and fires. She pulls back as he misses.

                    LIAM
          Stay back!

Sarah freezes. Torn between rushing to her son and the threat.

Liam turns back to Eli. Draws his own KNIFE. The same one that killed Hank.

He crouches beside the wounded boy.

                    LIAM
          Brave kid. Stupid. But brave.

Eli looks up at him. Blood on his teeth. Defiance burning through the pain.

                    ELI
          You aren't a man.

Liam's expression flickers. The words hit something.

                    ELI
          You're just... a thing. A broken thing that pretends—

Liam drives the knife into Eli's chest. Vicious. Angry. The insult cut deep.

He TWISTS the blade. Once. Twice.

                    LIAM
          Not even worth the effort.

But his voice is tight. It stung.

Eli shudders. Goes still.

Sarah watches from above. Hand over her mouth. Tears streaming.

Her oldest child. Her son.

Dead.

Because he opened the doors.

                    SARAH
          No... no, no, no...

Liam stands. Wipes the blade on Eli's shirt.

Looks up at Sarah. Then at the OPEN GATEHOUSE DOORS behind her.

They both realize it at the same time.

The doors are open.

Sarah RUNS.

Liam CHARGES up the stairs.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR HALLWAY - CONTINUOUS

Sarah sprints for the doors—

Liam is faster. He SLAMS through them before she can close them.

She pivots. Runs down the hallway.

Liam pursues.

Sarah ducks into a BEDROOM. Emma and Olivia are already there, retreating toward the closet with the attic ladder.

                    SARAH
          GO! UP! NOW!

She turns. Sees Liam entering the hallway.

She pulls out a can of MACE. Industrial. Bear spray.

Aims at the doorway—

UNLOADS.

A cloud of chemical fog fills the hallway. Liam walks straight into it.

                    LIAM
          FUCK—

He staggers. Eyes burning. Coughing. The Oakleys offer some protection but not enough.

Sarah SLAMS the bedroom door.

                    SARAH
          ATTIC! ALL OF YOU!

Emma helps Olivia up the ladder. Sarah follows.

THUD.

The door shudders. Liam on the other side.

THUD. THUD.

This door isn't reinforced. Regular bedroom door. Hollow core.

CRASH.

Liam's boot EXPLODES through the wood. He rips it off its hinges.

Eyes red and streaming. Rage.

The room is empty.

He sees the open closet. The attic ladder—pulled up.

Above, the ATTIC HATCH. Solid wood. Latched from inside.

Liam wipes his eyes. Studies the hatch.

                    LIAM
          Hank built this.

He examines the edges. The hinges. The latch.

                    LIAM
          Good work. Solid construction.

He raises the shotgun. Aims at the LOCK.

                    LIAM
          But the lock is store-bought. Amateur mistake.

BOOM.

The lock SHATTERS. Metal fragments spray.

He pushes up the hatch.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - ATTIC - CONTINUOUS

Dark. Cramped. Emergency supplies stacked against walls.

Sarah stands in the center. Knife in hand. Between Liam and her daughters.

Behind her, Emma pushes Olivia toward the SECRET PASSAGE entrance.

                    SARAH
          Go to the secret room. Wait there. Don't come out until I call.

                    EMMA
          Mom—

                    SARAH
          GO.

Emma takes Olivia's hand. They disappear into the passage.

Liam pulls himself through the hatch. Bloody. Burned. Maced. Stabbed.

But still coming.

                    LIAM
          Just us now.

Sarah raises the knife.

                    SARAH
          Just us.

He moves toward her—

She backs toward the passage entrance.

                    LIAM
          No more running, Sarah.

He follows.
