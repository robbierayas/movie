# Scene 16 - The Crawling Struggle

## LOCATION
EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - NIGHT

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Police, Emma, Olivia

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Liam emerges from the burning house. Sarah is dead. The girls have escaped.

### Key Beats
1. Liam limps out of the secret basement exit. Armorless. Broken.
2. The house burns behind him. Smoke rising against the stars.
3. He drags himself across the grass. One step at a time.
4. SIRENS. Police cars tear up the driveway.
5. Liam stops. Looks at the approaching lights. No chance of escape.
6. He reaches into his jacket. Pulls out HANK'S BLACKBELT. The trophy he kept.
7. He ties it around his head. A crude bandana.
8. Emma and Olivia watch from behind a police car. A female officer has an arm around each of them.
9. Liam raises his hands—shaped like guns—and aims at the officers.
10. "BANG BANG."
11. The officers FIRE.
12. Liam falls.
13. The nightmare is over. The children survived.

### Character Moments
- **Liam**: Got the challenge he wanted—and it destroyed him. Suicide-by-cop with Hank's trophy.
- **Emma/Olivia**: Safe because they chose the sensible option. They called 911.
- **The irony**: Survivalist ingenuity failed. A phone call succeeded.

### Closing
The killer is dead. The children are orphans. But alive.

## FULL SCRIPT

EXT. SECRET BASEMENT EXIT - NIGHT

Liam pulls himself through the hidden door. Out into the night air.

No armor. Just clothes. Blood. Burns. Broken bones.

The house burns behind him. Flames licking up toward the roof. Smoke blotting out the stars.

He stands there for a moment. Swaying.

Then starts walking.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - CONTINUOUS

One foot in front of the other.

Liam crosses the lawn. Each step agony. But he keeps moving.

The open ground stretches before him. The "kill zone" Hank designed. Two hundred feet of exposure.

Liam laughs. A broken sound.

                    LIAM
          Good design, Hank. Just... wrong direction.

He keeps walking.

Then—

SIRENS.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - DRIVEWAY - CONTINUOUS

Police CARS tear up the driveway. Lights flashing. Red and blue cutting through the smoke.

One. Two. Three cars.

They screech to a halt. Officers pour out. Weapons drawn.

                    OFFICER
          FREEZE! HANDS WHERE I CAN SEE THEM!

Liam stops.

He looks at the police cars. At the burning house. At the long distance between him and freedom.

No way out.

He reaches into his jacket—slowly, deliberately.

The officers tense. Fingers on triggers.

                    OFFICER
          SHOW ME YOUR HANDS!

Liam pulls out HANK'S BLACKBELT.

The worn fabric. The faded lettering. His trophy.

He ties it around his head. A crude bandana. A crown.

                    LIAM
          One more challenge.

EXT. EDGE OF PROPERTY - CONTINUOUS

Behind a police car, EMMA and OLIVIA watch.

A FEMALE OFFICER has an arm around each of them. Protective. Gentle.

The girls' faces are tear-streaked. Hollow. They've seen too much tonight.

But they're here. Outside. Safe.

                    FEMALE OFFICER
          Don't look, sweethearts. You don't have to—

But they look.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - CONTINUOUS

Liam raises his hands.

Slowly. Deliberately.

He shapes them into GUNS. Fingers extended. Thumbs cocked.

Aims at the officers.

                    LIAM
          BANG BANG.

The officers FIRE.

Liam's body jerks. Once. Twice. Three times.

He crumples to the grass.

Falls face-down.

The blackbelt—Hank's blackbelt—still tied around his head.

Silence.

Then the sound of radios crackling. Officers advancing. Checking the body.

                    OFFICER
          Suspect down! We need medical—

But it's too late.

EXT. EDGE OF PROPERTY - CONTINUOUS

Emma and Olivia watch the officers surround the body.

The FEMALE OFFICER gently turns them away.

                    FEMALE OFFICER
          It's over. You're safe now.

Emma looks at the burning house. At the body on the lawn. At the police cars and ambulances arriving.

Then at her little sister.

Olivia's hand finds hers. Squeezes.

                    EMMA
          Let's go.

They turn away.

Behind them, FIREFIGHTERS rush toward the burning house. PARAMEDICS run toward the bodies—Liam on the lawn, the others inside.

But for Emma and Olivia Reeves, it's done.

They chose the sensible option.

They survived.

FADE OUT.

## Production Notes
- No crawling struggle—Sarah died in the fall
- Liam walks out, not crawls—he's broken but mobile
- The blackbelt: Hank's trophy becomes Liam's death shroud
- Suicide-by-cop: Liam chooses his ending, refuses to be captured
- The irony: all the traps, all the preparation, all the death—and the kids survived because they called 911
- Final image: orphaned children, but alive
- The sensible option wins
