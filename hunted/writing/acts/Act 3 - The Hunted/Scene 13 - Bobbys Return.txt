# Scene 13 - Bobby's Return

## LOCATION
EXT./INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Bobby, Girly, Sarah, Emma, Olivia (heard)

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Liam retreats to the basement after the first floor battle. Meanwhile, Bobby arrives—the girls called him.

### Key Beats
1. INT. SECOND FLOOR: The kids hear a truck engine. Emma and Olivia recognize it—they start cheering "Unky Buh-by! Unky Buh-by!" Liam, in the basement below, hears the children's cheering. Notes the name.
2. Bobby's truck pulls up. He sees smoke, broken windows, chaos.
3. He spots Girly's body near the porch—motionless from the taser. Bobby immediately grabs his shotgun.
4. He approaches Girly. She stirs, wakes up. Relief—she's alive.
5. Bobby and Girly move toward the house. They've hunted together before—they have a rhythm.
6. The smoke messes with Girly's sense of smell, but Bobby keeps her focused.
7. They make their way to the basement door. Bobby tells Girly to wait, stay.
8. Bobby starts down the basement stairs. Liam has already poured accelerant on the steps.
9. Liam lights it. Bobby catches fire. He tumbles down the stairs, screaming.
10. Bobby lands at the bottom—one arm outstretched, reaching toward nothing. Dead.
11. Girly hears her friend and ignores the command—jumps down to attack.
12. Brutal fight. Girly tears at Liam's armor, doing real damage—ripping plates, shredding chainmail gloves.
13. Liam fights her off, crawls toward Bobby's body, grabs the shotgun.
14. First shot—Girly yelps, staggers, but keeps coming. Not enough.
15. Second shot—direct hit. The blast flings Girly across the floor. She slides into Bobby's outstretched arm—as if he caught her. Holding her in death.
16. The stairs collapse from the fire damage. Liam removes damaged armor pieces.
17. He climbs out using debris, moves toward the stairwell.
18. At the bottom of the stairs to the second floor, Liam calls up mockingly: "Unky Buh-by?" The children's silence confirms Bobby is dead.

### Character Moments
- **Bobby**: Dies trying to save his friend's family. His "pray and spray" gun ends up being used against the family pet.
- **Girly**: Loyal to the end. Protects her family even when commanded to stay. Dies in Bobby's arms.
- **Liam**: Brutal, efficient, no hesitation. The "Unky Buh-by" mockery shows his cruelty.
- **Emma/Olivia**: Their hope dies when Liam mocks the nickname.

### Closing
Liam climbs out of the basement. Bobby and Girly are dead together. The fight continues upstairs.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR HALLWAY - NIGHT

Smoke seeps up through the floorboards. The first floor crackles below.

Sarah keeps the kids close. Listening. Waiting.

Then—a sound from outside. An ENGINE. A vehicle coming up the driveway.

Emma's head snaps up. She recognizes it.

                    EMMA
          That's Bobby's truck!

Olivia's face lights up.

                    OLIVIA
          Unky Buh-by!

She starts toward the window—

                    EMMA
          Unky Buh-by! UNKY BUH-BY!

Both girls cheer, hope flooding their voices.

                    SARAH
          Quiet! Both of you—

But it's too late. The name echoes through the burning house.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - BASEMENT - CONTINUOUS

Liam crouches in the darkness. Flashlight off. Listening.

Above, muffled through the floor, he hears it: children cheering. A name.

                    EMMA (O.S., muffled)
          Unky Buh-by!

Liam's head tilts. Processing.

                    LIAM
          Reinforcements.

He moves deeper into the shadows. Waits.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - NIGHT

BOBBY's SUV tears up the driveway, headlights cutting through smoke.

He barely stops before jumping out. Shotgun already in hand.

                    BOBBY
          Sarah! Eli!

No answer. Just crackling flames.

He scans the property. The house is burning—first floor engulfed, upper floors still holding. Smoke pours from broken windows.

Then he sees her.

Girly. Lying motionless near the porch steps.

                    BOBBY
          No. No, no, no—

He runs to her. Drops to his knees. The shotgun clatters beside him.

                    BOBBY
          Girly? Come on, girl. Come on—

He checks her. She's breathing. Twitches.

Her eyes open. Dazed. Confused. But alive.

                    BOBBY
          There you go. That's my girl. What happened? What did he do to you?

Girly struggles to her feet. Wobbly but recovering. She shakes her head, snorting.

Bobby looks at the burning house. Sets his jaw.

                    BOBBY
          Alright. Let's go hunting.

He picks up the shotgun. Girly falls in beside him—muscle memory from dozens of hunts.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FIRST FLOOR - NIGHT

The fire has spread but not consumed everything. Smoke fills the air. Visibility is shit.

Bobby moves in, low. Girly beside him.

                    BOBBY
          Find 'em, girl. Find the family.

Girly sniffs. Snorts. The smoke is overwhelming her senses. She whines.

                    BOBBY
          I know. I know. Just do your best.

They move through the wreckage. Past overturned furniture. Broken glass. Blood spots.

Bobby checks corners with the shotgun. Girly checks with her nose.

They reach the basement door. It's open.

Bobby looks down into the darkness. Listens.

Movement below.

Using body language, Bobby and Girly "speak" to each other subtitled. Bobby motions for her to stay.

                    BOBBY(silent, subtitled)
          Stay.

Girly tilts head.

                    Girly(silent, subtitled)
          But...

Bobby motions for her to stay.

                    BOBBY(silent, subtitled)
          I said stay.

Girly whines but sits. Obedient. For now.

                    Girly(silent, subtitled)
          If you're not back in 5 seconds...

Bobby pats her head quick.

                    BOBBY(silent, subtitled)
          I know.

Bobby starts down the stairs. Shotgun first.

                    BOBBY
          I know you're down there, you piece of—

INT. REEVES HOUSE - BASEMENT - CONTINUOUS

Bobby is halfway down when his foot hits something slick.

He looks down. Liquid on the stairs. The smell hits him—

Accelerant.

Liam steps out of the shadows. Lighter in hand.

                    LIAM
          Should've stayed home, gun man.

He flicks the lighter.

WHOOSH.

The stairs ERUPT.

Bobby screams as fire races up his legs, his torso. He loses his footing. The shotgun flies from his grip as he tumbles down the burning stairs.

He hits the concrete floor. Rolling. Burning.

                    BOBBY
          AAAHHHH—

Bobby's movements slow. The screaming becomes groaning. Then nothing.

He lies at the base of the stairs. One ARM stretched out across the concrete—reaching toward nothing. Palm up. Fingers slightly curled.

Liam watches. Calm. Waiting.

                    LIAM
          Spray and pray. Neither one helped.

He steps toward Bobby's body. Eyes on the shotgun that landed a few feet away.

A GROWL from above.

Low. Primal. The sound of something that's decided to die fighting.

Liam looks up—

GIRLY launches herself down the burning stairs. Eighty pounds of muscle and teeth. Airborne. Coming straight at him.

                    LIAM
          Shit—

She hits him like a freight train. All four paws driving into his chest. They go down HARD.

Liam's back slams against concrete. Air explodes from his lungs.

Girly's jaws find his forearm. The armored one. Teeth SINK into the plates—and TEAR.

Metal SCREECHES. Plates RIP from their stitching. She's shredding his armor.

Liam SCREAMS.

                    LIAM
          GET OFF—

He punches her head with his free hand. Once. Twice. Three times.

She doesn't let go. Her eyes are locked on his. Pure animal fury.

She SHAKES her head. The forearm plate COMES OFF—clatters away. Now she's got flesh.

Teeth sink deep. Hit bone.

Blood pours from his arm. Muscle tearing.

They roll across the basement floor. Through debris. Through Bobby's blood.

Girly releases the arm—goes for his throat.

Liam gets his chainmail-gloved hand up just in time. Her jaws CLAMP onto the chainmail.

She BITES DOWN. The rings BURST apart. Metal and leather shredding.

His hand is exposed now. Bleeding from a dozen punctures.

He kicks. Gets leverage. Throws her off.

She tumbles. Rights herself instantly. Circling. Teeth bared.

Liam scrambles backward. His eyes find the SHOTGUN. Five feet away. Near Bobby's body.

Girly lunges.

He DIVES for the shotgun. Fingers close around the stock.

She's on him—jaws snapping at his face.

He swings the shotgun like a club. CRACKS her in the skull.

She staggers. Shakes it off. Keeps coming.

He pumps the action. Aims.

BOOM.

The shot catches her in the hip. She YELPS. Spins. Goes down.

But she's up again. Leg dragging. Still coming. Still protecting her family.

Liam pumps again.

                    LIAM
          Stay down, you stupid—

She lunges.

BOOM.

Direct hit. Center mass.

The blast LIFTS her off her feet. She flies backward across the concrete—

—and SLIDES into Bobby's outstretched arm.

She comes to rest against him. Her body curled into the crook of his arm. His dead hand resting on her fur.

Like he caught her.

Like he's holding her.

Together.

Liam lowers the shotgun. Checks the tube—still shells left. Bobby's "pray and spray" has an extended magazine.

Chest heaving. Arm torn to ribbons. Burns on his legs. Blood everywhere.

He looks at Bobby and Girly. Something flickers across his face—not guilt, just... recognition. They died together. Loyal to the end.

He shakes it off.

Above him, the burning stairs GROAN. CRACK.

The structure collapses. Flaming debris crashes down, blocking the way up.

                    LIAM
          Shit.

He looks at his armor. The forearm plates are gone. Chainmail gloves shredded. Dog bites all down his arm. The leg plates are warped from heat, restricting movement.

He strips off the damaged pieces. Drops them on the concrete. Lighter now. More exposed.

He climbs the debris pile. Pulls himself through the hole where the stairs used to be.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FIRST FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Liam emerges from the basement. Smoldering. Bleeding. Burned.

But alive.

He moves toward the main stairwell. Stops at the bottom.

Looks up toward the second floor. The GATEHOUSE DOORS are visible at the top—reinforced, sealed.

The children are up there. Sarah too.

He calls up. Mocking. Testing.

                    LIAM
          Unky Buh-by?

Silence.

No cheering now.

He smiles. They know.

                    LIAM
          That's what I thought.

He starts up the stairs.

## Production Notes
- Bobby's death should hit hard—he was likeable in Scene 3 and 4
- Girly's death equally brutal—she was family
- The poetic image: Bobby and Girly together in death
- The "Unky Buh-by" mockery: establishes the nickname payoff, confirms Bobby's death to the kids
- Armor damage: forearm plates gone, chainmail shredded, leg plates removed
- Shotgun still has shells—Bobby's extended magazine "pray and spray" gun pays off for Liam
