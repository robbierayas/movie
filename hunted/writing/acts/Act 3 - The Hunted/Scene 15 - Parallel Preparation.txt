# Scene 15 - Parallel Preparation (MONTAGE)

## TYPE
Parallel Montage / Intercut

## PURPOSE
Show both Liam and the Reeves family preparing for the final confrontation. ~24 hours after Hank's death.

## TIME SPAN
Late afternoon to nightfall (~24 hours after Hank's death)

## MONTAGE STRUCTURE

Intercut between Liam preparing in hiding and the Reeves family preparing at home.

---

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. ABANDONED BUILDING - DAY

Liam sits in a dusty corner. Makeshift camp—sleeping bag, first aid supplies, bottled water.

He unwraps bloody bandages from his torso. Examines the wounds. Grimaces.

On the floor beside him: HANK'S BLACKBELT. He picks it up. Studies it.

                    LIAM
          You earned this.

He sets it aside carefully. A trophy.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - BASEMENT - LATE AFTERNOON

Sarah descends the stairs. She scans Hank's prepper storage: shelves of supplies, tools, emergency gear.

She starts pulling items: trip wire, steel cables, a propane tank, containers of oil.

Eli appears behind her.

                    ELI
          What do you need?

                    SARAH
          Everything.

INT. ABANDONED BUILDING - DAY

Liam unpacks a DRONE from a box. Small, commercial model. He attaches something to its underside—a modified taser mechanism.

He tests the controls. The drone lifts, hovers, responds.

He smiles.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR - LATE AFTERNOON

Eli sets up a CROCKPOT near the stairwell. Fills it with cooking oil.

He positions a QUIVER OF ARROWS against the wall. Checks sight lines to the stairs below.

Eli nods to himself.

                    ELI
          Perfect.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FIRST FLOOR - LATE AFTERNOON

Emma and Olivia move through the first floor. Several windows are BROKEN from the earlier battle—glass shattered, frames damaged.

Emma pulls a heavy SHUTTER across a broken window frame. Olivia helps secure it with a latch.

                    OLIVIA
          Why are we doing this?

                    EMMA
          Mom said to.

                    OLIVIA
          But... shouldn't we just leave?

Emma pauses. Looks at her little sister.

                    EMMA
          I don't think Mom wants to leave.

They exchange a look. Fear.

INT. ABANDONED BUILDING - AFTERNOON

Liam does push-ups. Sit-ups. Tests his range of motion. Winces but pushes through.

He examines himself in a cracked mirror. The bruises dark. The swelling still there. Not healed—but good enough.

He picks up a DIRTBIKE HELMET. Holds up small metal plates and a drill.

CUT TO: The helmet now has metal plates drilled around the outside—top, sides, back. DIY armor. Ugly but functional.

He tries it on. Then grabs a pair of big OAKLEY SUNGLASSES. Puts them on under the helmet.

Protection from flashbangs. He can see clearly. Good.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - VARIOUS - AFTERNOON TO DUSK

MONTAGE of trap-building:

- Sarah rigs a MOLOTOV COCKTAIL above the main doorway, connected to trip wire.

- Eli tests his BOW, aiming down the stairwell.

- Sarah positions BROKEN GLASS beneath windows.

- Emma and Olivia watch from a doorway, holding each other.

INT. ABANDONED BUILDING - DUSK

Liam adds the MAKESHIFT ARMOR—a vest covered in small metal plates (~3x2 inches each) sewn together in overlapping rows like fish scales. Arm and leg pieces follow. Chainmail-style gloves with leather fingers.

He looks like a nightmare. A homemade knight.

He picks up Hank's blackbelt. Ties it around his arm like a trophy.

                    LIAM
          Let's finish this.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - DUSK

The family gathers. The house looks different now—weapons positioned, trip wires barely visible, defensive positions established. Late sunlight fades through the windows.

Sarah stands before her children. Eli beside her. Emma and Olivia on the couch, holding hands.

                    SARAH
          I need to talk to you about what we're doing.
          About staying safe.

                    EMMA
          This isn't staying safe. This is—

                    SARAH
          Let me finish.

Emma falls silent. But her jaw is set.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          Actually... no. If you have something to say,
          say it. All of you. I want to hear it.

A long pause. Olivia looks at Emma. Emma looks at the floor.

                    OLIVIA
          I'm scared.

                    SARAH
          I know, baby. I know.

                    EMMA
          This is wrong. All of this. Dad taught us to
          defend ourselves, not... not this.

She gestures at the weapons. The trip wires. The transformed house.

                    EMMA (CONT'D)
          He wouldn't want this.

Sarah's face tightens. Something flickers—pain, anger, grief.

                    SARAH
          Dad's dead.

The words hang in the air. Too sharp. Too cold.

Sarah's expression shifts. She regrets it immediately. But she doesn't take it back.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          I'm sorry. I shouldn't have...

She takes a breath. Tries again.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          The police know who did this now. They have
          his name. His face. It's only going to be
          like this until they catch him.

                    OLIVIA
          What if he comes here first?

                    SARAH
          He might not even come here. He knows we're
          ready now. He knows we're watching.

She doesn't believe it. They can all tell.

                    SARAH (CONT'D)
          But if he does... we'll be ready.

Eli puts a hand on Emma's shoulder.

                    ELI
          It's gonna be okay. We just have to be
          smart. Stay together.

Emma doesn't look convinced. Olivia buries her face in Emma's side.

Sarah stares at the front door. The last light fading outside.

                    SARAH
          He'll come. And we'll be ready.

EXT. RURAL ROAD - NIGHT

Liam's car moves through darkness. No headlights.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - NIGHT

The car pulls off the road. Disappears into the trees.

A figure emerges. Drone in hand. Armor glinting in moonlight.

Liam walks toward the house.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT

Sarah sits by the window. Watching. Waiting.

The family behind her—awake, alert, in position.

Her eyes narrow. Movement in the treeline.

                    SARAH
          He's here.

The outdoor lights turn on detecting Liam's motion.

---

## Production Notes
- Parallel editing builds tension—both sides preparing simultaneously
- ~24 hours after Hank's death - compressed timeline
- Liam hasn't fully healed, just pushed through pain
- Armor construction is rushed but functional
- Family dialogue shows the fracture: Sarah/Eli vs Emma/Olivia
- Sarah's "Dad's dead" is the emotional turning point
- End leads directly into Scene 12 (The Return)
