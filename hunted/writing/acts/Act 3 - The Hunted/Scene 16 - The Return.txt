# Scene 16 - The Return

## LOCATION
EXT./INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Sarah, Eli, Emma, Olivia, Girly

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Night. Liam returns to the Reeves property, expecting easy cleanup kills.

### Key Beats
1. Liam approaches the house with a BATTERING RAM. Camera spots him. He rams the front door down.
2. SECONDARY DOORS slam shut—they don't go all the way to the ceiling. Flashbang is thrown over the top gap. Sarah
reaches her hand with the gun (from Bobby) over the door and fires blindly at Liam. They realize he has armor protection
and retreat.
3. Liam breaks through the secondary door. MOLOTOV TRAP triggers behind it—fire erupts. Some fire gets on his leg and the
house starts burning. Family runs upstairs while he is slowed.
4. At bottom of stairs, Eli fires bow at Liam as he dodges. Sarah gets into position and they fight on the stairwell.
Sarah wounds him but gets hurt and retreats.
5. Family tries to go out window only to see Liam has attack drone keeping them from leaving.
6. Liam yells while going up stairwell "how dare you burn me" as hot oil pours down. He misses most but removes more
armor from burns. He pulls back to recover. When he tries to leave the house, Eli shoots at him yelling "two men enter,
one man leaves." Liam knows he can't just leave—retreats to basement.
7. With Liam in basement, Emma grabs her .22 rifle and shoots down the drone.

### Character Moments
- **Liam**: Overconfident, then shocked, then desperate.
- **Sarah**: Cold, tactical, waiting for her moment.
- **Eli**: Eager to engage, reckless.
- **Emma/Olivia**: Scared, staying back.

### Closing
Liam is now the prey. The family has the advantage—for now.

## FULL SCRIPT

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - NIGHT

Liam crosses the open ground. Confident. DIRTBIKE HELMET on—metal plates drilled around the outside. Big OAKLEY
SUNGLASSES underneath for flashbang protection. MAKESHIFT ARMOR visible beneath his jacket—small metal plates sewn
together, overlapping like fish scales.
Messenger bag repurposed, holding DRONE controller, over his shoulder. BATTERING RAM in one hand. TASER in the other.

Motion-detector floodlights from the house turn on. He's halfway across the yard when—

BARKING. Loud. Aggressive.

Girly charges from the side of the house. Eighty pounds of Rottweiler, teeth bared.

Liam doesn't flinch. Waits.

The dog launches—

Liam pulls out taser gun and fires. The leads hit the dog.

CRACK.

Girly yelps. Convulses. Drops to the grass, twitching.

Liam looks down at her. No emotion.

Liam, quietly.

                    LIAM
          Good dog.

He steps over her body. Continues toward the house.

He spots the security camera on the porch. Looks directly into it.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - LIVING ROOM - NIGHT

Sarah watches him approach on a laptop screen—security feed.

                    SARAH
          Positions. Now.

Eli grabs his BOW and quiver. Moves to bottom of the stairs. EMMA and OLIVIA huddle near the stairs landing, ready to
retreat.

EXT. REEVES HOUSE - FRONT PORCH - NIGHT

Liam reaches the door. A BATTERING RAM over his shoulder—heavy, industrial.

He tests the handle. Locked.

He backs up. Swings.

BOOM.

The door shudders but holds.

BOOM.

Wood splinters.

BOOM.

The door CAVES IN. Liam steps through.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FOYER - CONTINUOUS

The first floor is dim. He moves toward the living room—

SECONDARY DOORS SLAM SHUT around him. Mechanical. Rigged to close from multiple triggers.

But the doors don't reach the ceiling—a two-foot gap at the top.

He's sealed in the foyer.

                    LIAM
          What the—

A FLASHBANG sails over the top of the secondary door. Through the gap.

BANG.

Liam staggers, helmet ringing. Half-blind.

A HAND reaches over the door gap—Sarah's hand. Holding Bobby's gun.

She FIRES blindly. Four shots. Five. Six.

Liam drops flat. Bullets spark off his armor. One grazes his leg.

Liam, growling.

                    LIAM
          Body armor, bitch!

The hand disappears. Sarah retreating.

He crawls toward a door. Kicks it. It doesn't budge—reinforced.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Sarah reloads. Eli nocks another arrow.

                    ELI
          Did we get him?

                    SARAH
          Not yet.

She listens. Movement below.

                    SARAH
          Fall back. Stairs.

They retreat to the stairwell position. Emma and Olivia move higher.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FOYER - CONTINUOUS

Liam finds a weakness in the secondary door. Shoulder-charges it. Once. Twice.

It BREAKS.

The MOLOTOV TRAP triggers. Hidden behind the secondary door. Glass shatters. Accelerant sprays.

FIRE ERUPTS.

Flames catch Liam's leg. He stumbles back, slapping at his pants.

                    LIAM
          FUCK—

His pant leg smolders. The armor took the worst, but his calf is scorched.

The fire spreads to the walls. Smoke billowing.

The house is starting to burn.

Liam pushes through. Reaches the bottom of the stairs.

Looks up.

Eli stands at the top, bow drawn.

Eli FIRES.

The arrow glances off a metal plate on Liam's helmet—CLANG—deflects into the wall beside him. Sticks there, quivering.

Liam flinches. Touches his helmet. Looks at the arrow in the wall.

He starts up the stairs.

Sarah appears beside Eli. She has a HUNTING KNIFE.

                    SARAH
          Go!

Eli hesitates—

                    SARAH
          GO!

Eli retreats. Sarah descends to meet Liam halfway.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - STAIRWELL - CONTINUOUS

They meet on the narrow staircase. No room to maneuver. Nowhere to run.

Sarah has the high ground. HUNTING KNIFE in her grip. Hank's knife. The one he taught her with.

Liam comes up. Armor scorched. Limping on his burned leg. But still moving.

They clash.

Sarah strikes first. Slash across his reaching arm. The blade bites through jacket, through skin.

Blood sprays the wall.

Liam jerks back. She presses. Stabs at his face.

He turns his head. The blade opens his cheek instead of taking his eye.

She's FAST. Faster than he expected.

Another slash. Catches his thigh. Muscle parts.

                    LIAM
          FUCK—

He stumbles. Nearly falls.

Sarah presses the advantage. Years of Hank's training flowing through her. Controlled. Precise.

But Liam is DESPERATE now.

He surges forward. Takes a slash across his forearm—doesn't care. Closes the distance.

His hand finds her wrist. The one holding the knife. His grip is iron.

He SQUEEZES.

Bones grind. Sarah screams.

Her fingers spasm open. The knife clatters down the stairs. Bounces into the darkness below.

Liam throws her. Full strength.

Sarah hits the banister. Wood cracks. She catches herself—barely—hanging over the drop to the first floor.

Liam advances. Blood dripping from half a dozen wounds now. Eyes wild. Smoke rising from the first floor behind him.

Sarah scrambles up the stairs. Retreating. Her wrist hurt, but not broken, she doesn't want to keep fighting now.

Liam tries to follow. His burned leg buckles under him. He catches himself on the railing, grimacing.

The second floor door shuts. The automated shutters on the first floor windows close.

Below them, the first floor is fully engulfed now. Flames spreading from the secondary door trap.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

The family clusters near a window. Emma tries to open it.

EXT. REEVES HOUSE - CONTINUOUS

The DRONE lifts off from where Liam left it. Rises to window level.

The small gun attachment FIRES. Rounds pepper the window frame.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Emma screams, ducking back.

                    EMMA
          There's something out there!

Sarah looks. Sees the drone hovering.

                    SARAH
          He's got a drone. We can't go out that way.

Olivia, crying.

                    OLIVIA
          What do we do?

Below, Liam's voice echoes up the stairwell—rage masking the pain in his burned leg.

Heavy footsteps on the stairs. He's coming.

Sarah moves to the stairwell entrance. The HOT OIL TRAP. Eli is already there, hand on the release.

                    SARAH
          Wait for it...

Liam appears on the stairs. Limping. Armor scorched. Helmet dented.

                    LIAM (O.S.)
          HOW DARE YOU BURN ME!

                    ELI
          NOW!

He releases the mechanism. The pot TIPS. Boiling oil POURS down.

Liam sees it—dives sideways. Most of the oil misses him, but some catches his shoulder, his arm.

He HOWLS. Rips off his jacket. The armor beneath saved him, but burns spread across exposed skin.

He retreats down the stairs. Pulls back into the burning first floor.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FIRST FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Liam staggers through smoke. Arm blistering. Leg burned. Multiple cuts bleeding.

He finds the front door and walks down the steps.

An ARROW slams into the frame inches from his head.

Eli (o.s.), shouting from above.

                    ELI (O.S.)
          TWO MEN ENTER! ONE MAN LEAVES!

Liam spins. Eli stands in a 2nd floor window, nocking another arrow.

                    LIAM
          Can't leave. Can't go up.

He looks at the burning walls around him. The smoke thickening.

                    LIAM
          Fine.

He moves toward the basement door.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Sarah pulls Eli back from the stairs.

                    SARAH
          He went down. Basement.

                    ELI
          I can go after him—

                    SARAH
          No. We hold the high ground. Let him come to us.

Eli's jaw tightens. He wants the kill. But he nods.

Sarah looks at Emma and Olivia, huddled together by the wall.

                    SARAH
          Stay close. Stay quiet.

Emma nods, arm around Olivia.

Below them, the first floor burns. Above them, the attic waits.

And somewhere in the basement, Liam is still coming.

Emma looks at the window. The drone still hovers outside.

                    EMMA
          The drone.

She moves to the corner. Grabs her .22 RIFLE.

                    SARAH
          Emma—

Emma shoulders the rifle. Takes aim through the window.

                    EMMA
          I got it.

She FIRES. Once. Twice.

The second shot clips the drone. It spins, sparks, CRASHES into the yard.

One less advantage for Liam.
