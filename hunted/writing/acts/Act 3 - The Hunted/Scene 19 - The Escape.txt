# Scene 19 - The Escape

## LOCATION
INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Liam, Emma, Olivia

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Sarah reaches the attic. Liam catches her at the secret passage entrance—the shaft that drops three stories to the secret basement room where the girls wait.

### Key Beats
1. Sarah reaches the attic. Liam follows. She heads for the secret passage entrance.
2. Liam catches her in the attic before she can descend. Fight at the top of the shaft.
3. Sarah is exhausted, wounded. Liam is relentless. He overpowers her.
4. He gets his hands around her throat at the edge of the shaft opening—three stories straight down.
5. Sarah, with her last breath, SCREAMS down the shaft: "GIRLS! RUN!"
6. Below, in the SECRET BASEMENT ROOM, Emma and Olivia hear her.
7. The passage shaft opens into the secret room where they are. They look UP—and SCREAM.
8. They see Liam holding their mother by the throat at the top of the shaft.
9. Liam is DISTRACTED by their screams. Looks down.
10. Sarah uses this moment. She grabs Liam and THROWS her weight backward into the shaft.
11. Both of them FALL. Three stories.
12. Liam's grip on Sarah's throat tightens reflexively as they plummet.
13. They hit the bottom. HARD.
14. Sarah is dead—neck broken from the fall and Liam's grip.
15. Liam is very hurt but alive. He lies in the wreckage, groaning.
16. Emma and Olivia, seeing their mother dead, grab a STEP LADDER from the secret room.
17. They use the step ladder to bridge the burned-out basement stairs and escape.
18. Liam slowly pulls himself up. Removes the last of his damaged armor.

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: Sacrifices herself to give her daughters a chance to escape.
- **Emma/Olivia**: Their screams save their mother's dignity—she dies fighting, not executed.
- **Liam**: Survives but is broken. The fall nearly killed him.

### Closing
Sarah is dead. The girls escape. Liam removes his armor and walks out.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. ATTIC - NIGHT

Sarah scrambles toward the secret passage entrance. The shaft yawns open in the floor—a vertical drop, three stories
straight down to the secret basement room.

Behind her—Liam. Bleeding. Burned. But relentless.

                    LIAM
          No more running, Sarah.

She turns. Nowhere to go but down—and the ladder is too slow. He'd catch her on it.

INT. SECRET BASEMENT ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Emma and Olivia huddle in the darkness. Surrounded by Hank's prepper supplies—canned food, water jugs, emergency gear.
The passage shaft opens into the room above them—the ladder running up into blackness.

A STEP LADDER leans against the wall. An emergency exit—a small window near the ceiling.

They listen. Footsteps above. Muffled voices.

                    OLIVIA
          Is that Mom?

Emma listens. Two sets of footsteps. One heavier than the other.

                    EMMA
          Stay quiet.

INT. ATTIC - CONTINUOUS

Liam closes in. Sarah backs toward the shaft opening.

He lunges. Grabs her. They struggle at the edge.

                    LIAM
          Nowhere left to go.

His hands find her throat. He forces her down near the opening. His grip is iron.

                    LIAM
          I could throw you down. Let you fall. But that's too quick.

He squeezes. Sarah's face turns red.

                    LIAM
          I want you to know you lost. Your husband. Your son. Your—

Sarah gathers everything she has left. Her voice is a ragged scream, torn from damaged lungs:

                    SARAH
          GIRLS! RUN! RUN NOW!

Her voice echoes down the shaft. Three stories.

INT. SECRET BASEMENT ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Emma and Olivia hear it. Their mother's voice echoing down the shaft. Desperate. Dying.

                    OLIVIA
          MOM!

They look UP the shaft—three stories of vertical darkness.

And at the top—

Liam. Holding their mother by the throat. Silhouetted against the faint light from the attic.

Emma and Olivia SCREAM.

INT. ATTIC - CONTINUOUS

Liam hears the screams. Looks down.

For just a moment, his grip loosens. His attention shifts.

Sarah's eyes meet her daughters' eyes. Three stories below. One last look.

Then she MOVES.

She grabs Liam's armor. His shirt. Anything she can reach.

And throws herself backward. Into the shaft.

                    LIAM
          WHAT—

His grip on her throat TIGHTENS reflexively. He won't let go.

They fall TOGETHER.

Three stories.

Tumbling. Twisting. Sarah's body between Liam and the ground.

FLASHBACK — EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - FRONT DOOR - DAY (YEARS EARLIER)

The house is finished. Brand new.

Sarah and Hank stand at the front door. She's in a wedding dress. He's in a tux. Both beaming.

Hank gestures toward the threshold.

                    HANK
          Shall we?

Sarah smiles.

                    SARAH
          We shall.

He scoops her up. Carries her across the threshold. Into their castle.

END FLASHBACK.

They HIT.

CRACK.

The sound of bodies breaking.

INT. SECRET BASEMENT ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Emma and Olivia watch through the passage opening.

Their mother hits the ground. Liam on top of her.

Neither moves.

                    OLIVIA
          MOM! MOMMY!

Emma pulls her back. Tears streaming.

                    EMMA
          She told us to run. We RUN.

She drags Olivia toward the secret door. Into the regular basement.

                    EMMA
          Help me. HELP ME.

They grab the STEP LADDER. Use it to bridge the burned-out basement stairs.

They climb and run out into the night.

INT. SECRET BASEMENT ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Silence. Dust settling.

Sarah lies at the bottom of the shaft. Neck twisted at an unnatural angle. Eyes open. Seeing nothing.

Liam lies on top of her. Broken but breathing.

He groans. Rolls off her body. Every movement agony. Taking large gasps.

He looks at Sarah. Dead.

Then up the shaft. Three stories.

He should be dead too.

                    LIAM
          Shit.

He tries to stand. His leg buckles. Something's broken—maybe several somethings.

He crawls toward the secret room. Away from the passage.

His remaining armor scrapes against concrete. Metal grinding on stone.

He stops. Looks at himself.

The torso plates are cracked. Bent. Digging into his flesh. The leg plates are twisted, restricting movement. Everything
hurts.

Piece by piece, he strips off what's left.

Chest plate. Shoulder pads. Shin guards.

Drops them on the floor. Dead weight.

He's down to clothes now. Bloody. Burned. Broken.

But lighter.

He pulls himself up. Uses the wall for support.

One foot in front of the other.

He limps toward the exit.

## Production Notes
- Sarah's sacrifice: she uses the girls' distraction to take Liam down with her
- The scream: her last act is trying to save her daughters
- The fall: 3 stories—should be fatal for both, but Liam survives because Sarah cushions part of the impact
- Sarah's death: neck broken from combination of fall and Liam's grip
- Emma and Olivia's escape: step ladder bridges burned-out basement stairs
- Liam's armor removal: symbolic—he's stripped of everything, walks out as just a man
- The "very hurt" state: broken bones, internal damage, barely mobile
