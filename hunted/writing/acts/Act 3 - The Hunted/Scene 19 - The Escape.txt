# Scene 19 - The Escape

## LOCATION
INT. REEVES HOUSE - NIGHT

## CHARACTERS
Sarah, Liam, Emma, Olivia

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Sarah flees into the secret passage. Liam pursues. The girls wait in the secret basement room below.

### Key Beats
1. Sarah enters the passage, descending toward the secret room where Emma and Olivia wait.
2. Liam catches up to her in the vertical shaft—the ladder section that drops three stories.
3. Fight on the ladder. Sarah is exhausted, wounded. Liam is relentless.
4. Liam overpowers her. Gets his hands around her throat.
5. He drags her to the edge of the shaft opening, preparing to throw her down.
6. Sarah, with her last breath, SCREAMS down the shaft: "GIRLS! RUN!"
7. Below, in the SECRET BASEMENT ROOM, Emma and Olivia hear her.
8. They open the secret door to the main basement—and SCREAM.
9. Through the passage, they see UP the shaft: Liam holding their mother by the throat.
10. Liam is DISTRACTED by their screams. Looks down.
11. Sarah uses this moment. She grabs Liam and THROWS her weight backward into the shaft.
12. Both of them FALL. Three stories.
13. Liam's grip on Sarah's throat tightens reflexively as they plummet.
14. They hit the bottom. HARD.
15. Sarah is dead—neck broken from the fall and Liam's grip.
16. Liam is very hurt but alive. He lies in the wreckage, groaning.
17. Emma and Olivia, seeing their mother dead, grab a STEP LADDER from the secret room.
18. They use it to climb out of the basement through a window/exit. (Offscreen)
19. Liam slowly pulls himself up. Removes the last of his damaged armor.

### Character Moments
- **Sarah**: Sacrifices herself to give her daughters a chance to escape.
- **Emma/Olivia**: Their screams save their mother's dignity—she dies fighting, not executed.
- **Liam**: Survives but is broken. The fall nearly killed him.

### Closing
Sarah is dead. The girls escape. Liam removes his armor and walks out.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. SECRET PASSAGE - ATTIC LEVEL - NIGHT

Sarah scrambles into the passage. The narrow space barely fits her.

Behind her—Liam. Bleeding. Burned. But relentless.

                    LIAM
          No more running, Sarah.

She descends the ladder. Hand over hand. Moving as fast as her wounded body allows.

Below her—three stories of vertical shaft. Darkness.

Liam enters the passage. Follows her down.

INT. SECRET BASEMENT ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Emma and Olivia huddle in the darkness. Surrounded by Hank's prepper supplies—canned food, water jugs, emergency gear.

A STEP LADDER leans against the wall. An emergency exit—a small window near the ceiling.

They listen. Footsteps above. Descending.

                    OLIVIA
          Is that Mom?

Emma listens. Two sets of footsteps. One heavier than the other.

                    EMMA
          Stay quiet.

INT. SECRET PASSAGE - MIDDLE LEVEL - CONTINUOUS

Sarah climbs down. Arms burning. Cracked ribs screaming with every movement.

Liam is faster. Stronger. He gains on her.

His hand shoots out. Grabs her ankle.

She kicks. Tries to shake him off.

He PULLS. She loses her grip on the ladder.

For a terrible moment, she dangles by one hand.

Liam climbs over her. Pins her against the ladder.

                    LIAM
          Nowhere left to go.

His hands find her throat.

INT. SECRET PASSAGE - SHAFT OPENING - CONTINUOUS

They're at the top of the three-story drop. The shaft yawns below them—darkness all the way down to the secret basement
room.

Liam drags Sarah to the edge. His grip on her throat is iron.

                    LIAM
          I could throw you down. Let you fall. But that's too quick.

He squeezes. Sarah's face turns red.

                    LIAM
          I want you to know you lost. Your husband. Your son. Your—

Sarah gathers everything she has left. Her voice is a ragged scream, torn from damaged lungs:

                    SARAH
          GIRLS! RUN! RUN NOW!

Her voice echoes down the shaft. Three stories.

INT. SECRET BASEMENT ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Emma and Olivia hear it. Their mother's voice. Desperate. Dying.

                    OLIVIA
          MOM!

Emma grabs her sister's hand. Pulls her toward the secret door.

                    EMMA
          We have to—

She pushes the door open.

The secret passage is visible. And through it—UP the shaft—

They can SEE.

Liam. Holding their mother by the throat. Silhouetted against the faint light from above.

Emma and Olivia SCREAM.

INT. SECRET PASSAGE - SHAFT OPENING - CONTINUOUS

Liam hears the screams. Looks down.

For just a moment, his grip loosens. His attention shifts.

Sarah's eyes meet her daughters' eyes. Three stories below. One last look.

Then she MOVES.

She grabs Liam's armor. His shirt. Anything she can reach.

And throws herself backward. Into the shaft.

                    LIAM
          WHAT—

His grip on her throat TIGHTENS reflexively. He won't let go.

They fall TOGETHER.

Three stories.

Tumbling. Twisting. Sarah's body between Liam and the ground.

They HIT.

CRACK.

The sound of bodies breaking.

INT. SECRET BASEMENT ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Emma and Olivia watch through the passage opening.

Their mother hits the ground. Liam on top of her.

Neither moves.

                    OLIVIA
          MOM! MOMMY!

Emma pulls her back. Tears streaming.

                    EMMA
          She's gone. Liv, she's gone. We have to go.

                    OLIVIA
          NO—

                    EMMA
          She told us to run. We RUN.

She drags Olivia toward the step ladder. The emergency exit.

                    EMMA
          Help me. HELP ME.

Olivia, sobbing, helps position the ladder under the window.

They climb. One after the other.

And disappear into the night.

INT. SECRET PASSAGE - BASEMENT LEVEL - CONTINUOUS

Silence. Dust settling.

Sarah lies at the bottom of the shaft. Neck twisted at an unnatural angle. Eyes open. Seeing nothing.

Liam lies on top of her. Broken.

But breathing.

He groans. Rolls off her body. Every movement agony.

He looks at Sarah. Dead.

Then up the shaft. Three stories.

He should be dead too.

                    LIAM
          Shit.

He tries to stand. His leg buckles. Something's broken—maybe several somethings.

He crawls toward the secret room. Away from the passage.

His remaining armor scrapes against concrete. Metal grinding on stone.

He stops. Looks at himself.

The torso plates are cracked. Bent. Digging into his flesh. The leg plates are twisted, restricting movement. Everything
hurts.

Piece by piece, he strips off what's left.

Chest plate. Shoulder pads. Shin guards.

Drops them on the floor. Dead weight.

He's down to clothes now. Bloody. Burned. Broken.

But lighter.

He pulls himself up. Uses the wall for support.

One foot in front of the other.

He limps toward the exit.

## Production Notes
- Sarah's sacrifice: she uses the girls' distraction to take Liam down with her
- The scream: her last act is trying to save her daughters
- The fall: 3 stories—should be fatal for both, but Liam survives because Sarah cushions part of the impact
- Sarah's death: neck broken from combination of fall and Liam's grip
- Emma and Olivia's escape: step ladder from prepper supplies, out through basement window (offscreen)
- Liam's armor removal: symbolic—he's stripped of everything, walks out as just a man
- The "very hurt" state: broken bones, internal damage, barely mobile
