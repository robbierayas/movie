# Scene 20 - The Crawling Struggle

## LOCATION
EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - NIGHT

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Police, Emma, Olivia

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Liam emerges from the burning house. Sarah is dead. The girls have escaped.

### Key Beats
1. Emma and Olivia are at the second floor gatehouse doors. Emma has Eli's gun. They've already called the cops.
2. Liam emerges from the basement onto the first floor. Looks up. Sees them.
3. Emma raises the gun. Aims at Liam.
4. Olivia pushes the gun down. Closes the gatehouse door on Liam.
5. Liam stares at the closed door. The girls who chose not to attack.
6. He limps out the front door. Into the night.
7. SIRENS. Police cars tear up the driveway.
8. Liam stops. Pulls out HANK'S BLACKBELT. Ties it around his head.
9. Suicide-by-cop. "BANG BANG."
10. The officers FIRE. Liam falls.
11. Emma and Olivia watch from the second floor window. Not with the police. Together. Alone.

### Character Moments
- **Liam**: Sees the girls refuse to attack—the only people who beat him tonight did it by not fighting. Dies confused.
- **Emma**: Has the weapon, has the shot—but Olivia's choice overrides.
- **Olivia**: Pushes the gun down. Closes the door. The theme in one gesture.
- **The irony**: Everyone who attacked Liam died. The ones who refused to fight survived.

### Closing
The killer is dead. The children are orphans. But alive. They didn't fight. They survived.

## FULL SCRIPT

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR - NIGHT

Emma and Olivia stand at the gatehouse doors. The reinforced metal doors Eli threw open. The doors that cost him his
life.

Emma holds ELI'S GUN. Her hands are shaking. Olivia stands beside her, one hand on the door.

A phone lies on the floor behind them—911 still connected. A DISPATCHER's tinny voice:

                    DISPATCHER (V.O.)
          Ma'am? Are you still there? Units are en route—

Below them, the first floor burns. Smoke seeps up through the stairwell.

Then—movement.

Liam pulls himself up from the basement. Emerges onto the first floor through the smoke. No armor. Just clothes. Blood.
Burns. Broken bones.

He looks up the stairwell.

And sees them.

Two girls. Standing at the top of the stairs. The older one holding a gun.

He stops.

Emma raises the gun. Aims at Liam's face. Her hands steady now. Finger on the trigger.

Liam stares at her. Waiting. Almost curious.

A long beat.

Olivia reaches over. Gently pushes the barrel down. Away from Liam.

                    OLIVIA
          Karate ni sente nashi.

Emma looks at her sister. Tears on both their faces.

Olivia pulls the gatehouse door closed. The heavy metal door SHUTS between them and Liam.

The lock engages. CLICK.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - FIRST FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Liam stands alone at the bottom of the stairs. Staring at the closed door.

Something crosses his face. Not anger. Not triumph.

Confusion.

They had the shot. They didn't take it.

He looks at the closed door for a long moment. The only people who beat him tonight.

He turns. Limps toward the front door.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - NIGHT

Liam pulls himself through the front door. Out into the night air.

The house burns behind him. Flames licking up toward the roof. Smoke blotting out the stars.

He stands there for a moment. Swaying.

                    LIAM
          Not bad. Small chance I could have lost there.

He starts walking. Each step agony.

Then—

SIRENS.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - DRIVEWAY - CONTINUOUS

Police CARS tear up the driveway. Lights flashing. Red and blue cutting through the smoke.

One. Two. Three cars.

They screech to a halt. Officers pour out. Weapons drawn.

                    OFFICER
          FREEZE! HANDS WHERE I CAN SEE THEM!

Liam stops.

He looks at the police cars. At the burning house. At the long distance between him and freedom.

No way out.

He reaches into his jacket—slowly, deliberately.

The officers tense. Fingers on triggers.

                    OFFICER
          SHOW ME YOUR HANDS!

Liam pulls out HANK'S BLACKBELT.

The worn fabric. The faded lettering. His trophy.

He ties it around his head. A crude bandana. A crown.

                    LIAM
          One more challenge.

Liam removes his hands from his jacket quickly. He shapes his hands into GUNS. Fingers extended. Thumbs cocked.

Aims at the officers.

                    LIAM
          BANG BANG.

The officers FIRE.

Liam's body jerks. Once. Twice. Three times.

He crumples to the grass.

Falls face-down.

The blackbelt—Hank's blackbelt—still tied around his head.

Silence.

Then the sound of radios crackling. Officers advancing. Checking the body.

                    OFFICER
          Suspect down! We need medical—

But it's too late.

INT. REEVES HOUSE - SECOND FLOOR - CONTINUOUS

Emma and Olivia sit against the gatehouse doors. The fire below. The sirens outside.

Emma still holds the gun. She looks at it. Sets it on the floor. Pushes it away.

Olivia's hand finds hers. Squeezes.

They sit there. Together. Alone.

Through the window, red and blue lights flash across their faces. FIREFIGHTERS rush toward the burning house. PARAMEDICS
run toward the bodies.

But no one has come upstairs yet.

For now, it's just them.

Emma and Olivia Reeves.

They didn't fight.

They survived.

FADE OUT.

## Production Notes
- The girls went BACK UPSTAIRS instead of running outside—to close the gatehouse doors and call 911
- Emma finds Eli's gun where it was knocked from his hand in Scene 18
- THE KEY MOMENT: Emma aims at Liam. Olivia pushes the gun down. Closes the door. This IS the theme.
- Olivia echoes Emma's line from Scene 15: "Karate ni sente nashi" — Hank's lesson, passed between sisters
- Liam's CONFUSION at the closed door: the only people who beat him did it by refusing to fight
- Liam walks out, not crawls—he's broken but mobile
- The blackbelt: Hank's trophy becomes Liam's death shroud
- Suicide-by-cop: Liam chooses his ending, refuses to be captured—one last attack, one last death
- The girls are NOT with the police at the end. They sit alone behind the doors. Together.
- Emma sets the gun down. Chooses not to be a fighter.
- "They didn't fight. They survived." — the thesis statement
