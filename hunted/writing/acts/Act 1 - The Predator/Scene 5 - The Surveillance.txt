# Scene 5 - The Surveillance (MONTAGE)

## TYPE
Linear Montage

## PURPOSE
Compress two days of surveillance into a single sequence. Show Liam learning everything about the family while
maintaining
his invisible existence.

## TIME SPAN
Two days

## MONTAGE STRUCTURE

### Shot 1
Liam parked outside the property at night, watching the family cookout through binoculars. "The knife man has a name.
Hank Reeves."

### Shot 2
Liam going through their mail dressed as Amazon delivery—voiceover introduces the family: wife Sarah, three kids. Liam
will have to get Hank alone.

### Shot 3
Through windows, Liam observes taxidermied trophies on the walls. Voiceover notes: "So he does hunt. The liar." Said he
was just "practical" — he's been hunting his whole life.

### Shot 4
Liam watches routines—Sarah's car, kids with backpacks, Girly bounding to greet them. Eli loads archery gear. Sarah herds
them to practice. Notes the Rottweiler: "Eighty pounds, maybe more." Hank comes home alone from work.

### Shot 5
Liam sketching the property layout, noting entry points, blind spots. Sees three floors from outside (basement, main,
upstairs)—misses hidden attic entrance and secret passage. Plans to catch Hank outside: "He built this place like a
fortress."

### Shot 6
Liam at his apartment that night, planning. Photos, notes, a crude schedule. "Now I know everything I need... Time to
hunt."

## FULL SCRIPT

MONTAGE - SURVEILLANCE (TWO DAYS)

EXT. RURAL ROAD - NIGHT

Liam's sedan parked in the shadows, fifty yards from the Reeves driveway. He watches through binoculars. The family
cookout
 in the distance.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          The knife man has a name. Hank Reeves.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - DAY

Liam walks up the driveway in an AMAZON VEST, package in hand. He lingers at the mailbox, rifling through envelopes.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          Wife—Sarah. Three kids. Two girls, one boy. All of them trained too.

He photographs the mail with his phone. Slides it back. Walks away.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          If I want Hank, I'll have to get him alone.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - DUSK

Liam crouches in the treeline with binoculars. Through the windows: TAXIDERMIED DEER HEADS on the walls. HUNTING PHOTOS.
COMPETITION RIBBONS.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          So he does hunt. The liar.

A beat.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          Said he was just "practical." He's been hunting his whole life.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - VARIOUS TIMES

Quick cuts: SARAH's car pulls in, kids piling out with backpacks. A ROTTWEILER bounds to greet them—GIRLY. EMMA and
Olivia chase each other in the yard, the dog following. ELI loads archery gear into the truck. Sarah herds them back into
the car for practice.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          Kids have activities. Wife drives them. House sits empty for hours.

He watches through binoculars as Girly patrols the yard.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          They've got a Rottweiler. Eighty pounds, maybe more. Noted.

Hank's truck pulls up the driveway. Alone. Work clothes dusty.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          But Hank comes home from work. Alone.

EXT. REEVES PROPERTY - DAY

Liam walks the perimeter at a distance, sketching in a small notebook. He notes and draws details.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          Three floors—basement, main, upstairs. Long sightlines from the second story. No cover approaching the house.

He pauses. Studies the roofline.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          He built this place like a fortress. Smart. But I'll come from outside. Catch him before he gets in.

INT. LIAM'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

Liam sits at his small table. PHOTOS spread out—the house, the family, Hank's truck. A hand-drawn SCHEDULE. A crude MAP
of the property.

He circles something on the schedule. Leans back.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          Now I know everything I need.

He looks at Hank's photo.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          Time to hunt.

---

## Production Notes
- Voiceover continues throughout montage
- Show meticulous planning but NOT hacking—physical surveillance only
- Contrast Liam's cramped apartment with the Reeves' large property
- End montage with sense of completion: he knows everything he needs
