# Scene 2 - Bar Revenge

## LOCATION
EXT. BAR / STREET ALLEY - NIGHT

## CHARACTERS
Liam, Glasses Guy

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Voiceover continues. Liam reflects on what happened next—hunting down a look-alike that same night.

### Key Beats
1. Liam walking down the street, following someone who looks similar to Regular Customer in Scene 1 walking with another
group, but obviously different person because of glasses. He is not trying to hide his presence. V.O.: "So we play a
little game... I'll teach this chump instead."
2. Voiceover: "I have to make a stand. Put people in their place. But I'm not going to boss anyone around. That would
make me just as unskilled as them."
3. Group splits up. Glasses Guy walks alone down quieter street. Liam uses doorways and darkness. Glasses Guy senses
something, checks over his shoulder, sees nothing. Liam emerges ahead of him.
4. Glasses Guy tries to talk his way out, swings messenger bag. Liam punches him down, drags him into alley. Victim
offers wallet. V.O.: "They always say that. Like skill is something you can buy."
5. Liam gives him a chance: "I want to see what you've got." Victim sprays mace—catches them both. Glasses Guy lands
blows while Liam is blinded, but Liam catches his wrist, breaks it, strangles him.
6. Liam stores the cracked glasses from his second kill as a trophy
7. We glimpse a hair bow—trophy from his first kill (unseen, unexplained)
8. Liam dumps the messenger bag—"The Most Dangerous Game" by Richard Connell falls out. Reads rubbing his aching eyes.
9. V.O.: "I'd been wasting my time. These people—they don't have skill. They just have luck. Bad luck." The idea takes
hold: he wants a real challenge. "The only prey worth hunting... is prey that can hunt you back."

### Character Moments
- **Liam**: The kill is displaced rage—wrong target, same satisfaction. Gives victim a "chance" to prove skill, framing
murder as a test. The trophies reveal this isn't his first time. Apartment reveals tinkerer side (electronics,
mechanical constructions).

### Closing
Voiceover establishes the new goal: worthy prey. The hunt for a real challenge begins.

## FULL SCRIPT

EXT. DOWNTOWN STREET - NIGHT

City street lights line the street creating dark spots. Liam walks with purpose, hands in his jacket pockets. Twenty feet ahead, a group of
FOUR PEOPLE laugh and stumble together. Among them—a GLASSES GUY, who bears a passing resemblance to the Regular
Customer, carrying a messenger bag.

Liam doesn't hide. He walks like any other pedestrian.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          So we play a little game. Obviously, I would like to test that regular asshat from the bar, but this will have
          to do. The most important part of my game is not getting caught. So I'll teach this chump instead.

The Glasses Guy says something that makes the group laugh. Liam winces at the laughter.

The group pauses at a crosswalk. Liam stops too, maintaining distance. The Glasses Guy glances back—just a casual
look, nothing suspicious.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          He thinks he already won. I have to show them they won't win. Put people in their place. But I'm not going to boss anyone around, wasting my life training them. That would make me just as blind and unskilled as them.

The light changes. The group crosses.

EXT. SIDE STREET - CONTINUOUS

The group reaches a corner. Hugs. Goodbyes. The Glasses Guy waves them off and turns down a quieter side street,
alone now, hands in his coat pockets.

Liam rounds the corner. The streetlights here are spaced further apart. Pools of darkness between them.

The Glasses Guy walks faster. Not running.

He glances over his shoulder. The street behind him is empty.

He relaxes slightly. Keeps walking, pulling out his phone.

Liam emerges from a doorway alcove several feet ahead. The Glasses Guy doesn't see him yet—he's looking at his phone.

The Glasses Guy looks up from his phone. Sees Liam walking toward him. Their eyes meet.

Something in Liam's expression makes the man's step falter.

Glasses Guy laughs nervously.

                    GLASSES GUY
          Hey, can I help you with something?

Liam doesn't answer. Keeps walking.

                    GLASSES GUY
          Look, I don't want any trouble—

He backs toward an alley entrance. Glances into the darkness—empty. When he looks back, Liam is closer.

                    GLASSES GUY
          What? What do you want?

                    LIAM
          I want to see what you've got.

Glasses Guy swings his messenger bag hard, but misses Liam. Liam's fist connects with the man's jaw. The Glasses Guy
goes down hard, bag and glasses skittering across the pavement.

EXT. ALLEY - CONTINUOUS

Liam drags him into the shadows. The Glasses Guy scrambles backward to the street, unable to fully stand.

                    GLASSES GUY
          Take it! Take whatever you want!

He fumbles out his wallet, throws it at Liam's feet.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          They always say that. Like skill is something you can buy.

Liam kicks the wallet aside. He walks closer.

                    GLASSES GUY
          Please—

The Glasses Guy lunges—not at Liam, but for his bag on the ground. He pulls out a small canister of mace.

He sprays wildly. The cloud catches them both. Liam staggers back, eyes burning. The Glasses Guy starts to stand up. Liam
grabs at him blindly, missing. The Glasses Guy punches his exposed side.

Liam takes the blows. Tears streaming, eyes swelling shut. But he doesn't retreat.

                    GLASSES GUY
          Fucking loser.

The Glasses Guy winds up for another punch—

Liam catches his wrist. Twists. CRACK.

The Glasses Guy screams. Liam's other hand finds his throat.

The struggle is brief. The Glasses Guy goes still.

Liam kneels there, breathing hard, eyes burning and swollen. He blinks through tears at the body beneath him.

He picks up the man's glasses. Holds them up to the distant streetlight. One lens is cracked. He pockets the glasses.
Grabs the messenger bag and rifles through it—takes nothing useful, just slings it over his shoulder and walks further
into the alley darkness.

INT. LIAM'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

A small studio. Neat. Impersonal. The only decoration: a wooden box on a shelf. Several tables of scattered experiments
of electronics and mechanical constructions. On the end of one table near a chair, there is a stack of laptops with a satellite modem on top.

Liam enters, face swollen, eyes red and watering.

He goes to the shelf. Opens the wooden box with care.

Inside: he places the cracked glasses gently beside—

A HAIR BOW. Pink. Faded. Small enough to belong to a child.

Liam stares at it for a moment. Then closes the box.

He moves to his small table. He dumps the messenger bag on the table. A dog-eared paperback pops out: "THE MOST DANGEROUS
GAME" by Richard Connell.

Liam sits. Rubs his aching eyes. Opens the book to a page near the front.

He reads silently. We see his swollen eyes moving across the text.

He sets the book down. Stares at nothing.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          I finished the book. And I realized I'd been wasting my time. These people—they don't have skill. They just
          have luck.
          Bad luck.

He smiles to himself, glances at the wooden box on the shelf.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          I need more, a real challenge. Someone with actual skill. Someone dangerous. Someone who can fight back. Zaroff
          understood. The only prey worth hunting... is prey that can hunt you back.

Liam closes the book. A small smile forms on his swollen, bruised face.

He looks out his small window at the city lights.

                    LIAM (V.O.)
          Time to find a real challenge.
