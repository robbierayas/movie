# JOE

## BASIC INFORMATION
- **Full Name**: Joe (last name unknown)
- **Age**: 40s
- **Class Status**: Unhoused
- **Occupation**: Informal community organizer / neighborhood representative at Tracktown
- **Family**: Not mentioned
- **Background**: Long-time Tracktown resident. Knows everyone and everything happening in the community. Self-appointed social glue.

## ARCHETYPE
**Primary**: The Heart of the Community / The Connector
**Secondary traits**: The Busybody (nosy but from genuine care), The Witness (sees Adam's transformation)

## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Represents the warmth and human connection of Tracktown. Functions as the community's voice — welcoming, observant, and genuinely invested in everyone's well-being. His death in the massacre carries weight because he IS the community. He's the person who made Tracktown feel like home, not just survival.

## PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION
- 40s, weathered but warm face
- Always moving, always talking to someone
- Friendly body language — open posture, easy smile
- The kind of person who pats shoulders, makes eye contact, remembers your name

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
- Knows everyone in Tracktown and their business
- Genuinely cares about the community — nosiness comes from caring, not gossip
- Optimistic about people despite circumstances
- Notices changes in people before they notice themselves
- Talks easily and naturally — silence makes him uncomfortable
- Trusting — takes people at their word, doesn't push when they deflect

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Adam**: Watches Adam transform from isolated loner to community member. Proud of the change. Genuinely happy when Adam contributes. Doesn't know about Adam's disposal work. Unknowingly helps carry a body in Scene 15. Dies in the Tracktown gassing.
- **Tracktown community**: The connective tissue. Coordinates barbecues, checks on people, introduces newcomers. Marie, the residents — he's the one who makes it all feel like a neighborhood.
- **Tyson**: Knows him as a Tracktown kid. Protective in a community-elder way.
- **Gal**: Welcomes her when Adam brings her to the barbecue.

## CHARACTER ARC
**Act 1**: Background presence → Notices Adam's change → Welcomes Adam into community circle → Represents what Adam is building

**Act 2**: Unknowingly helps Adam carry a body → Continues being the community heart → Suspects something about Gal's disappearance but doesn't press

**Act 3**: Learns the truth → Tries to protect community during gassing → Dies with everyone else

Arc Type: The Innocent Destroyed — never knew the danger Adam brought to his doorstep. His death is the cost of Adam's choices.

## DIALOGUE STYLE
- **Warm and inviting**: Opens every interaction like you're already welcome ("Adam! You made it back! And brought friends!"). Never interrogates — draws people in through enthusiasm. Makes everyone feel noticed.
- **Observational compliments**: Notices and calls out positive changes in people ("You know, we look out for each other here" / "People notice"). Encouragement delivered casually, never preachy.
- **Name-drops and callbacks**: References other community members constantly ("Didn't I say that, Marie?" / "Marie was saying just yesterday..."). Weaves people together through conversation. Makes the community feel connected.
- **Genuine curiosity without pressure**: Asks questions because he cares, not to pry ("What kind of work?" / "Haven't seen you around much"). Accepts deflections without pushing. If you don't want to share, that's fine — he'll still be friendly.
- **Community philosopher**: Speaks about belonging and mutual support naturally ("We look out for each other here. That's what makes this place work"). Not sentimental — matter-of-fact about the importance of community. Earned wisdom, not platitudes.
- **Easy humor**: Gentle teasing that shows affection ("I always said you looked like a scientist type"). Never mean-spirited. Laughs easily at others' jokes and his own.

## KEY SCENES

Claims credit for spotting Adam's potential: "I always said you looked like a scientist type." Marie corrects him: "You said he didn't look dumb enough to scam." That's what makes this place work." Observes Adam's progress warmly: "You're doing good. People notice."

**Act 1, Scene 11: Meeting Gal** - Welcomes Adam back with friends: "Adam! You made it back! And brought friends!" Asks the trio: "You three look good together. Friends?" Represents the community warmth that names what Adam is feeling before he can say it himself.
