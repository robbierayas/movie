----------------------------------------
# SCENE 0 - THE RAILYARD
----------------------------------------

EXT. ABANDONED RAILYARD - HOMELESS ENCAMPMENT - DAY

Wide shot: A sprawling abandoned railyard transformed into a makeshift city. Dozens of illegal structures - tents, tarps,
jury-rigged shacks - fill every available space between the train tracks. People move between structures, cooking fires
smoke, children run between the gaps.

The tracks themselves remain conspicuously empty and clear.

In the far distance, gleaming towers of the Amazing Company campus rise above the urban skyline. The contrast is stark -
polished corporate glass against rusted metal and canvas.

Push in through the encampment. HOMELESS RESIDENTS go about their routines - cooking, trading goods, repairing shelters.
Conversation and activity everywhere.

Near the center of the settlement, a low concrete structure half-buried in dirt. A maintenance hatch, almost hidden
behind stacked pallets and trash.

The hatch shifts slightly. Opens a crack.

ADAM (30s, weathered but sharp-eyed) peers out, checking if anyone's watching. His face shows intelligence worn down by
circumstance. He wears layers - practical, mismatched, forgettable.

Coast clear. Adam slips out, closing the hatch behind him. He moves with purpose but without drawing attention.

INT. UNDERGROUND TUNNEL - CONTINUOUS

Adam descends a rusted ladder into darkness. His movements are confident - he knows this place.

The tunnel is part of the old railyard infrastructure. Concrete walls marked with decades of water damage and graffiti.
Dim emergency lighting flickers, barely functional.

Adam walks through intersecting passages with certainty. No hesitation at forks or turns. This is his domain.

As he passes a recessed alcove, light glints off glass. A chemistry setup - beakers, tubing, heat sources - partially
hidden behind tarps and debris. Industrial-grade equipment, scavenged but meticulously arranged.

Adam doesn't stop. Just keeps walking.

The tunnel opens into a larger chamber. More evidence of habitation - a sleeping bag, stacked supplies, books and
notebooks. A home, hidden from the world above.

Adam grabs a small backpack from the floor, checks its contents. Satisfied, he slings it over his shoulder.

Above, a deep RUMBLING sound. The walls vibrate.

Adam freezes. Looks up.

The vibration intensifies. Dust falls from the ceiling. Something heavy is moving above - the entire structure of the
settlement shifting, settling.

A crack appears in the concrete above Adam's head. Small at first, then spreading.

Adam, under his breath.

                    ADAM
          Shit.

He moves quickly now, abandoning stealth for speed. Back through the tunnels, toward another exit point.

The rumbling continues, then stops as suddenly as it started.

Adam pauses at a different ladder, listening. Silence above. He climbs.

EXT. ABANDONED RAILYARD - LATE AFTERNOON - CONTINUOUS

Adam emerges from a different hatch, this one on the outskirts of the encampment. The sun is lower now, casting long
shadows.

He stays low, moving between structures without entering the main pathways. Avoiding eye contact, avoiding conversation.

A WOMAN calls out to someone nearby. Adam immediately shifts direction, putting a tent between himself and the voice.

He reaches the edge of the settlement where it borders the actual rail line. Stands there for a moment, facing the sun.

His expression softens slightly. This is the only moment of peace in his day.

Then voices approach from behind - residents passing by. Adam's face closes off. He turns and walks away, deeper into the
maze of structures.

EXT. TRANSITION AREA - DUSK TO NIGHT

A montage as darkness descends:

Adam walks from the railyard encampment toward the main city. The landscape changes gradually - industrial wasteland
giving way to developed areas.

He passes through a market built inside old train cars. Converted freight cars now house food vendors, clothing stalls,
electronics repair. People crowd the aisles.

Adam walks through alone, hands in pockets. His eyes track the food - fresh bread, cooked meat, vegetables. His stomach
may be empty but his face shows no emotion. He has no money. He doesn't stop.

The market vendors call out prices, haggle with customers. Adam is invisible to them.

EXT. CITY CHECKPOINT - NIGHT

A police checkpoint marks the boundary between the outer zone and the main city. Two OFFICERS stand beside a barrier,
checking IDs, waving people through or turning them back.

AUTO-CARS glide past on the city side of the checkpoint - sleek, silent, expensive. The contrast with the foot traffic on
this side is obvious.

Adam approaches the checkpoint. One officer barely glances at him before waving him through. He's unremarkable enough not
to register as a threat.

EXT. MAIN CITY STREETS - NIGHT

Adam walks on the sidewalk. AUTO-CARS fill the streets - no human drivers, no variation in speed or spacing. Perfect
efficiency.

The buildings here are taller, cleaner. Digital advertisements glow from screens. Everything is lit, controlled,
monitored.

Adam walks alone on the sidewalk. Occasionally another pedestrian passes, but most people are in the cars.

He is isolated even here, in the heart of the city.

Wide shot: Adam, a single figure on foot, surrounded by the endless flow of autonomous vehicles. Small and alone against
the mechanical city.

FADE TO BLACK.


----------------------------------------
# SCENE 1 - THE WITNESS
----------------------------------------

EXT. CITY STREET - WEALTHY DISTRICT - NIGHT

Gleaming storefronts. Auto-cars gliding silently past. Everything pristine, expensive, controlled.

Adam walks the sidewalk, clearly out of place. His worn clothes stand in contrast to the polished surroundings. He stays
close to the buildings, trying to blend with shadows.

He pauses at a trash receptacle - one of the smart bins that separate recyclables automatically. Glances around, then
quickly checks inside. Empty. The wealthy district is too clean.

Adam moves on, hands in pockets, eyes scanning for anything useful. He's scavenging, but there's nothing to find here.

From around the corner ahead, voices. One high-pitched, strangely gleeful. The other lower, pleading.

Adam stops. Should walk away. Instead he edges toward the sound.

EXT. ALLEY ENTRANCE - CONTINUOUS

Adam reaches the corner. Peers around the wall into a dark alley.

Three figures visible in the dim light:

GEOFF (40s, expensive suit, slight build) stands watching with hands clasped. His face shows anticipation, like a child
about to open a present.

MOOSK - humanoid robot, imposing build, movements unnaturally precise - holds a VICTIM (30s, business attire) by the arm.
The victim is trembling, tears streaming.

On the ground: a pool innertube, brightly colored. Incongruous next to a helium tank.

Geoff, cheerful.

                    GEOFF
          Alright! Let's get you set up. Moosk, if you would?

Moosk moves with mechanical efficiency, forcing the victim to the ground. The victim struggles but it's useless against
robotic strength.

                    VICTIM
          Please - please I have a family - I'll give you anything -

Geoff, dismissive wave.

                    GEOFF
          Oh stop. You think I need your money?
          (giggles)
          No no. This is just... fun.

Adam watches from his hiding spot, frozen. He should run. He doesn't.

Moosk forces the victim's arms and torso through the innertube. The victim fights but Moosk's grip is absolute. The tube
settles around the victim's waist like a flotation device.

                    VICTIM
          What are you doing - what is this -

                    GEOFF
          An experiment! Well, not really. I've done this before. But it's still fun every time.
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.

That laugh - high-pitched, affected, unsettling. Like someone pretending they find something funny but getting the sound
wrong.

Moosk connects a hose from the helium tank to the innertube's valve. The tank makes a sharp HISS as helium begins
flowing.

The innertube inflates rapidly. The victim's eyes widen in horror as the tube expands around their waist.

                    VICTIM
          No - no please - stop -

Geoff, leaning in, fascinated.

                    GEOFF
          Here we go!

The innertube is fully inflated now, enormous. The victim struggles against Moosk's grip but the robot holds them in
place.

Then the physics takes over.

The helium's buoyancy overcomes the victim's weight. Their feet begin to lift off the ground.

Victim, panicking.

                    VICTIM
          HELP! SOMEBODY HELP ME!

The victim flips upside down as the innertube rises, their head now pointing toward the ground, legs kicking uselessly
above. They begin to ascend, slowly at first.

Geoff, clapping.

                    GEOFF
          Beautiful! Just beautiful!

Geoff walks beneath the rising victim, looking up. His face shows pure delight.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          You know what's funny? I used to feel bad about this. Used to have nightmares, guilt, all that messy stuff.
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.
          (shrugs)
          But then I realized - why feel bad? I can do whatever I want. So I might as well enjoy it!

The victim is ten feet up now, still ascending. Their screams echo in the alley.

                    VICTIM
          PLEASE! SOMEBODY! ANYBODY!

Adam presses against the wall, his breathing shallow. His face shows shock, horror, disbelief at what he's witnessing.

Geoff pulls out a small device - a remote control. Points it upward.

                    GEOFF
          Okay, this part's my favorite.
          (presses button)
          Goodbye!

CLICK.

A mechanism on the innertube triggers. The valve opens.

The helium escapes with a loud HISSSSSSS.

The victim stops rising. Hangs in the air for one suspended moment.

Then falls.

The victim screams. Flails. Twenty feet of empty air.

IMPACT.

The body hits the pavement with a sickening CRUNCH. Blood spreads across the concrete.

Geoff tilts his head, examining the result like an artist checking their work.

Geoff (cont'd), satisfied.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          There we go.
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.

Moosk stands motionless, staring at the body with no reaction.

Adam can't breathe. Can't move. Can't process what he just witnessed.

His hand shakes. Bumps against the wall.

SCRAPE.

Metal trash lid rattling.

Geoff's head snaps toward the sound.

Geoff (cont'd), curious.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Hmm?

Moosk's head turns with mechanical precision toward Adam's hiding spot. Red lights activate in the robot's eyes -
scanning.

Moosk, flat robotic voice.

                    MOOSK
          Motion detected. Human signature.

Adam's eyes go wide. Terror floods his face.

Geoff, delighted.

                    GEOFF
          Oh! We have an audience!
          (calling out)
          Hello there! Don't be shy!

Adam steps back from the corner, about to run.

Moosk moves. Impossibly fast for something that size. In three mechanical strides the robot rounds the corner and grabs
Adam's arm.

Adam tries to pull away but it's like fighting a steel clamp.

Adam, panicking.

                    ADAM
          I didn't see anything - I was just walking - I don't -

Moosk drags Adam back into the alley, presenting him to Geoff.

Geoff, amused, circling Adam.

                    GEOFF
          Look at you. Unhoused, I'd guess? Wrong district for scavenging, friend. The rich don't throw out anything
          good.
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.

Adam, stammering.

                    ADAM
          Please - I won't say anything - I didn't see -

Geoff laughs.

                    GEOFF
          Oh you definitely saw. You watched the whole thing!
          (leans closer)
          What did you think? Honest opinion. Five stars? Three stars?

Adam stares at him, speechless. This man just murdered someone and he's asking for a review.

Geoff (cont'd), sighs.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          You're no fun. Most people at least scream.

Geoff looks down at the body, then back at Adam. His expression shifts - calculating.

Geoff (cont'd), thoughtful.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Actually... you might be useful. Do you know chemistry?

Adam freezes. That was too specific a question.

Geoff (cont'd), noticing the reaction.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Ooh, you do! Perfect!
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh. This is serendipity!

Adam, desperate.

                    ADAM
          What do you want?

Geoff gestures to the body.

                    GEOFF
          I need this cleaned up. Professionally. And I think you're exactly the person to do it.

                    ADAM
          I'm not - I can't -

Geoff, interrupting, suddenly serious.

                    GEOFF
          You witnessed a murder. Moosk has you on camera.
          (cheerful again)
          So! You can help me dispose of the body, or you can join it. Your choice!
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.

Moosk's grip tightens on Adam's arm. A mechanical warning.

Adam looks at the body. At Geoff's smiling face. At Moosk's blank robotic stare.

He's trapped.

FADE TO BLACK.


----------------------------------------
# SCENE 2 - THE OFFER
----------------------------------------

EXT. CITY STREET - ALLEY - NIGHT (CONTINUOUS)

Adam stands frozen in Moosk's grip, staring at Geoff. The body lies motionless on the pavement between them. Blood pools
on concrete.

Geoff examines Adam with detached curiosity, like a scientist observing an insect.

Geoff, sighing.

                    GEOFF
          Well. This is unfortunate.
          (to Moosk)
          Can't have witnesses wandering around. You know the drill.

Moosk's grip shifts on Adam's arm, moving toward his throat.

Adam, panicking.

                    ADAM
          Wait - WAIT!

Geoff, dismissive.

                    GEOFF
          Nothing personal. Just tidying up.

Adam struggles against Moosk's hold but the robot's strength is absolute. Cold metal fingers close around his neck.

Adam, desperate, choking.

                    ADAM
          I can - I can help!

Geoff pauses. Holds up a hand. Moosk stops immediately, fingers still wrapped around Adam's throat but not squeezing.

Geoff, amused.

                    GEOFF
          Help? You?

Adam, gasping.

                    ADAM
          The body - I can get rid of it -

Geoff tilts his head, suddenly interested.

                    GEOFF
          Really.
          (circling Adam)
          And why exactly would you do that?

Adam, desperate.

                    ADAM
          Because you'll kill me if I don't!

Geoff laughs.

                    GEOFF
          Fair point!
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.
          (serious again)
          But why should I trust you? What's stopping you from running straight to the police the moment Moosk lets go?

Adam's eyes dart between Geoff, Moosk, and the alley exit. His mind races.

                    ADAM
          I - I have chemistry training. Equipment. I can dissolve it. Completely. No trace.

That gets Geoff's attention. He stops pacing.

                    GEOFF
          Chemistry training. How... specific.
          (leaning closer)
          What kind of training?

Adam, stammering.

                    ADAM
          University. Research. I - it doesn't matter. I know how to break down organic material. Make it disappear.

                    GEOFF
          And where would you do this miraculous disappearing act?

                    ADAM
          The railyard settlement. Underground. I have access to spaces nobody else knows about.

Geoff considers this. His expression shifts from amused to calculating.

Geoff, thoughtful.

                    GEOFF
          Hidden spaces. Chemistry equipment. Knowledge nobody's looking for in a homeless chemist.
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.
          (to Adam)
          You might actually be useful.

He nods at Moosk. The robot releases Adam's throat but maintains a grip on his arm.

Adam gasps for air, rubbing his neck.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Alright. I'll take you up on your generous offer. You dispose of this-
          (gestures to body)
          -and if you do a good job, maybe we work together again.

Adam, quickly.

                    ADAM
          Just disposal. I won't help you kill anyone.

Silence.

Then Geoff bursts out laughing. Genuine amusement this time.

                    GEOFF
          Oh that's RICH! You have MORALS about this!
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh!
          (wiping eyes)
          You'll help me hide a murder, help me avoid consequences, help me get away with it... but you won't help with
          the actual killing.
          (shakes head)
          That's the line? Really?

Adam, defensive.

                    ADAM
          Yes.

Geoff, still amused.

                    GEOFF
          Interesting. You're an interesting person, homeless chemistry man.
          (sudden shift to business)
          Fine. Disposal only. I'm very good at the killing part anyway.

Geoff pulls out his phone. Holds it up.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Smile for the camera!

                    ADAM
          What-

FLASH.

Geoff takes a photo of Adam standing over the body. The composition is damning - Adam clearly visible, body at his feet,
blood on the ground.

Geoff, cheerful.

                    GEOFF
          Insurance! In case you get any ideas about going to the authorities.
          (shows phone)
          See? Crystal clear. You, with a dead body, in an alley. Very incriminating.

Adam stares at the photo. His face goes pale.

Geoff (cont'd), pocketing phone.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Now! Dispose of my little problem. If you do a good job-
          (pulls out a business card)
          -come see me here. I'll pay you. We'll see about future opportunities.

He holds out the card. Adam doesn't move.

Moosk's grip tightens. A warning.

Adam slowly takes the card. Looks at it. An address in the wealthy district.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Don't disappoint me! I hate being disappointed.
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.

Geoff turns and walks toward the alley exit. Moosk releases Adam and follows, moving with mechanical precision.

Geoff (cont'd), calling back.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Oh, and do it tonight! Fresh is easier than ripe, trust me!
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh!

Their footsteps fade. Auto-car doors open and close. The sound of a vehicle gliding away.

Silence.

Adam stands alone in the alley. The body lies at his feet.

He looks at the business card in his hand. Then at the body. Then at the card again.

His hands are shaking.

Adam turns to himself, hollow.

                    ADAM
          What did I just do?

He looks around the alley. No one. The city continues beyond the alley entrance - auto-cars passing, distant sounds of
normal life.

But Adam's life is no longer normal.

He crouches beside the body. Stares at the dead person's face.

Adam (cont'd), whisper.

                    ADAM (CONT'D)
          I'm sorry.

Adam grabs the body under the arms. Starts dragging it deeper into the alley, toward a maintenance access door
half-hidden behind dumpsters.

His face shows horror, disgust, desperation.

But he doesn't stop.

He can't stop now.

FADE TO BLACK.


----------------------------------------
# SCENE 3 - THE POLICE STOP
----------------------------------------

INT. AMAZING COMPANY AUTO-CAR - NIGHT

The interior is pristine. Sleek leather seats, touchscreen controls glowing soft blue, ambient lighting. Everything
expensive and new.

Adam sits rigid in the passenger seat, hands gripping his knees. His worn clothes look jarring against the luxury.

The car drives itself - no steering wheel, no pedals. Just smooth autonomous movement through city streets.

Behind the seats: the cargo area. A large bundle wrapped in dark plastic sheeting lies there. The shape is unmistakable
if you know what you're looking at.

Adam doesn't turn around. Stares straight ahead. His breathing is shallow.

EXT. TRACKTOWN PERIMETER - NIGHT (THROUGH WINDSHIELD)

The auto-car approaches a checkpoint. Simple setup: barriers across the road, portable lights, a couple of folding
chairs. Nothing official.

Two GUARDS stand watch - wearing black vests over regular clothes. No badges, no uniforms. Just the vests marking them as
Tracktown night patrol.

GUARD KANE (40s, tired) holds up a hand. The auto-car slows automatically.

GUARD REED (30s, newer to patrol) steps forward with a flashlight.

The auto-car's display shows: "CHECKPOINT STOP."

Adam, whisper.

                    ADAM
          No no no...

The car stops at the barrier.

Adam's hands shake. He forces them still.

The guards approach the auto-car, one on each side. Kane taps the window. It slides down automatically.

                    GUARD KANE
          Evening. Checkpoint security. Where you coming from?

Adam, carefully.

                    ADAM
          Tracktown. I live in Tracktown.

Guard Reed shines a flashlight through the passenger side window, scanning the interior. The beam passes over Adam's
face, making him squint.

                    GUARD KANE
          Step out of the vehicle, please.

Adam's heart hammers. He opens the door, steps out onto the pavement.

Guard Kane examines him - the worn clothes, the nervous energy, the fact he doesn't belong in a luxury vehicle.

                    GUARD KANE (CONT'D)
          What are you doing in an Amazing Company auto-car?

Adam, forcing calm.

                    ADAM
          I was offered a job. With the company. This is - employee transport. A perk.

Silence.

Guard Reed circles the car, still shining his flashlight through windows. Checking the cargo area.

Adam's blood turns to ice. The flashlight beam passes over the wrapped bundle.

Guard reed, calling to Kane.

                    GUARD REED
          There's something in the cargo area.

Guard kane turns to Adam.

                    GUARD KANE
          What's in the back?

Adam, too fast.

                    ADAM
          Equipment. For the job. Chemistry equipment.

                    GUARD KANE
          Chemistry.
          (pause)
          What kind of job involves chemistry equipment at-
          (checks watch)
          -two in the morning?

Adam, desperate.

                    ADAM
          Research position. They need materials moved. I'm starting tomorrow - today - and they wanted me to get
          familiar with the transport system first.

It sounds thin even to him.

Guard Kane studies Adam's face. Looking for tells, for lies, for anything suspicious.

                    GUARD KANE
          Show me your phone. The company app.

Adam pulls out his phone with trembling hands. Opens it.

The screen shows the Amazing Company employee transport app. It's real - Geoff actually set this up. The destination is
logged: Research Facility, Industrial District.

Guard Kane takes the phone. Examines it. Checks the credentials.

Long, terrible silence.

Guard kane (cont'd), thoughtful.

                    GUARD KANE (CONT'D)
          Huh.

He hands the phone back.

Guard reed, joining them.

                    GUARD REED
          Kane, you see this?

                    GUARD KANE
          Yeah. Checks out.

Guard reed turns to Adam, skeptical.

                    GUARD REED
          You really work for Amazing Company? You?

The question stings. The implication clear: someone from Tracktown doesn't get jobs like that.

Adam, quiet.

                    ADAM
          I start today.

Guard kane, softer now.

                    GUARD KANE
          What are you wearing under that jacket?

Adam, confused.

                    ADAM
          What?

                    GUARD KANE
          Your clothes. What else you got?

Adam, understanding.

                    ADAM
          This is it. Everything I own.

He's not lying. Guard Kane sees the truth in it.

Guard kane turns to Reed.

                    GUARD KANE
          Let him through.

Guard reed, protesting.

                    GUARD REED
          But-

Guard kane, firm.

                    GUARD KANE
          Let him through.

Guard Reed steps back, frustrated but obedient.

Guard Kane looks at Adam with something like sympathy now.

Guard kane (cont'd), genuine.

                    GUARD KANE (CONT'D)
          Good for you. Getting out of Tracktown. Amazing Company - that's a real opportunity.

Adam, hollow.

                    ADAM
          Yeah. Real opportunity.

                    GUARD KANE
          We've been checking all vehicles passing through. People have been going missing. Just looking out for the
          community.

Adam nods. Can't speak. The irony is suffocating.

                    GUARD KANE (CONT'D)
          You're free to go. Good luck with the new job.

Adam nods again. Gets back in the auto-car.

The door closes. The window slides up.

Through the glass, Guard Kane gives a small wave - encouragement.

The auto-car's system reactivates: "RESUMING ROUTE."

The barrier lifts. The vehicle pulls smoothly through the checkpoint.

INT. AUTO-CAR - CONTINUOUS

Adam sits frozen for a moment.

Then he exhales. A shuddering, terrified breath he's been holding.

His hands are shaking violently now. He grips his knees to make them stop.

Adam, whisper, to himself.

                    ADAM
          Oh god. Oh god.

He looks at the phone in his lap. The Amazing Company credentials that saved him.

Behind him, in the cargo area, the wrapped body lies undisturbed.

The auto-car drives on through the night, carrying its terrible cargo toward the disposal site.

Adam stares out the window, seeing nothing.

The reality of what he's doing crashes down. Not just disposing of a body. Transporting it through checkpoints. Nearly
getting caught. Being congratulated by Tracktown guards while a corpse sits three feet behind him.

He's in too deep now.

No way out but through.

FADE TO BLACK.


----------------------------------------
# SCENE 4 - THE DISPOSAL
----------------------------------------

EXT. RAILYARD - EDGE OF SETTLEMENT - NIGHT

The auto-car stops at the periphery of the railyard settlement. Silent. Clean. Completely out of place.

The door opens. Adam emerges, looking around nervously. He pops the cargo area, pulls out the wrapped bundle.

The weight is awkward. He struggles briefly, then gets a better grip. Drags it into the shadows between makeshift
structures.

The auto-car's door closes. The vehicle glides away silently, disappearing into the night.

Adam watches it go, then turns toward the settlement with his terrible burden.

INT. RAILYARD TUNNELS - NIGHT

Adam descends through the maintenance hatch we saw in Scene 0. The wrapped body drags behind him, bumping down metal
rungs.

The tunnels are dark except for flickering emergency lights. He navigates by memory, moving with certainty despite the
poor visibility.

Takes a left turn. Then a right. Another left. The path is complex, deliberately so. Nobody would stumble on this route
by accident.

He passes his sleeping chamber. Doesn't stop. Goes deeper.

Another hatch - this one lower, more concealed. Almost invisible if you don't know it's there.

Adam opens it. A ladder descends into complete darkness.

He pushes the body through first. It tumbles down with a heavy THUD.

Then he climbs down after it.

INT. SECRET CHAMBER - NIGHT

Adam's feet hit solid ground. He reaches up, pulls the hatch closed above him. Complete darkness.

A click. A switch.

LIGHT.

Chemical glow - green and eerie. The chamber is larger than expected. Ten by fifteen feet. Low concrete ceiling.

Against the far wall: a large industrial vat, maybe four feet deep. The liquid inside glows sickly green, viscous and
bubbling slightly. Acid.

Around the chamber: chemistry equipment. Beakers, burners, tubes coiled like intestines. Bottles of chemicals on shelves.
A proper lab, hidden underground.

This is no improvised solution. This is planned. Prepared. Professional.

Adam looks at the body on the floor. Takes a breath.

Adam, quiet, to the body.

                    ADAM
          I'm sorry. I don't know who you were.

He crouches, begins unwrapping the plastic. The victim's face is visible now - peaceful in death, but still a person who
had a life.

Adam closes his eyes for a moment. Then opens them.

Adam (cont'd) turns to himself.

                    ADAM (CONT'D)
          Do the work. Just do the work.

He grabs a pair of heavy rubber gloves from a shelf. Puts them on. The gloves go up to his elbows.

Then a face shield. Safety equipment. He's done this before - or at least planned for it.

Adam drags the body to the edge of the vat. The acid bubbles, hungry.

He lifts the body - struggling with the weight - and rolls it into the vat.

SPLASH.

The liquid surges over the edges, splashing onto the concrete floor. Adam steps back, avoiding it.

The body begins to dissolve immediately. Skin bubbling, tissue breaking down. The chemical reaction is violent,
efficient.

Adam watches for a moment, then turns away. Walks to a small desk in the corner - more makeshift workspace.

A notebook sits there. He opens it. Pages of notes, calculations, chemical formulas. This is serious work.

He adds a new entry:

"Subject disposed successfully. Time to full dissolution: estimated 6-8 hours. Acid pH levels stable. No complications."

He pauses, pen hovering over paper. Then adds:

"First job complete. God help me."

Adam sets the pen down. Looks at the vat. The body is barely visible now, already broken down to organic sludge.

His expression is hollow. This is what he is now. This is what he does.

He moves to another shelf, begins checking chemical levels in various containers. Routine maintenance. Making sure the
next disposal will go just as smoothly.

Because there will be a next disposal. He knows it.

TIME CUT:

Hours pass. Adam works methodically. Testing pH levels. Adjusting the mixture. Disposing of the plastic wrapping in a
separate chemical bath.

Cleaning every surface.

The body is completely gone now. Nothing but glowing liquid in the vat. No evidence. No trace.

Adam pulls off the gloves. The face shield. Sets them aside for cleaning.

He looks at the chamber - his hidden laboratory, his terrible secret.

Adam, whisper.

                    ADAM
          What am I becoming?

No answer. Just the quiet bubble of acid dissolving reality.

INT. RAILYARD TUNNELS - NIGHT

Adam climbs back through the hatch. Seals it behind him. Retraces his steps through the maze of passages.

Emerges in his sleeping chamber. The contrast is stark - the sophisticated lab below, this bare-bones survival space
above.

He has a sleeping bag. Some clothes. Books. Basic supplies.
