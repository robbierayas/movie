# SCENE 2 - THE OFFER

## LOCATION
EXT. CITY STREET - ALLEY - NIGHT (CONTINUOUS)

## CHARACTERS
- ADAM
- GEOFF
- MOOSK

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Continuation from Scene 1 - Adam has offered to help get rid of the body, Geoff is intrigued. Tense negotiation, desperate survival tone. Moosk has Adam by the arm.

### Key Beats
1. DESPERATE OFFER: Adam offers to dispose of body
   - Blurts it out in panic
   - "I can get rid of it! The body!"
   - Geoff pauses, intrigued
   - "And why would I trust you?"

3. THE PITCH: Adam leverages his knowledge
   - Mentions chemistry background
   - Access to railyard's hidden areas
   - Can dissolve body completely
   - No evidence, no trace

4. THE LINE: Adam refuses to help kill
   - "Only disposal. I won't help you kill."
   - Geoff finds this amusing
   - "So you have morals... interesting"
   - Titter laugh

5. THE DEAL: Geoff accepts
   - Takes picture of Adam with the body
   - "Insurance policy"
   - "If it works out, come get paid later"
   - Gives Adam an address/contact method
   - Geoff and Moosk leave

### Character Moments
- Adam: Panicked offer reveals his chemistry knowledge and resourcefulness under pressure. Draws a moral line - won't help kill, only dispose. Desperate but principled. His face goes pale seeing the incriminating photo.
- Geoff: Amused by Adam's "morals," manipulative. Sudden shifts between cheerful and threatening. The titter laugh punctuates his cruelty. Takes the photo as "insurance" - casual threat. Establishes leverage over Adam.
- Moosk: Minimal speech, robotic efficiency. Threatening presence - cold metal fingers, steel clamp grip. Mechanical warning through tightening grip.
- The photograph of Adam with the body - damning composition
- Dark alley, limited escape routes
- End of setup, beginning of Act 1 - the deal that changes everything. Adam crosses moral line (helping murderer). Point of no return - Adam commits.

### Closing
Adam alone with the body. Realizes what he's agreed to. Looks at the body. No choice now - has to follow through. Begins dragging it away. Adam alone with body leads to disposal (Scene 3).

## FULL SCRIPT

EXT. CITY STREET - ALLEY - NIGHT (CONTINUOUS)

Geoff laughs.

                    GEOFF
          Fair point!
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.
          (serious again)
          But why should I trust you? What's stopping you from running straight to the police the moment Moosk lets go?

Adam's eyes dart between Geoff, Moosk, and the alley exit. His mind races.

                    ADAM
          I - I have chemistry training. Equipment. I can dissolve it. Completely. No trace.

That gets Geoff's attention. He stops pacing.

                    GEOFF
          Chemistry training. How... generic.
          (leaning closer)
          What kind of training?

Adam, stammering.

                    ADAM
          University. Research. I used to - it doesn't matter. I know how to break down organic material. Make it disappear.

                    GEOFF
          And where would you do this miraculous disappearing act?

                    ADAM
          The railyard. Tracktown. Underground. I have access to spaces nobody else knows about.

Geoff considers this. His expression shifts from amused to calculating. Geoff, thoughtful. Then back to amused.

                    GEOFF
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.
          (to Adam)
          You might actually be useful.

He nods at Moosk. The robot loosens its grip on Adam's arm but doesn't let go.

Adam gasps for air, rubbing his neck.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Alright. I'll take you up on your generous offer. You dispose of this-
          (gestures to body)
          -and if you do a good job, maybe we work together again.

Adam, quickly.

                    ADAM
          Just disposal. I won't help you kill anyone.

Silence.

Then Geoff bursts out laughing. Genuine amusement this time.

                    GEOFF
          Oh that's RICH! You have MORALS about this!
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh!
          (wiping eyes)
          You'll help me hide a murder, help me avoid consequences, help me get away with it... but you won't help with
          the actual killing.
          (shakes head)
          That's the line? Really?

Adam, defensive.

                    ADAM
          Yes.

Geoff, without emotion.

                    GEOFF
          Well, everyone has a price.

Geoff, back to amused.

                    GEOFF
          Interesting. You're an interesting person, Mister..?

                    ADAM
          Adam.

                    GEOFF
          Mr. Adam! Fine. Disposal only. I'm very good at the killing part anyway.

Geoff pulls out his phone. Holds it up.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Smile for the camera!

                    ADAM
          What-

FLASH.

Geoff takes a photo of Adam standing over the body. The composition is damning - Adam clearly visible, body at his feet,
blood on the ground.

Geoff, cheerful.

                    GEOFF
          Insurance! Can never have too much.
          (shows phone)
          See? Crystal clear. Very incriminating.

Adam stares at the photo. His face goes pale.

Geoff (cont'd), pocketing phone.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Now! Dispose of my little problem. If you can actually do it-
          (pulls out a business card)
          -come see me here. I'll pay you. We'll see about future opportunities.

He holds out the card. Adam doesn't move.

Moosk's grip tightens. A warning.

Adam slowly takes the card. Looks at it. An address in the wealthy district. An autocar arrives at the end of the alley.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          This autocar will take you home so you aren't seen. Don't disappoint me! I hate being disappointed.
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh.

Geoff turns and walks toward the alley exit. Moosk releases Adam and follows, moving with mechanical precision.

Geoff (cont'd), calling back.

                    GEOFF (CONT'D)
          Oh, and do it tonight! Fresh is easier than ripe, trust me!
          (titter laugh)
          Heh-heh-heh!

Their footsteps fade. Auto-car doors open and close. The sound of a vehicle gliding away.

Silence.

Adam stands alone in the alley. The body lies at his feet.

He looks at the business card in his hand. Then at the body. Then at the card again.

His hands are shaking.

Adam turns to himself, hollow.

                    ADAM
          What did I just do?

He looks around the alley. No one. The city continues beyond the alley entrance - auto-cars passing, distant sounds of
normal life.

But Adam's life is no longer normal.

He crouches beside the body. Stares at the dead person's face.

Adam (cont'd), whisper.

                    ADAM (CONT'D)
          I'm sorry.

Adam grabs the body under the arms. Starts dragging it deeper into the alley, toward a maintenance access door
half-hidden behind dumpsters.

His face shows horror, disgust, desperation.

But he doesn't stop.

He can't stop now.

FADE TO BLACK.
