# SCENE 11 - MEETING GAL

## LOCATION
EXT. RAILYARD - BODEGA - AFTERNOON

## CHARACTERS
- ADAM
- GAL (woman, principled survivor, Tracktown resident - Tyson's friend)
- TYSON
- JOE
- MARIE

## SCENE LAYOUT

### Opening
Warm, community, belonging, hopeful - end of Act 1 high point. Adam and Tyson arrive at a small bodega near the railyard. Tyson is bringing Adam to meet his friend Gal — he mentioned her in Scene 10. Neighborhood bodega atmosphere.

### Key Beats

1. THE BODEGA: Tyson brings Adam to meet Gal
   - Adam and Tyson arrive at store
   - Tyson knows Gal is here
   - Adam looking for Marie's coffee
   - Tyson spots Gal inside

2. THE SETUP: Coffee scam on Adam
   - Gal has the last coffee Adam needs
   - Tyson plays innocent, acts like he doesn't know her
   - Gal plays the stranger, acts like it's her coffee
   - Adam doesn't realize they know each other

3. THE NEGOTIATION: Adam's earnest offer
   - Adam offers to buy anything else in store for the coffee
   - Gal: "Keeping your word matters, huh?"
   - Adam genuine: "I haven't been good at keeping promises lately"
   - Gal tests him — browses expensive items
   - Adam doesn't flinch: "Whatever you want"

4. THE REVEAL: They got him
   - After Adam pays for everything
   - Tyson and Gal look at each other and crack up
   - They know each other — this was a setup
   - Gal was never going to keep the coffee
   - They hustled Adam to see what kind of person he is

5. ADAM'S REACTION: He got played
   - Realizes the misdirection — same trick he pulled on Tyson
   - Laughs at himself
   - Callback to pickpocket lesson: "Misdirection"
   - Mutual respect established — they're all hustlers

6. THE INVITATION: Barbecue
   - Adam invites Gal to the community barbecue
   - Gal already knows about it through Tyson
   - The three walk back together

7. THE MEAL: Sharing food at the barbecue
   - Return to barbecue from Scene 9
   - The three share the meal together
   - Joe welcomes them back
   - Marie gets her coffee
   - Natural, warm atmosphere

8. THE BELONGING: Adam's realization
   - Sitting with Tyson and Gal
   - For the first time in years: he has friends
   - Joe asks: "Friends?"
   - Toast together
   - This is what he has to lose

### Character Moments
- Introduce Gal through her existing dynamic with Tyson — they're a team
- The coffee scam tests Adam's character and shows Gal/Tyson's bond
- Adam passes the test by being genuine, not by being clever
- Callback to Scene 10's misdirection lesson — now Adam's the mark
- Show Adam has found family — transformation complete: isolated → belonging
- End Act 1 on high note before it falls apart
- Set up Act 2 stakes: he'll lose this

### Closing
End of Act 1 - peak of Adam's happiness. The three friends together, belonging. Final moment of hope before everything falls apart in Act 2.
- IN: From Scene 10 (meeting Tyson, going to store)
- OUT: End of Act 1 → Act 2 begins (darkness returns)

## FULL SCRIPT

EXT. NEIGHBORHOOD BODEGA - AFTERNOON

A small store near the railyard. Worn but functional. Serves the homeless and working poor.

Adam and Tyson approach. Adam checks his phone - the grocery list.

                    ADAM
          Coffee. Marie specifically asked for coffee.

                    TYSON
          Perfect. My friend Gal hangs around here. I told you about her.

                    ADAM
          The one who'd love chemistry magic?

                    TYSON
          That's the one.

They enter.

INT. BODEGA - CONTINUOUS

Cramped aisles. Basic supplies. A SHOPKEEPER (60s) behind bulletproof glass at the counter.

Adam walks to the coffee section. Scans the shelf. Empty. The specific brand Marie wanted - gone.

Adam, muttering.

                    ADAM
          Come on...

At the counter, a WOMAN (GAL, early-mid 20s, worn clothes but carrying herself with quiet confidence) completes a purchase.

She holds a bag. Inside: the exact coffee Adam is looking for.

Tyson sees Gal. His face doesn't change. Doesn't acknowledge her.

Adam walks to the counter. Sees the coffee in her bag.

                    ADAM (CONT'D)
          Excuse me. That coffee - did you just buy the last one?

Gal, glancing at bag.

                    GAL
          Yeah. Why?

                    ADAM
          I promised someone, Marie, I'd get it. That specific brand.

Gal smiles.

                    GAL
          So did I.

They look at each other. Impasse.

Adam, shrugging at Tyson.

                    ADAM
          I'll just get a different kind.

Adam shakes his head.

                    TYSON
          Well, Marie is super picky about her coffee. I'd get this one. She asked for it specifically.

Gal, studying him, not sure but going along with Tyson.

                    GAL
          Oh Marie, looks like she's gonna be mad about not getting the coffee she wanted, huh?

                    ADAM
          Yeah. I guess she's gonna be mad.

Gal considers this. Looks at Adam more carefully.

                    GAL
          I'll make you a deal. You buy me anything else in this store - anything I want - and the coffee's yours.

Adam, immediately.

                    ADAM
          Done.

Gal, surprised, smiles at Tyson before looking back to Adam.

                    GAL
          You didn't even ask what I'd pick.

                    ADAM
          Doesn't matter. I said I'd get the coffee. So whatever you need, it's yours.

Gal turns back into the store. Adam and Tyson follow. She browses, testing him. Picks up expensive items, watches his reaction.

Adam, patient.

                    ADAM (CONT'D)
          Whatever you want.

Gal, picking up toiletries.

                    GAL
          These. And bread. And soup.

Gal notices Tyson out of Adam's sight gesture at the candy.

                    GAL
          And a chocolate... from behind the counter.

Adam nods.

                    ADAM
          Done.

Gal, quieter.

                    GAL
          Why's it matter so much? The coffee?

                    ADAM
          Because someone asked me to get it. And I haven't been good at keeping promises lately. So when I can... I do.

They go to the counter. Adam pays for everything - Gal's items and the rest of his list.

The shopkeeper bags it all.

Outside, Adam holds up the coffee.

                    ADAM
          Thank you. Really.

Gal looks at Tyson. Tyson looks at Gal.

They both crack up laughing. Gal hands Tyson the chocolate.

Adam stares at them. Confused.

                    ADAM (CONT'D)
          What?

Tyson, barely containing himself.

                    TYSON
          Adam... this is Gal.

                    ADAM
          I know, she just told me her -

                    TYSON
          No. This is GAL. My friend Gal. The one I told you about.

Adam looks at Gal. Gal grins at him.

                    GAL
          Hi.

It hits him. He looks between them.

                    ADAM
          You... you two set this up?

                    TYSON
          You're not the only quick one.

Adam stares.

                    ADAM
          You hustled me.

                    GAL
          Wanted to see what kind of person you were.

                    TYSON
          And you passed! You bought her bread AND soup!

Gal hands him back the toiletries, the bread, the soup.

                    GAL
          I don't need these. I just wanted to see if you'd actually do it.

Adam holds the bag. Looks at the items she's returning. Then at Tyson's grinning face. Then at Gal's.

He starts laughing.

                    ADAM
          Misdirection.

                    TYSON
          Can't spell it without T!

He laughs harder. Shakes his head.

                    ADAM
          I can't believe I fell for it.

Tyson punches Adam's shoulder lightly.

                    TYSON
          Don't let it go to your head.

The three of them standing outside the bodega. Something settling between them. Easy. Natural.

                    ADAM
          We're having a barbecue. Back at Tracktown. You should come.

Gal, amused.

                    GAL
          T already invited me.

                    TYSON
          Before we even came in here.

Adam laughs again.

                    ADAM
          Is anything about today not a setup?

EXT. RAILYARD - ENCAMPMENT - LATE AFTERNOON

Adam, Tyson, and Gal walk back together. Easy conversation. Tyson bounces between them, telling stories. Gal and Adam talk around him.

They arrive at the barbecue. The community from Scene 9, still gathered.

Joe spots them.

                    JOE
          Adam! You made it back! And brought friends!

Marie, seeing the coffee.

                    MARIE
          You got it! Thank you!

Adam, handing it over.

                    ADAM
          Told you I would.

He glances at Gal. She smiles.

Plates of food passed around. The three sit together on a bench. Eating, talking, laughing.

AT THE BARBECUE:

Gal watches the community. Sees the warmth, the support system.

                    GAL
          This is nice. You've got a good thing here.

Adam, looking around.

                    ADAM
          Yeah. I do.

Tyson tells another story. Both Adam and Gal laugh.

Adam looks at them - Tyson's energy, Gal's quiet confidence. Feels something he hasn't felt in years.

Belonging.

Joe, approaching with more food.

                    JOE
          You three look good together. Friends?

Adam smiles.

                    ADAM
          Yeah. Friends.

Gal and Tyson exchange a look. Smile.

Tyson, grinning.

                    TYSON
          Damn right.

Gal, raising cup.

                    GAL
          To friends.

They toast - makeshift cups of water.

The sun sets behind them. The railyard community continues around them.

For the first time in years, Adam is not alone.

He has people. He has a place. He has friends.

This is what he's been building.

This is what he has to lose.

The three of them together, smiling, belonging.

FADE TO BLACK.

END ACT 1
