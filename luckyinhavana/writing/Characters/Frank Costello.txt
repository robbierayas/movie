## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Costello represents the political, elegant side of organized crime -- a boss with connections rather than just muscle. As
the acting head of Lucky's family in New York, he is Lucky's proxy in America and an important ally at the Havana
Conference. His presence reinforces Lucky's continued authority and the stakes of the power struggle with Genovese. His
eventual fate (forced out by Genovese in 1957) adds to the epilogue's catalogue of Genovese's ruthless consolidation.
