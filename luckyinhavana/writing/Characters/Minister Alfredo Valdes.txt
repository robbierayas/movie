## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Valdes represents Cuba itself -- the welcoming paradise that profits from mob money until American leverage crushes it.
He is the face of systemic corruption: smooth, efficient, and genuinely likable even as he greases every permit and takes
his cut. His arc from opening every door for Lucky to being forced to close them all mirrors the film's larger theme
about small nations caught between superpowers and organized crime. He is not a villain but a pragmatist operating within
an inherently corrupt system.

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
- Smooth, charming, and efficient -- the consummate fixer who makes problems disappear
- Corrupt but not malicious -- takes his cut of everything, but operates with genuine warmth and hospitality
- Pragmatic above all else -- understands how Cuba works and works the system expertly
- Genuinely likes Lucky -- they understand each other as men who navigate power structures
- Capable of genuine pain -- his delivery of the deportation news is emotionally difficult, not performative
- Powerless against larger forces -- despite his influence within Cuba, he cannot resist American economic leverage
- Professional and competent -- arranges residency papers, mansions, permits, hotel bookings, and Batista's cooperation
with apparent ease

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Lucky Luciano**: A relationship of mutual understanding and genuine warmth. Valdes welcomes Lucky to Cuba with the
red carpet treatment: "Senor Luciano, whatever you need." They understand each other as men who work the system. When
Valdes must deliver the deportation news, the scene mirrors their first meeting -- same warmth gone cold. Valdes is
genuinely pained but powerless.
- **Carlos Delgado**: Assigns Delgado as Lucky's personal security detail. Represents the chain of command that puts
Delgado in his impossible position.
- **President Batista (off-screen)**: Valdes operates as Batista's fixer, clearing everything through Batista's office.
When the U.S. ultimatum comes, Batista is the one who decides Lucky must go, and Valdes must carry out the order.
- **The Cuban System**: Valdes is the face of Cuba's open-door policy toward mob money. He smooths every permit,
introduction, and police lookaway. He thrives as the money flows.

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Beginning State (Act 1)**: Welcomes Lucky to Cuba with open arms. Arranges everything: residency papers, the Miramar
mansion, security. Takes his envelope. This is how Cuba works, and Valdes is the man who makes it work.
- **Escalation (Act 1-2)**: Smooths Lucky's entire operation -- permits for casinos, introductions at racetracks, police
lookaway. Arranges the Hotel Nacional booking for the Havana Conference, clears it with Batista's office under the
Sinatra cover story. Valdes is thriving; the money has never been better.
- **Crisis Point (Act 3)**: Must deliver the news that Cuba is deporting Lucky. The U.S. medical supply ultimatum leaves
no choice. The man who opened every door now closes them. "The Americans... they've left us no choice."
- **End State**: Implied in epilogue -- Castro's revolution in 1959 expels the mob from Cuba. Everything Valdes built is
gone.
- **Arc Type**: Disillusionment -- Valdes builds a profitable relationship with the mob only to watch it crushed by
forces beyond his control (American leverage, then revolution).
