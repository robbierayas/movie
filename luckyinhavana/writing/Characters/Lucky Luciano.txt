# LUCKY LUCIANO

## BASIC INFORMATION
- **Full Name**: Salvatore Lucania (known as Lucky Luciano)
- **Age**: 49 (born 1897)
- **Occupation**: Exiled mob boss; formerly America's most powerful organized crime figure
- **Family**: Italian family in Sicily (barely knows them after decades in America); girlfriend Igea Lissoni
- **Historical/Composite**: Historical figure. Born 1897 in Lercara Friddi, Sicily. Died January 26, 1962 in Naples,
Italy.

## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Lucky is the tragic center of the film -- the exiled king whose hubris leads to his downfall, but whose removal creates
something far worse. He represents "the devil you know": a criminal with restraint and a code, as opposed to Genovese's
unchecked ruthlessness. The irony of the film is that Lucky, while unquestionably a criminal who has been trafficking
heroin since 1916, is paradoxically the better alternative. His deportation creates the power vacuum that enables the
heroin epidemic of the 1950s-60s.

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
- Professional and charismatic -- commands respect through presence and competence, not just fear
- Believes he is untouchable -- hubris is his defining flaw; builds an empire 90 miles from Florida without imagining the
consequences
- Personable and genuinely curious -- asks Delgado about his family, treats people well, can be warm
- Strategic and visionary -- sees Cuba as the perfect hub for his operations, plans the French Connection pipeline,
invests in casinos and airlines
- Restless and driven -- cannot accept exile in Italy as a dead end; needs power and action
- Possesses restraint that Genovese lacks -- follows a code, has limits, which makes him the lesser evil
- Calm under pressure -- reads rooms well, stays composed when Genovese challenges him at the conference
- Resigned dignity in defeat -- doesn't hold Delgado's betrayal against him, recognizes when he's been outmaneuvered

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Meyer Lansky**: Lifelong friend and partner. Their relationship is the emotional backbone of Lucky's story. Lansky
manages finances, greets Lucky at the airport, organizes the Conference, and visits him at Tiscornia. Two old partners
excited again when planning Cuba; two old friends saying goodbye through a fence.
- **Vito Genovese**: Ambitious underboss who wants Lucky's throne. Genovese challenges Lucky at the Conference and loses,
but secretly feeds the FBN intelligence to engineer Lucky's deportation. Their rivalry is the engine of the plot.
Genovese visits Tiscornia to gloat and demand the heroin network.
- **Albert Anastasia**: Fierce loyalist. Anastasia's famous defense of Lucky at the Conference is a key dramatic moment.
Lucky knows Anastasia's loyalty will cost him his life (assassinated 1957 on Genovese's orders).
- **Carlos Delgado**: Professional relationship that evolves into mutual respect. Lucky is charming with Delgado, asks
about his family. Delgado ultimately helps arrest him, but Lucky doesn't hold it against him -- Delgado was the only
honest man in the arrangement.
- **Igea Lissoni**: Lucky's girlfriend. Present in Cuba, at the arrest, and at the deportation. Their relationship
provides personal tenderness amid the crime world.
- **Minister Valdes**: Mutual understanding between two men who work the system. Valdes opens every door; when he must
close them, Lucky understands immediately that Cuba is being squeezed.
- **Frank Costello**: Acting boss of the Luciano family in New York. Ally at the Conference. Will be forced out by
Genovese in 1957.

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Beginning State**: Deported from America, bitter homecoming to Italy where he barely knows his family. Powerless,
restless, at a dead end.
- **Rise (Act 1)**: Arrives in Cuba and builds a new empire: casinos, racetracks, airline, the French Connection
pipeline. A king rebuilding his court in exile. Purpose restored.
- **Peak**: Hosts the Havana Conference. Defeats Genovese's challenge. Wins the vote. Forces the humiliating handshake.
Presents the $50 million heroin network. At the height of his power.
- **Fall (Act 2-3)**: Assassination attempt. Sinatra photos create public outrage. U.S. ultimatum forces Cuba's hand.
Arrested at El Jardin restaurant without resistance.
- **End State**: Detained at Tiscornia. Visited by Lansky (friendship), Genovese (gloating), and Delgado (respect).
Boards the S.S. Bakir for exile in Italy. "I'll be back when the current government is gone." He never returns. Dies in
Naples, 1962, age 64.
- **Arc Type**: Tragic fall -- a powerful man brought down by hubris, external betrayal, and forces beyond his control.
The added tragedy: his removal makes things worse for everyone.
