## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Delgado is the moral conscience of the film and the character who sees the truth most clearly. As Lucky's assigned
protector who ultimately helps bring him down, Delgado embodies the impossible position of a good man working within a
corrupt system. He is the one who warns Brennan that removing Lucky will create a bigger monster (Genovese), a warning
that goes unheeded. His arc mirrors Cuba itself -- caught between American power and mob money, trying to do right while
the system makes that nearly impossible.

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
- Family man first -- his wife and daughters anchor his moral compass and give him something worth protecting
- Dutiful and professional -- carries out his assignment to protect Lucky even as he grows uncomfortable with what he
witnesses
- Morally conflicted -- believes in serving Cuba but works for a corrupt government; takes better pay from the Lucky
assignment while insisting he's not "in" with the mob
- Perceptive and observant -- watches Lucky's dock meetings, senses the heroin network being built even without
understanding the specifics
- Quietly courageous -- makes the pivotal choice to share information with Brennan, risking his career and safety
- Capable of respect across moral lines -- develops genuine understanding of Lucky as a person, even while working to
bring him down
- Pragmatic wisdom -- sees the bigger picture that Brennan misses: "You're creating a bigger monster"

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Lucky Luciano**: Assigned as Lucky's personal security detail, then promoted to personal bodyguard after the
assassination attempt. Their relationship evolves from professional distance to something approaching mutual respect.
Lucky is charming and personable with Delgado, asks about his family. Delgado is guarded but not immune. He ultimately
helps arrest Lucky but visits him at Tiscornia to say goodbye -- the only honest man in the whole arrangement.
- **Jack Brennan**: Forms an unlikely alliance with the American FBN agent. They meet at the Hotel Nacional bar. Delgado
agrees to share information on Christmas Eve 1946, becoming Brennan's key source. Warns Brennan repeatedly that targeting
Lucky instead of Genovese will make things worse. Their partnership is built on shared purpose despite different
nationalities and positions.
- **Maria Delgado (wife)**: Warm family relationship. Maria and the daughters represent what Delgado is trying to protect
and the life he's trying to maintain as "normal" despite his dangerous assignment.
- **Minister Alfredo Valdes**: Valdes assigns Delgado to Lucky's detail. Delgado works within the system Valdes
represents -- the corrupt Cuban government that profits from mob money.
- **Extended Family**: The cousin's firing from Oriental Park creates tension -- family sees Delgado as "connected" to
the mob, which he angrily denies, but can't fully refute.

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Beginning State (Act 1)**: A professional police officer assigned to protect Lucky without fully understanding who
his charge is. Proud of the better pay, providing for his family.
- **Inciting Incident**: Witnesses the dock meeting with Corsican and Sicilian contacts (heroin network), his cousin is
fired by Lucky's associates, and his family confronts him about being "in" with the mob. The personal cost of Lucky's
empire hits home.
- **Escalation (Act 2)**: Assigned conference security, positioned to hear everything at the Havana Conference. Makes the
pivotal choice to share information with Brennan on Christmas Eve. Becomes Lucky's personal bodyguard after the
assassination attempt. Deepens alliance with Brennan while debating whether they're making things worse.
- **Crisis Point**: Must deliver Lucky's schedule to Brennan for the arrest -- betraying a man he has come to understand
and respect.
- **End State (Act 3)**: Visits Lucky one last time at Tiscornia. Mutual respect between two men on opposite sides.
Returns home to hold his daughters, troubled by what he helped set in motion. Final image: watching Castro enter Havana
in 1959 -- the Cuba he tried to serve has changed completely.
