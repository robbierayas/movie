## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Anastasia embodies the theme of "loyalty's price." His fierce, unwavering defense of Lucky at the Havana Conference is
one of the film's most dramatic moments -- the famous quote that silences the room and checkmates Genovese. But this very
loyalty marks him for death. Anastasia represents the old guard's code of honor: stand by your boss, no matter what. In
the film's world, that code is both noble and fatal. His eventual assassination by Genovese's orders is the ultimate
consequence of standing up for the right man in the wrong room.

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
- Fiercely loyal -- his devotion to Lucky is absolute and public, the defining trait of his character
- Intimidating physical presence -- when he stands to speak at the Conference, the room listens; his hand resting near
his waist carries an implied threat
- Volatile and dangerous -- known as "The Mad Hatter" and boss of Murder Inc., a man whose anger is feared
- Bristles at disrespect -- reacts immediately to Genovese's challenges, unable to tolerate disloyalty to Lucky
- Direct and confrontational -- does not deal in subtlety; says exactly what he means, dares anyone to disagree
- Courageous to the point of recklessness -- standing up to Genovese in a room full of mob bosses, knowing the
implications

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Lucky Luciano**: Absolute loyalty. Anastasia is Lucky's enforcer and most vocal defender. His famous quote at the
Conference -- "For me, you are the Big Boss, whether you like it or not" -- is the defining moment of their relationship.
Lucky knows Anastasia's loyalty will ultimately cost him his life.
- **Vito Genovese**: Bitter enemy. Anastasia sees Genovese as a snake and bristles at his challenges to Lucky. The
tension between them at the Conference is palpable. Genovese will order Anastasia's assassination in 1957 as revenge.
- **Meyer Lansky**: Both serve as Lucky's inner circle at the Conference. Aligned against Genovese's ambitions.

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Introduction (Act 2)**: Part of the Inner Circle meeting at the Hotel Nacional. Bristles at Genovese's subtle
challenges to Lucky.
- **Defining Moment**: The full Conference session. When Genovese openly challenges Lucky's authority, Anastasia stands
and delivers the famous quote: "For me, you are the Big Boss, whether you like it or not. That's the way I look at it,
and I would like to hear from anybody who don't feel the same way." Seconds Lucky's motion. Glares around the room. Hand
near waist. Genovese is checkmated.
- **Aftermath**: After Lucky's deportation, Lucky warns Anastasia that he and Lansky are targets. Genovese will move
against all of them.
- **Fate (Epilogue)**: Assassinated October 25, 1957 in a barbershop on Genovese's orders. The man who stood up for
Lucky, killed for it. The ultimate price of loyalty.
- **Arc Type**: Tragic loyalty -- stands for what he believes, and it costs him everything.
