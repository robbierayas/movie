## CHARACTER FUNCTION
Lansky is Lucky's lifelong partner and the emotional anchor of Lucky's story. He represents competence, loyalty, and
strategic thinking -- the financial brain behind the empire. His role in the film is multifaceted: he sets the Cuba plan
in motion by telling Lucky about the opportunity, greets him at the airport, organizes the Havana Conference, manages the
gambling operations, and provides the final emotional beat at Tiscornia -- two old friends, one behind a fence. Lansky
may also serve as the vehicle for the film's twist, revealing to Brennan that Genovese fed the FBN.

## PERSONALITY TRAITS
- Financial genius -- manages the Cuba gambling empire with 30% ownership of the Hotel Nacional, oversees racetrack
operations
- Lucky's lifelong friend -- their partnership is built on decades of trust and shared history
- Strategic thinker -- sees the big picture, understands the logistics of building a criminal empire across international
borders
- Perceptive about threats -- recognizes before anyone else that Genovese will move on everything after Lucky's
deportation
- Pragmatic -- handles the practical side of Lucky's ambitions (investments, bookings, financial arrangements)
- Calm and intelligent -- provides a counterbalance to the more volatile personalities in the mob world
- Loyal but realistic -- sticks by Lucky to the end while clearly seeing the coming disaster

## RELATIONSHIPS
- **Lucky Luciano**: Lifelong friend and partner. Their relationship is the emotional heart of Lucky's story. Lansky
tells Lucky about Cuba, greets him at the airport, lays out the business vision with him, organizes the Conference, and
visits him at Tiscornia to settle finances and warn about Genovese. The scene at Tiscornia -- "two old friends, one
behind a fence" -- is the partnership that built an empire winding down.
- **Vito Genovese**: Sees Genovese clearly as a threat. Backs Lucky against Genovese at the Conference. Warns Lucky about
Genovese during the Tiscornia visit. Knows he himself is a target after Lucky's deportation.
- **Albert Anastasia**: Allied with Anastasia as part of Lucky's inner circle. Both support Lucky against Genovese at the
Conference.
- **Jack Brennan**: Possible source of the twist revelation -- Lansky may be the one who tells Brennan the truth about
Genovese feeding intel to the FBN.
- **Frank Costello**: Fellow member of Lucky's alliance at the Conference.

## CHARACTER ARC
- **Setup (Act 1)**: Tells Lucky that Cuba is wide open during their meeting in Italy. Greets Lucky at Havana airport.
Together they lay out the grand vision: casinos, racetracks, airline, French Connection pipeline. Two old partners
excited again.
- **Conference (Act 2)**: Organizes the Havana Conference. Part of the Inner Circle meeting. Backs Lucky financially and
strategically against Genovese's challenge. Present for the Conference decisions on heroin territories and the Bugsy
Siegel situation. Convinces Commission to give Bugsy until spring.
- **Farewell (Act 3)**: Visits Lucky at Tiscornia. Settles finances. Warns Lucky about Genovese -- he can see what's
coming. Two old friends, one behind a fence, watching the end of an era.
- **Legacy (Epilogue)**: The French Connection remains operational (Lucky and Corsicans still partner from Italy). Lansky
loses everything when Castro takes power in 1959. Dies in Miami, 1983 -- outlived everyone.
- **Arc Type**: Loyal partner arc -- stands by Lucky through rise and fall, sees the end coming clearly, endures the
longest.
