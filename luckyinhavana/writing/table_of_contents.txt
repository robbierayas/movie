# TABLE OF CONTENTS

## Act 1 - The Exile King

### Scene 1 - The Deportation
- Location: EXT./INT. - FEDERAL COURTHOUSE / NEW YORK HARBOR - DAY (February 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Jack Brennan, Director Hartley, Igea Lissoni, Federal Escorts, Press Photographers
- Summary: Newsreel-style voiceover details Lucky Luciano's criminal history while a montage shows him leaving the
courthouse under federal escort. Brennan and Hartley watch from the steps -- Brennan's personal stake is established
through his brother's heroin overdose death. Lucky boards the S.S. Laura Keene as the ship pulls away from New York
Harbor.

### Scene 2 - Italy
- Location: EXT./INT. - SICILY/NAPLES, ITALY - DAY (Mid-1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Meyer Lansky, Lucky's Italian Family
- Summary: Lucky's homecoming to Italy is bittersweet -- he barely knows his family after decades in America. Restless
and powerless, he learns from Lansky that Cuba is wide open with Batista on the payroll. Lucky makes the fateful decision
to go to Havana, ninety miles from Florida.

### Scene 3 - The Arrival
- Location: EXT./INT. - HAVANA AIRPORT / MIRAMAR MANSION - DAY (October 29, 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Meyer Lansky, Minister Alfredo Valdes, Carlos Delgado
- Summary: Lucky arrives at Havana airport and is greeted by Lansky. Minister Valdes gives him the red carpet treatment
-- residency papers, a mansion in Miramar, and a promise that whatever he needs, Cuba will provide. Valdes assigns
Delgado as Lucky's personal security detail; Delgado doesn't yet fully understand who his new charge really is.

### Scene 4 - The Cuba Plan
- Location: INT. - HOTEL NACIONAL / ORIENTAL PARK RACETRACK - DAY (Late October 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Meyer Lansky
- Summary: Lucky and Lansky lay out their grand vision for Cuba: casinos, racetracks, an airline, and a distribution hub
ninety miles from Florida. Lucky invests $150,000 in the Hotel Nacional and leases Jockey Club tables at Oriental Park
for $50K per season. They map out the French Connection pipeline -- Turkey to Sicily to France to Cuba to U.S. ports --
with Cuba as the missing piece.

### Scene 5 - The New Home
- Location: EXT./INT. - MIRAMAR MANSION / HAVANA STREETS - DAY (Early November 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Carlos Delgado
- Summary: Lucky settles into the Miramar mansion as Delgado shows him around Havana. A professional relationship begins
to form -- Lucky is charming and personable, asking Delgado about his family, while Delgado remains guarded but not
immune to the charm. We see Lucky's daily life taking shape: the ocean-view mansion, the car, the clothes, the staff of a
king rebuilding his court in exile.

### Scene 6 - November Montage
- Location: EXT./INT. - ORIENTAL PARK RACETRACK / CASINOS / HAVANA DOCKS / MIRAMAR MANSION - DAY/NIGHT (November 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Carlos Delgado, Minister Alfredo Valdes, Corsican and Sicilian Contacts
- Summary: Montage of Lucky building his operations -- racetracks, casinos, the mansion -- with Valdes smoothing every
permit and introduction. At the docks, Lucky meets with Corsican and Sicilian contacts in a hushed meeting about
"shipments" and "routes" while Delgado watches uneasily, not yet understanding he's witnessing the heroin network being
built. Delgado's cousin is fired from Oriental Park when Lucky's American associates take over, landing the first
personal cost of the empire close to home.

### Scene 7 - Delgado Family Dinner
- Location: INT. - DELGADO HOME - NIGHT (November 1946)
- Characters: Carlos Delgado, Maria Delgado, Delgado's Daughters, Extended Family
- Summary: A warm family dinner turns uncomfortable when Delgado's cousin's firing comes up. The family asks Delgado to
use his connections, assuming he's "in" with the mob. Delgado pushes back -- he's a police officer, not a gangster -- but
the family doesn't see the difference. Left alone after dinner, he stews over an uncomfortable truth: they're not
entirely wrong.

### Scene 8 - Conference Preparations
- Location: INT. - HOTEL NACIONAL / MIRAMAR MANSION - DAY (Late November - Early December 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Meyer Lansky, Minister Alfredo Valdes, Carlos Delgado
- Summary: Lucky and Lansky plan the Havana Conference, sending invitations to every boss in America. Valdes arranges the
Hotel Nacional booking and clears it with Batista's office under the cover story of a celebrity party with Frank Sinatra
performing. Delgado is briefed on increased security for a large private event with VIPs from America, not yet grasping
the scale of what's coming.

### Scene 9 - The Hunt Begins
- Location: INT. - FBN HEADQUARTERS, WASHINGTON D.C. - DAY (December 1946)
- Characters: Jack Brennan, Director Hartley
- Summary: Intelligence reaches the FBN that Lucky Luciano is in Cuba and every major mob boss in America is heading to
Havana. Hartley brings Brennan in for the assignment, knowing it's personal -- Brennan's brother died from heroin, and
Lucky is the man he holds responsible. Brennan is on the next plane to Havana. The hunt begins.

## Act 2 - The Conference

### Scene 10 - Arrival and Opening Night Montage
- Location: INT./EXT. - HOTEL NACIONAL - DAY/NIGHT (December 20-22, 1946)
- Characters: Jack Brennan, Carlos Delgado, Lucky Luciano, Meyer Lansky, Frank Costello, Vito Genovese, Albert Anastasia,
Joe Bonanno, Santo Trafficante, Fischetti Brothers, Joe Adonis, Willie Moretti, Frank Sinatra, Hotel Informants
- Summary: Brennan checks into the Hotel Nacional under cover and rapidly recruits informants -- a telephone operator, an
elevator attendant -- while having his first encounter with Delgado at the hotel bar. His voiceover narrates as 20+ mob
bosses arrive in a lavish montage. The opening night features a dinner at the Hotel Nacional with Frank Sinatra
performing, $200,000 in "Christmas presents," and the Fischetti Brothers delivering a $2 million suitcase.

### Scene 11 - The Inner Circle
- Location: INT. - HOTEL NACIONAL, PRIVATE SUITE - NIGHT (December 22, 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Meyer Lansky, Vito Genovese, Albert Anastasia
- Summary: The four most powerful men at the conference meet privately. Lucky lays out his vision for running operations
from Cuba with Lansky backing him financially. Genovese subtly pushes back, questioning whether bosses in America will
keep taking orders from an exile. Anastasia bristles at Genovese's tone, his fierce loyalty to Lucky on full display. The
dynamics are set: Lucky and Lansky are partners, Anastasia is the enforcer, and Genovese is the ambitious outsider
looking for an opening.

### Scene 12 - The Power Struggle
- Location: INT. - HOTEL NACIONAL, CONFERENCE ROOM - DAY (December 23, 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Vito Genovese, Albert Anastasia, Meyer Lansky, Frank Costello, All Conference Bosses
- Summary: Full conference session with all bosses present. Genovese openly challenges Lucky's authority from exile. In
the dramatic climax, Anastasia stands and delivers his famous defense: "For me, you are the Big Boss, whether you like it
or not." Lucky wins the vote and forces Genovese into a humiliating handshake. The heroin network is formally presented
-- projected at $50 million annually -- and distribution territories are divided among the families.

### Scene 13 - Christmas Eve
- Location: INT. - HOTEL NACIONAL - NIGHT (December 24, 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Meyer Lansky, Conference Bosses, Jack Brennan, Carlos Delgado
- Summary: The conference addresses the Bugsy Siegel problem -- the Flamingo's budget has ballooned from $1 million to $6
million with suspected skimming. The decision is to wait for the Flamingo's opening on December 26th. Meanwhile, Delgado
makes a pivotal choice: he meets Brennan and agrees to share information, forming the alliance that will ultimately bring
Lucky down.

### Scene 14 - Christmas Day / Conference Ends
- Location: INT. - HOTEL NACIONAL - DAY/NIGHT (December 25-26, 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Vito Genovese, Meyer Lansky, Conference Bosses
- Summary: As the conference wraps up, Genovese is seen making secret phone calls -- feeding intelligence to the FBN,
using the U.S. government as his weapon against Lucky. News arrives that the Flamingo opening is a disaster. Lucky uses
his influence to convince the Commission to give Bugsy Siegel until spring to turn things around.

### Scene 15 - The Assassination Attempt
- Location: EXT./INT. - MIRAMAR MANSION - NIGHT (Late December 1946)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Carlos Delgado, Gunmen
- Summary: Gunmen attack Lucky's Miramar mansion in a brazen assassination attempt. Lucky survives, but the perpetrator
remains unknown -- Genovese is the prime suspect. In the aftermath, Delgado is assigned as Lucky's personal bodyguard,
deepening his involvement with the man he will eventually help bring down.

### Scene 16 - The Investigation
- Location: INT. - HOTEL NACIONAL / VARIOUS HAVANA LOCATIONS - DAY (January 1947)
- Characters: Jack Brennan, Carlos Delgado, Director Hartley (via phone)
- Summary: Brennan compiles evidence from informants and Delgado, documenting the complete heroin network discussed at
the conference. He reports to Hartley, who praises him while already possessing much of the same intelligence from
Genovese. Brennan notices that some of his intelligence seems already known in Washington but trusts Hartley. Delgado and
Brennan deepen their alliance, debating whether targeting Lucky instead of Genovese will only create a bigger monster.

### Scene 17 - The Pressure
- Location: EXT. - ORIENTAL PARK RACETRACK / HAVANA STREETS - DAY (February 1947)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Frank Sinatra, Director Hartley, Press Photographers
- Summary: Sinatra returns to Havana for four days and is photographed constantly with Lucky at the racetracks. The
photos are published in the U.S., sparking public outrage. Hartley seizes the political moment, using the scandal to
pressure the State Department into action against Luciano's presence in Cuba.

### Scene 18 - The Ultimatum
- Location: INT. - FBN HEADQUARTERS, WASHINGTON D.C. / STATE DEPARTMENT - DAY (February 10-21, 1947)
- Characters: Director Hartley, Jack Brennan (via phone), U.S. State Department Officials, President Batista
- Summary: Hartley proposes economic blackmail to the State Department, leveraging Brennan's evidence combined with
Genovese's intel. On February 21st, the U.S. delivers its ultimatum to Cuba: deport Luciano or lose all medical supplies.
Hartley tells Brennan his evidence made it possible, and Brennan feels proud -- unaware that Genovese engineered this
entire outcome, using the FBN as his personal hit squad.

## Act 3 - The Fall

### Scene 19 - The News Delivered
- Location: INT. - MIRAMAR MANSION - DAY (February 21, 1947)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Minister Alfredo Valdes
- Summary: The U.S. has threatened to cut all medical drug shipments to Cuba -- hospitals will run out of morphine,
anesthesia, and antibiotics. Batista is forced to choose, and Luciano must go. Valdes must deliver the devastating news
to Lucky, mirroring their first warm meeting now gone cold. Lucky sees immediately that Valdes isn't betraying him --
Cuba is being squeezed by American leverage, and they're both pawns in a bigger game.

### Scene 20 - Final Decisions
- Location: EXT. - HAVANA STREET / SAFE LOCATION - NIGHT (February 22, 1947)
- Characters: Jack Brennan, Carlos Delgado
- Summary: Delgado meets Brennan one last time and agrees to help with Lucky's arrest, providing his schedule. The scene
carries the weight of what they're about to do -- Delgado has come to understand Lucky, perhaps even respect him. But the
assignment is the assignment, and both men know there's no turning back.

### Scene 21 - The Arrest
- Location: EXT./INT. - EL JARDIN RESTAURANT, VEDADO DISTRICT - DAY (February 23, 1947)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Igea Lissoni, Assistant Police Chief Hernandez, Carlos Delgado, Press Photographers
- Summary: On a Saturday afternoon, Lucky is having lunch with Igea at El Jardin restaurant when Assistant Police Chief
Hernandez arrives and arrests him without resistance. Photographers are present for the publicity arrest. Lucky is taken
to the Tiscornia immigration detention camp, ending his 141 days of freedom in Cuba.

### Scene 22 - Lansky's Visit
- Location: EXT. - TISCORNIA IMMIGRATION CAMP - DAY (Late February 1947)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Meyer Lansky
- Summary: Lansky visits Lucky at Tiscornia, Cuba's harsh camp for extraditable foreigners. He settles finances and warns
Lucky about Genovese -- he can see what's coming, that Genovese will move on everything they built. Two old friends, one
behind a fence, watch the partnership that built an empire begin to wind down.

### Scene 23 - Genovese's Visit
- Location: EXT. - TISCORNIA IMMIGRATION CAMP - DAY (Early March 1947)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Vito Genovese
- Summary: Genovese comes to the detention camp for a final confrontation, gloating that he fed the FBN everything they
needed to deport Lucky -- using the United States government as his personal weapon. He demands Lucky turn over the
heroin network. Lucky refuses but is powerless as Genovese reveals his expansion plans: bigger, more aggressive, no
restraint. After Genovese leaves, Lucky warns Lansky and Anastasia that they're all targets now.

### Scene 24 - The Twist
- Location: INT. - HOTEL NACIONAL / HAVANA STREET - DAY (Mid-March 1947)
- Characters: Jack Brennan, Director Hartley (via phone), Meyer Lansky
- Summary: Brennan receives a "mission accomplished" telegram from Hartley but feels no triumph. He discovers the
devastating truth -- Genovese has been feeding intelligence to the FBN for months, and Hartley knew his source was a
rival mobster engineering a coup. Brennan confronts Hartley by phone: "We didn't take down Lucky. Genovese did. We were
his muscle." Hartley is unapologetic. Brennan writes a report warning about Genovese, but it will be buried.

### Scene 25 - Delgado's Goodbye
- Location: EXT. - TISCORNIA IMMIGRATION CAMP - DAY (Mid-March 1947)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Carlos Delgado
- Summary: Delgado visits Lucky one last time before deportation. There is mutual respect between the two men despite
being on opposite sides -- Delgado protected him, watched over him, and ultimately helped bring him down. Lucky doesn't
hold it against him; Delgado was the only honest man in the whole arrangement. A quiet scene of two men who understand
each other saying goodbye.

### Scene 26 - The Deportation
- Location: EXT. - HAVANA HARBOR - NIGHT (March 19, 1947)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Igea Lissoni, Jack Brennan, Carlos Delgado, Maria Delgado, Delgado's Daughters
- Summary: Late evening at the harbor. Lucky boards the S.S. Bakir, a rusty Turkish cargo freighter, for the 23-day
voyage to Italy. Igea is present for a tearful goodbye. Lucky delivers his final line: "I'll be back when the current
government is gone." Brennan watches from the dock with doubt in his eyes, knowing now what he helped set in motion.
Delgado returns home and holds his daughters, troubled by it all.

### Scene 27 - Epilogue Montage
- Location: EXT./INT. - GENOA, ITALY / NAPLES / NEW YORK / VARIOUS U.S. CITIES / HAVANA - DAY/NIGHT (1947-1970s)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Vito Genovese, Albert Anastasia, Frank Costello, Meyer Lansky
- Summary: A montage spanning decades traces the consequences of Lucky's deportation. The S.S. Bakir arrives in Genoa;
Lucky is detained then released, settling in Naples. Genovese takes over: the French Connection becomes fully
operational, heroin floods American cities, Anastasia is assassinated in a barbershop on Genovese's orders in 1957, and
Costello is forced out. Castro's revolution in 1959 expels the mob from Cuba. Lucky dies in Naples in 1962, Genovese dies
in prison in 1969, and the French Connection is finally broken in the early 1970s.

### Scene 28 - Final Image
- Location: EXT. - S.S. BAKIR (1947) / GENOVESE'S HEADQUARTERS (1950s) / HAVANA STREETS (1959)
- Characters: Lucky Luciano, Vito Genovese, Carlos Delgado, Jack Brennan
- Summary: Split screen cross-cutting between three men in three different times: Lucky on the S.S. Bakir sailing into
exile in 1947, Genovese standing victorious at the peak of his heroin empire in the 1950s, and Delgado watching Castro
enter Havana in 1959. Three men, three paths, all shaped by five months in Cuba. Brennan and Delgado tried to do the
right thing -- but sometimes doing the right thing just changes which evil is in power.

## Estimated Runtime
- Act 1 (Scenes 1-9): ~35 minutes - Establishes Lucky's deportation, arrival in Cuba, empire building, and the FBN
discovering his presence. Mix of montage sequences and character-building scenes.
- Act 2 (Scenes 10-18): ~50 minutes - The longest act, covering the Havana Conference, power struggle, assassination
attempt, investigation, and the U.S. ultimatum. Dense with dialogue, confrontation, and political maneuvering.
- Act 3 (Scenes 19-28): ~40 minutes - The arrest, detention visits, the twist reveal, deportation, and epilogue montage.
Builds to emotional climax with the Pyrrhic victory theme.
- **Estimated Total Runtime: ~125 minutes**
