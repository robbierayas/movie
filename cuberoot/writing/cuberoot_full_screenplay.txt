# SCENE 0 - OPENING TRAP
----------------------------------------

FADE IN:

INT. ROOT CUBE - TRAP ROOM - UNKNOWN TIME

Darkness.

White walls fade into view. A CUBE ROOM - sterile, identical surfaces on all sides.

A MAN (30s, business casual, INZO employee badge) crawls through a doorway. Breathing hard. Frightened but controlled.

He stands carefully. Scans the room.

Empty. No visible threats.

He steps forward cautiously. One step. Another.

Reaches the CENTER of the room.

Stops.

Something's wrong. He feels it.

Looks down at his hands.

His FINGERTIPS are DISSOLVING - pixels breaking apart in slow motion.

Simultaneously, they REAPPEAR six feet to his right.

His fingers exist in two places at once - dissolving on his hands, manifesting in empty air.

The man's eyes widen. Fear, but quiet. Too strange to scream.

He tries to move his fingers. Both sets respond. The dissolved ones flicker.

The effect SPREADS.

Both hands now. Toes. Feet. Dissolving from his body, reappearing impossibly far away.

He looks at his RIGHT HAND - half-dissolved, flickering.

Looks to his RIGHT - sees his hand floating in space, ten feet away, fully formed.

His left foot dissolves. Reappears behind him.

He's being TORN APART across dimensional space.

Not violent. Not bloody. Just... wrong.

His expression: horror, confusion, a terrible understanding.

He tries to speak. His jaw dissolves. Reappears above his head.

20 SECONDS since activation.

Pieces of him exist in a dozen impossible locations.

Fingers here. Forearm there. Foot across the room. Torso splitting.

Then -

COMPLETE DISSOLUTION.

He's gone.

The room is EMPTY again.

White walls. Silent. Waiting.

TITLE CARD: CUBE^(0.333)

FADE OUT.

# SCENE 5 - CUBE TRAP DEATH
----------------------------------------

FADE IN:

INT. ROOT CUBE - TRAP ROOM - DAY

BARRY (40s, bearded, exhausted) navigates the white cube room. Wearing business casual clothes with a CARDIGAN SWEATER -
now worn and disheveled from weeks in the cube.

Moves cautiously. Tests each step. Clearly experienced.

His backpack is worn. Face haggard. Been here for weeks.

He reaches the CENTER of the room.

A subtle SHIFT. Temperature change. Air shimmers.

BARRY WINCES.

He knew this would happen.

Not surprised. Just... resigned.

He RUNS toward the exit door.

Reaches it. Claws at the surface.

SEALED.

He pounds on it. Once. Twice.

Then stops.

Slowly turns around.

Looks at the room. The trap that will kill him.

His shoulders slump.

BARRY slides down against the door. Sits.

Quiet acceptance on his face.

The air shimmers more visibly now.

BARRY's skin begins to REDDEN. Microwave radiation at cellular level.

His breath comes in gasps.

Water in his cells boiling from inside out.

He closes his eyes.

20 SECONDS.

No screaming. Too exhausted. Too resigned.

He slumps to the side.

Gone.

The room is QUIET.

Barry's NOTEBOOK lies on the floor nearby. Pages scattered.

A destroyed DEVICE - the quantameter - smoking, melted.

The trap fades.

Room returns to normal.

Empty. Waiting for the next victim.

CAMERA PUSHES IN on Barry's body.

His INZO EMPLOYEE BADGE visible on his chest. The photo shows him younger, smiling, full of life.

CLOSE-UP: Barry's badge photo.

The image frozen in time. A reminder of who he was before the cube.

                    MARCUS
